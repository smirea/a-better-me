## Setting up
1. Select your desired weight with the loading pin and adjust the machine’s seat to a position that allows your knees to be mildly bent when your feet are on the foot platform.
    1. If the seat is positioned too far back, the machine may not be able to provide tension throughout your full ankle range of motion.
2. Sit down with your back in full contact with the backrest.
    1. Rather than being rounded in a slouched posture from sitting your hips excessively forward, your back should be near a neutral position, like when you are normally standing upright.
3. With a hip-width to shoulder-width stance, place the balls of your feet onto the foot platform with your heels hanging off the platform.
4. Grasp the handles for additional stability and continue holding them throughout your set.
5. While letting your heels move slightly forward to stretch your calves, straighten your knees to press your feet into the platform.
    1. After doing so, your knees should either be straight or very slightly bent. If you have the mobility to hyperextend your knees (which is when the knees bend backward beyond a straight position), you can avoid hyperextending them.
    2. The purpose of this step is to place your calves into a stretched starting position to help you train through a longer range of ankle motion compared to if you started with your ankles in a neutral position (like when you are normally standing upright and your foot and shin form a 90-degree angle). Ideally, you should now feel a stretching sensation in your calves.
    3. While this type of machine often has a foot platform that can pivot and a seat that is stationary after adjusting it, some machine models have a stationary foot platform and a mobile seat. If your machine has a mobile seat, you will press yourself away from the platform during this step.
## Exercise technique
1. Push through the balls of your feet to press the platform fully away from you and point your feet forward, in a manner similar to stepping on a car’s gas pedal.
    1. If your machine has a mobile seat and a fixed platform, you can have the same intent, but you will press yourself away from the platform instead of pressing the platform away from you. With this machine style, you will similarly perform the reverse of that motion during the next step.
    2. Throughout the exercise, keep the balls of your feet firmly planted on the platform as movement occurs exclusively at your ankles without any knee or hip motion.
2. Reverse the movement by letting the balls of your feet arc back toward your shins in a controlled manner through the full range of motion permitted by your calf flexibility and machine.
    1. Ideally, you should feel a deep stretching sensation in your calves at the end of this step.
    2. Avoid letting your knees bend.
    3. While you do not have to perform this step with an extremely slow tempo, avoid rapidly moving and trying to bounce into the next rep.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
    1. Following your last rep, bend your knees to lower the weight you selected onto the rest of the weight stack.
    2. If you feel your feet start sliding down on the platform during your set, make sure to shift them back up to keep the balls of your feet on the platform.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop the gastrocnemius and soleus that constitute your calves.
