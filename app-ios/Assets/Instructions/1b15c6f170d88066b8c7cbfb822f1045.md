## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to its highest level, connect a V-bar attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. While facing the cable station, reach up and grasp the V-bar with your palms facing at an angle downward and inward while keeping your wrists fairly straight.
    1. Keep your wrists in a fairly straight position throughout your set.
3. Take a step backward and slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips.
    1. Maintain this slight torso lean throughout your set.
4. Pull your upper arms down and back until they are at your sides, with your elbows close to fully bent.
    1. Maintain this upper arm position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Push the V-bar downward by extending your elbows until they are straightened.
    1. At the end of this motion, the V-bar should be situated in front of your thighs.
2. Reverse the motion by bending your elbows through your available range of motion to raise the bar in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position.
    1. Strive to have your elbows as close to fully bent as you can by the time you have finished this motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop all three heads of your triceps, which constitute the majority of muscle mass in your upper arms.
