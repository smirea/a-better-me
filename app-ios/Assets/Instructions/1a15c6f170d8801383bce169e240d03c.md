## Setting up
1. Stand upright with your back against a wall.
2. While keeping your buttocks and back against the wall, walk your feet forward before straightening your knees with your soles flat on the ground.
    1. As you walk your feet farther forward, the difficulty of the exercise can increase, so you can select your positioning based on the challenge you wish to have, which may take some trial and error.
    2. At any point during the exercise, you can walk your feet closer toward the wall or farther away from it if you wish to adjust the difficulty.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your heels on the ground, lift up the rest of your feet off the ground through your available ankle range of motion.
    1. As only your ankles move, continue keeping your knees straight while your buttocks and back remain in contact with the wall.
    2. You will likely feel the muscles on the front of your shins engage while doing so.
    3. With the balls of your feet lifting up rather than your heels, this is the opposite movement of a calf raise.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering your feet in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with the soles of your feet fully contacting the ground.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can train the four muscle ankle dorsiflexor muscles on the front of your lower leg, the largest of which is the tibialis anterior.
