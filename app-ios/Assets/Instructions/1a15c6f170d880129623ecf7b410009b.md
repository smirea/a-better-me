## Setting up
1. After loading your desired amount of weight onto the machine, stand centered between its handles in a shoulder-width stance with your back facing the machine.
2. With extended elbows and your arms positioned by your sides, hinge at your hips while bending your knees until your hands are level with the handles.
    1. Keep your lower back near a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright, rather than meaningfully rounding your lower back while doing so.
3. Firmly grasp each handle with a neutral grip such that your palms are facing in toward each other.
4. By extending your hips and knees, stand upright with the handles while continuing to keep your elbows extended and your lower back near a neutral position.
## Exercise technique
1. Shrug your shoulders upward through your available range of motion while keeping your elbows extended, which will lift the handles upward.
    1. While doing so, keep your neck in a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright and looking straight ahead.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your shoulders to descend in a controlled manner through your available range of motion, which will lower the handles.
    1. At the end of this motion, your shoulders should ideally be positioned a little lower than where they are normally situated while standing at rest due to the downward stretch that the weight can provide.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise primarily trains your upper traps and levator scapulae.
