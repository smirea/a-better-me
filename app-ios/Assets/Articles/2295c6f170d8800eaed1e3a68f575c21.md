In contrast to squat variations, where you do not keep your back against a wall, wall squat variations allow you to keep a completely vertical torso position that shifts your center of mass closer toward your hips and farther away from your knees. This, correspondingly, results in wall squats with a greater lever arm of resistance acting on the knees and a shorter lever arm acting on the hips than in a free squat, where your torso naturally inclines to maintain your balance. As a result, wall squats can be more quad-dominant than free squats, which may train hip extensor muscles such as the gluteus maximus more effectively.

However, the way in which you perform a wall squat can influence how much load is shifted onto your quads or onto your hip extensor muscles. During a wall squat, you can transmit pressure to the wall through your upper back and pelvis in an even manner, or you can predominantly transmit pressure through one of these sites while still keeping both of them in contact with the wall. Biscarini et al. (2020) found that chiefly applying pressure to the wall through the pelvis shifted almost all the resistive torque onto the knees (which would train the quads more effectively) relative to the hips, which were minimally loaded. In contrast, applying all pressure to the wall through the upper back minimized knee loading while increasing hip loading (which would train the gluteus maximus more effectively), especially when participants focused on bearing weight through the forefoot (ball of the foot and toes) rather than the rearfoot. It is worth noting that even when participants used the technique that maximized hip loading, the magnitude of peak hip loading was approximately half the magnitude of peak knee loading during the condition that maximized knee loading. Consequently, you can modify your wall squat technique intentionally to make it as hip-dominant as possible, but you will likely be better served by simply performing a different exercise if you wish to train your hip extensor muscles most effectively.
