If you lack a V-bar attachment, you can still perform a neutral grip landmine T-bar row with a very similar technique if you grasp the barbell directly at the end of the shaft where the V-bar would otherwise be situated. With this modification, one hand would grasp the barbell, and the other would wrap around the hand holding it. Rowing can feel awkward when your hands are on top of each other, but you still use this approach in the absence of a V-bar attachment. Alternatively, certain landmine attachments have a pair of parallel handles that can be used to perform a neutral grip landmine T-bar row. If you have access to one of these attachments, you can slide it onto the barbell’s sleeve before adding the weight plates and row with the same technique described above. These landmine attachments can be quite comfortable to use, but V-bar attachments are much more commonly available.
