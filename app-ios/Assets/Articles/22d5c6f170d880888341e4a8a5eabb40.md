Some lifters prefer to train barefoot, but this type of ankle dorsiflexion exercise can be fairly uncomfortable without shoes. With the force from your bodyweight concentrated on the bottom of your heels, where the plantar fascia (AKA plantar aponeurosis) attaches, you may experience an unpleasant pinching sensation as this portion of the plantar fascia is compressively stressed against the heel bone without wearing a cushioned heel. This can especially be the case for lean individuals who have lower than typical fat reserves that can otherwise help cushion the heel, so wearing shoes can make this exercise more comfortable.
