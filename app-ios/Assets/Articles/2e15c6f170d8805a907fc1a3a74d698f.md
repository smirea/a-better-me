The barbell deficit Romanian deadlift is identical to the standard form of the barbell Romanian deadlift, except that it allows you to hip hinge through a greater range of motion before the plates contact the ground. Correspondingly, using the deficit variation does not provide any advantage over the standard Romanian deadlift if your hinging range of motion is not constrained by the plates contacting the ground at the end of the descent during a standard barbell Romanian deadlift.
