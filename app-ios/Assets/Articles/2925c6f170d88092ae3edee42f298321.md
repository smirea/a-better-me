Kelso shrugs are named after the American Paul Kelso (1937-2016), who authored numerous magazine articles and several books on resistance training, including *Kelso’s Shrug Book*. Paul Kelso advocated training direct scapular exercises from a variety of angles rather than relying solely on typical vertical shrugging variations and compound exercises such as rows to train the upper back. One of the scapular exercises about which he wrote is the horizontal shrug that directly trains scapular retraction and has come to be known as the Kelso shrug.
