Compared with other exercises, barbell exercises using a power rack may require more time initially to set them up properly, as evidenced by the fairly lengthy “setting up” section. However, subsequent sessions in which you perform the same exercise may require a shorter setup time than the first time you use it. To save time in future sessions, you can record the height you determined to be appropriate for positioning the J-hooks and safety pins. If your power rack has numbered pinholes, you can simply note the positions you used. Without numbered holes, you can note how many holes were above your J-hook position and how many holes were between your safety pin position and your J-hook position. Consequently, you will not need to take a trial-and-error approach when setting up your power rack each session.
