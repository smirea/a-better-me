You can optionally place a full diameter weight plate (like a 45lb (20kg) plate or thick bumper plate) or an aerobic step underneath, where you will keep your non-working leg’s foot. While not required, standing on a slightly elevated, sturdy surface can provide clearance for your working leg, so you do not have to lean forward to avoid brushing your foot against the ground. What you use as a step should be far enough in front of your working leg that your working leg’s foot does not contact it.
