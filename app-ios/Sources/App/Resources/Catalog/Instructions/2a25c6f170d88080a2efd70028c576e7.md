## Setting up

1. Place an inflated stability ball (which is also known as a Swiss ball or physio ball) on the ground.
2. Sit down on the center of the ball with your torso upright, knees bent, and feet on the floor.
3. Walk your feet forward while leaning your torso backward and rolling your buttocks closer to the front of the ball until your back is resting on top of the ball. Simultaneously allow your back to arch naturally.
   1. Your back and the back of your hip bones should be supported by the ball.
   2. After walking forward, your knees should be bent by about 90 degrees with your feet flat on the floor.
4. Place the fingertips of each hand lightly in contact with the sides of the back of your head as your upper arms and elbows are directed out away from your sides.
   1. Maintain this arm position throughout your set.
   2. If you wish to reduce the difficulty of this exercise, you can alternatively cross your arms across your chest.

## Exercise technique

1. Curl your spine as you pull the bottom of your ribs toward the tops of your hip bones in a controlled manner while tightening your abs and lifting your upper back off the ball.
   1. Once you feel that you can no longer curl your lower and middle back while keeping your hip bones and lower back against the ball, you have reached your end range. In this position, your shoulder blades should be above the ball.
   2. To train your abs dynamically, you should be intentionally rounding your back, since this motion is performed by contraction of your abs.
   3. You can imagine that a laser beam is shooting out from the middle of your chest, and you are trying to point that closer toward the middle point between your knees.
   4. Keep your feet planted on the ground and your lower body stationary throughout the set.
   5. Based on what feels most comfortable to you, you can mildly tuck your chin toward your chest at the start of the lifting phase, or you can keep your neck near a neutral position like you would normally do when standing upright.
2. Reverse the movement by uncurling your back in a controlled manner as you lower your upper back to the ball.
   1. After transitioning from a rounded position to a flat position, allow your lower back to arch around the ball.
   2. The imaginary laser beam shooting out from your chest should angle progressively backward as you let your ribs flare away from your hip bones.
   3. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should actively control it the entire time rather than dropping down in an abrupt fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your rectus abdominis, which is assisted by your obliques.
