## Setting up

1. With a cable station’s pulley positioned to knee height or lower, connect a straight bar attachment and select your weight with the loading pin.
   1. You can use any attachment that lets you use a shoulder-width underhand grip.
2. While facing the middle of the bar, squat down and firmly grasp it with straight elbows and your hands near shoulder-width apart as your palms face forward, resulting in an underhand grip.
3. Stand upright with the bar.

## Exercise technique

1. Curl the bar upward until your forearms are parallel to the ground by bending your elbows by about 90 degrees. Let the force from the cable bend your wrists to angle your palms forward and pull your knuckles below your wrists.
2. While keeping your forearms parallel to the ground, fully curl your wrists to angle your palms back toward you and lift your knuckles up above your wrists.
   1. With the exception of your hand moving through wrist motion, you should keep the rest of your body stationary throughout the set.
   2. To maintain the same degree of elbow bend that keeps your forearms in a horizontal position, you will be performing a static biceps curl during this exercise.
3. Reverse the movement in a controlled manner by uncurling your wrists to angle your palms away from you and move your knuckles lower than your wrists.
   1. Move through the full range of motion that is comfortable to your wrists as you continue keeping your forearms parallel to the ground.
   2. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should actively control the weight rather than letting the cable yank your hands down abruptly.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set.
   1. Following your last rep, let your forearms straighten to lower the bar before squatting down to set the bar on the ground.

## Description

This exercise can effectively train the six wrist flexor muscles on the palm side of your forearm.
