## Setting up
1. At a dual cable station, adjust each pulley to near waist height, select your desired load with the two weight stacks, and leave the cables without any handle attachments.
    1. A dual cable station that can situate the two pulleys no further apart than your wingspan is ideal to use for this variation.
    2. A dual cable station that has its pulleys rather far apart from each other (often with a long upper beam connecting each end of the station) is not as practical to use. With this type of equipment, the single arm cable overhead triceps extension may be preferable.
2. Stand centered between the pulleys facing toward the base of the cable station.
3. Firmly grasp the ball at the end of each cable with the hand that is closer to that cable.
4. Pivot your body 180 degrees while simultaneously bending your elbows and lifting them upward to cross the cables over each other.
    1. Finish with your hands close to each other behind your neck as your elbows are fully bent and raised to head height.
    2. The cable you are holding with your right hand should now be exerting a force angled to your left, while the cable you are holding with your left hand is exerting a force angled to your right.
5. Adopt a staggered stance by stepping forward with one foot, and slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips
    1. In this starting position, your upper arms should be angled out to your sides, upward, and somewhat forward as they are aligned with the cables exerting a force that is angled inward, downward, and somewhat backward.
## Exercise technique
1. Drive your hands upward, outward, and mildly forward in an arcing motion by extending your elbows until they are fully straightened. Keep your elbows up as your upper arms remain essentially stationary throughout the set.
    1. While your upper arms remain aligned with the direction in which the cables are pulling, this movement should resemble drawing a pair of katana from their sheaths behind your back.
    2. During this exercise, nearly all the motion should occur from your forearms hinging at your elbows without any shoulder motion. You can think of each elbow as the center of an analog clock, while your forearm is the hand that rotates about this stationary center elbow.
2. Reverse the motion in a controlled manner by bending your elbows and allowing your hands to move downward, in toward each other, and back behind your neck.
    1. Allow your elbows to bend fully, which typically produces a stretching sensation in your triceps.
    2. There is no need to perform this extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the cables abruptly yank your hands toward the pulleys in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
    1. After your last rep, perform the opposite of the pivoting maneuver you utilized while setting up to uncross the cables and face toward the base of the cable station.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop all three heads of your triceps, including its large long head, which may not be trained as well by compound pressing exercises.
