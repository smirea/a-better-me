## Setting up

1. Adjust the preacher bench’s seat to a height that allows you to rest your elbows and most of your triceps (on the back of your upper arms) on the sloped arm pad when sitting on the seat.
   1. The appropriate seat height should allow the exercise to feel stable and comfortable, and it will also typically result in the top of the pad being close to your armpits once you are seated.
   2. Some trial and error may be needed to find the ideal seat height.
2. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and straddle the seat while holding them firmly with your palms facing backward (resulting in an overhand grip that is also called a reverse grip).
   1. Initially stand with enough space between you and the pad that you will be able to curl the dumbbells upward.
   2. Maintain this reverse grip and avoid rotating your palms as you finish setting up and perform the exercise.
3. Curl the dumbbells upward by fully bending your elbows while lifting them upward to point straight ahead.
   1. Your arms and dumbbells should now be fully above the arm pad, which will make it easier to get into the starting position.
4. Step forward until your torso is very close to the pad before sitting down on the seat and resting your triceps and elbows on the pad.
   1. Continue keeping your elbows bent, which should result in your palms now facing forward.
   2. In the starting position and throughout the exercise, your wrists should be straight.

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping your elbows and triceps in contact with the arm pad, lower the dumbbells in a slow and controlled manner until you have fully straightened your elbows.
   1. While your forearms arc forward and downward through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary.
   2. You do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, but you should avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the dumbbells down.
   3. After initially pointing forward away from you in the starting position, your palms should be angled backward and downward at the bottom of the rep.
2. Reverse the movement by bending your elbows to curl the dumbbells upward and back toward you in an arcing motion until your elbows are fully bent.
   1. Continue keeping your elbows and triceps snugly against the arm pad.
   2. If your preacher bench allows you to curl the dumbbells past the point when your forearms are completely vertical, you can finish curling once your forearms are fully vertical (rather than once your elbows are fully bent), since the target muscles will not be resisted beyond this point.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
   1. Once you have finished your last rep with your elbows fully bent, stand upright to lift your arms off the pad, and step backward before lowering the dumbbells.

## Description

This exercise can develop your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis, but it may train your biceps somewhat less effectively than a curl with an underhand or neutral grip.
