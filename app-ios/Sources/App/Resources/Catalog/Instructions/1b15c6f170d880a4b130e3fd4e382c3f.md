## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable to approximately hip height, connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. Stand facing the cable station with the cable just in front of one of your arms.
3. With the hand in front of the cable, firmly grasp the handle with an underhand grip that directs your palm up with a bent elbow.
4. Take a step backward so that you feel tension from the cable as the weight stack separates.
5. As you keep your upper arm aligned with your torso, hinge at your hips to sit your buttocks back, and incline your torso so that it is fairly close to being in a horizontal position.
    1. If you wish to do so, you can place your free hand up against the cable station’s beam for additional stability.
    2. Maintain this bent-over-torso position throughout your set.
6. Drive your elbow backward and upward until your upper arm is approximately parallel to the ground while you allow tension from the cable to keep your elbow bent.
    1. Your elbow should now be somewhat above the side of your torso (which would look like behind your torso if you were standing upright) rather than being aligned with your torso.
    2. In this starting position, your elbow should be bent by at least 90 degrees.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your upper arm parallel to the ground, progressively straighten your elbow to drive the handle backward and upward in an arcing motion until you have fully straightened your elbow.
    1. As you move your elbow, you should keep the rest of your body stationary.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your elbow to bend in a controlled manner as you bring the handle downward and forward in an arcing fashion until you have returned to the starting position with your elbow bent by at least 90 degrees.
    1. Allow your elbow to continue bending as long as you feel tension from the cable pulling you toward a greater degree of bending.
    2. Continue keeping your upper arm parallel to the ground.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
This exercise trains all three heads of your triceps, which together constitute the majority of muscle mass in your upper arms.
