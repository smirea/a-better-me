## Setting up

1. Place a furniture slider on the ground.
2. Lie down with your back on the ground, and situate the bottom of one heel on top of the slider with your knees fully bent and feet hip-width apart.
   1. You can rest the back of your head on the ground and leave your arms on the ground by your sides or in whichever position you prefer.
3. On the side not in contact with the slider, lift your foot off the ground by marching that side’s thigh up with your knee bent.
   1. Statically keep this leg lifted off the ground throughout the set you perform with your other side.
   2. All the subsequent directions will apply to the leg contacting the slider.

## Exercise technique

1. Lift your hips up off the ground by driving your heel down into the slider, squeezing your glute, and tightening your abs.
   1. After performing this glute bridge motion, you should now be able to draw a straight line from your knee through your hip to your shoulder when viewed from the side.
   2. Focus on moving your hip and avoid arching your lower back excessively.
   3. Throughout the set, keep your hip bones level with each other rather than letting the hip bone on the side of your free leg drop down or hike up above your working leg’s hip bone.
2. Without bending your hip, move your foot forward by progressively straightening your knee in a controlled manner until it is almost fully straightened.
   1. Continue keeping your glute squeezed, abs tensed, and pressure concentrated through your heel throughout the exercise.
   2. All the motion should occur from your shin moving at your knee joint, while you are still able to draw a straight line from your knee through your hip to your shoulder.
   3. At the end of this lowering phase, your buttocks should just barely be above the ground.
   4. You do not have to perform this movement with an extremely slow tempo, but you should avoid letting your foot abruptly slide forward in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Reverse the movement by driving your heel toward your buttock to bend your knee and pull the slider backward.
   1. You should finish with your knee close to being fully bent as the slider is vertically under your knee.
   2. If you experience a notably uncomfortable cramping sensation in your hamstrings when your knee is fully bent, you can stop bending your knee just before reaching that point.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set and are ready to lower your buttocks to the ground by bending at your hip before repeating the exercise with your other side.

## Description

While statically engaging your gluteus maximus, this exercise can train all your hamstrings, which are assisted by your gracilis, sartorius, and gastrocnemius.
