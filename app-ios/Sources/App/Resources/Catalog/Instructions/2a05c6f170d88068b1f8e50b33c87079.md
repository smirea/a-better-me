## Setting up

1. Position a cable station’s pulley to its lowest height, select your weight with the loading pin, and connect an EZ bar (also known as a multi-grip curl bar) attachment to the cable.
2. While facing the middle of the bar, squat down or hinge at your hips to grasp the bar firmly with approximately a shoulder width semi-underhand grip such that your palms are directed at an angle that is facing forward and inward.
   1. With the zig-zag-like shape of the EZ bar attachment, you may have to rotate it initially before a near shoulder width semi-underhand grip is accessible on the bar.
3. While keeping your elbows straight, stand upright with the bar just in front of your legs.
4. If you do not currently feel resistance from the cable or are standing too close to the pulley to curl the bar without bumping the station, take a step backward while keeping your elbows straight.
   1. The appropriate positioning should allow you to feel resistance from the cable throughout the curl’s full range of motion.
   2. When stepping backward, the cable will now have a mildly diagonal line of the pull rather than being vertical. To maintain a straight elbow position with a mildly diagonal line of pull, you will have to raise your arms slightly in front of your body instead of keeping them pinned to your sides.

## Exercise technique

1. Curl the bar upward and back toward you in an arcing motion by bending your elbows until they are fully bent.
   1. Rather than swinging them further forward, keep your upper arms fixed in the same position in which they started (i.e., a little in front of you).
   2. While your forearms move through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering the bar in a controlled manner until you have fully straightened your elbows.
   1. Nothing else should move except for your forearms lowering in an arcing manner through elbow motion.
   2. While you do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, you should avoid letting the cable’s resistance abruptly swing the bar down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
   1. After your last rep, step forward and squat down or hinge at your hips to set the bar down.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
