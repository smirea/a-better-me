## Setting up
1. Lie down with your back on the ground or an exercise mat, and bend your knees to at least 90 degrees to position the soles of your feet flat on the ground.
    1. You can position your feet shoulder-width apart, but it is up to you whether you would like them closer together or farther apart.
    2. Based on what feels comfortable to you, you can point your toes forward or slightly out to the sides.
    3. Specifically how much you wish to bend your knees (and correspondingly bring your heels toward your buttocks) is also up to your preference and which position feels comfortable to you.
    4. You can rest the back of your head on the ground and leave your arms on the ground by your sides.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift your hips up off the ground by driving your heels into the ground and squeezing your glutes.
    1. Finish with your hips fully extended, allowing you to draw a straight line from your knee through your hip to your shoulder when viewed from the side. Your thighs and torso should naturally become aligned when you fully contract your glutes.
    2. Focus on moving your hips and avoid excessively arching your lower back. Rather than flaring up (which occurs from lower back arching), the bottom of your ribs should remain pointing down toward your hip bones.
2. While keeping your hips up off the ground, lift one foot off the ground to march that side’s knee up toward your chest.
    1. March that thigh up through your hip’s available range of motion, which will vary among different individuals.
    2. Avoid letting your buttocks drop down, or either hip bone tilt down toward the ground as you march. Strive to keep your right and left hip bones fully level.
    3. As your one leg marches up, the hip muscles on the same side of your leg in contact with the ground will have to work more intensely to keep your buttocks bridged up.
3. Reverse the marching motion by lowering your thigh in a controlled manner until that side’s foot returns to contacting the ground.
    1. Continue keeping your buttocks fully lifted above the ground.
4. March up with your other side, and then lower that side’s foot back to the ground in the same way you just performed during steps 2 and 3.
5. With both feet in contact with the ground, lower your hips in a controlled manner until you can lightly contact the ground with your buttocks.
6. Alternate between steps 1-5 until you have completed your set.
    1. You complete one rep each time you perform a cycle of steps 1-5.
## Description
While your hamstrings and adductor magnus can assist to a degree, this exercise primarily targets your gluteus maximus.
