## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells, and stand upright in a near hip width stance with your arms hanging down by your sides as your elbows are straight.
## Exercise technique
1. Turn your palms in toward your sides to adopt a neutral grip, which is also called a hammer grip.
2. Curl the dumbbells upward in an arcing manner by bending your elbows while keeping your palms facing in toward each other.
    1. Keep your wrists straight, torso upright, and elbows by your sides, rather than swinging your elbows forward through shoulder motion.
    2. While your forearms move through elbow joint motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary
    3. At the top of the curl, your elbows should be fully bent with your forearms touching your biceps.
3. Rotate your palms back toward your shoulders to adopt an underhand grip.
4. Lower the dumbbells in a controlled manner by allowing your elbows to straighten progressively without rotating your palms.
    1. Since you will maintain an underhand grip during this lowering phase, your palms should be facing upward when your forearms are parallel to the ground before facing forward once you have fully straightened your elbows.
    2. You do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, but you should avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the dumbbells down.
5. Alternate between steps 1-4 until you have completed your set.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively develop your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
