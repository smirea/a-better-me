## Setting up

1. Position an adjustable bench at a moderate incline angle, which is near 45 degrees.
   1. Whether the bench is set to a 45-degree angle or a somewhat steeper angle will not meaningfully affect the stimulus this exercise provides to your biceps and other arm muscles. However, using an angle closer to 60 degrees may result in setting up being easier if you are using heavy weights.
2. Pick up a pair of dumbbells, and straddle the bench as you face toward the elevated head of the bench.
3. While keeping your toes on the ground, position the front of your torso on top of the bench with your chest at the head of the bench, so that your head is above the end of the bench.
   1. Transitioning to lying on top of the bench may be easier if you initially position the dumbbells against the head of the bench, lower your torso to the bench in a push-up-like motion, and then lift the dumbbells out to the sides off the bench to hang down.
   2. Once on the bench, your neck should be near a neutral position, like when you are looking straight ahead when normally standing upright.
4. With your arms hanging straight down toward the ground with straight elbows, direct your palms to face forward as you hold the dumbbells with an underhand grip.
   1. You will maintain this underhand grip throughout your set.

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping your upper arms directed straight down toward the ground, curl the dumbbells upward in an arcing motion by bending your elbows until they are fully bent.
   1. While your forearms move through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
   2. Avoid swinging your elbows forward.
2. After your elbows have fully bent, reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled arcing manner until your elbows have returned to a fully straightened position with your forearms directed straight down toward the ground.
   1. Continue keeping your upper arms directed straight down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
