## Setting up
1. Sit on the ground with an upright torso and your legs straight out in front of you.
    1. The back of your heels should be on the ground as your feet are close together.
2. Place your hands on the ground approximately shoulder-width apart, slightly behind your hips, with your fingers spread apart and elbows straight.
    1. Rotating your hands such that your middle fingers point straight out to your sides is appropriate. However, you can feel free to rotate your hands to point in whichever direction you find most comfortable.
    2. The proper hand placement should allow your arms to point straight down toward the ground during the exercise.
3. Pull your shoulder blades back together to assume a “proud chest” posture.
    1. Rather than being slumped forward, your chest should be directed slightly upward.
    2. As a cue, you can think about pinching a pencil with your shoulder blades.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift your hips up off the ground by driving your heels into the ground and squeezing your glutes.
    1. At the top, you should be able to draw a straight line from your legs through your hips to your shoulders when viewed from the side.
    2. Keep your elbows straight as the weight you bear through your arms predominantly goes through the bases of your palms (AKA heels of your hands) rather than closer to your knuckles.
    3. Continue keeping your shoulder blades pulled back with a “proud chest” as you avoid letting your shoulders round forward.
    4. Keep your neck near a neutral position, like when you are normally standing upright and looking straight ahead.
    5. Squeezing your glutes can help you avoid excessively arching your lower back, which should exhibit its natural mild curvature. As a cue for engaging your glutes, you can think about trying to crush a walnut between them.
2. Shift your weight into one foot on the ground, and lift the other foot off the ground with a straight knee. Except for the leg you lift, keep the rest of your body in the same position you adopted from step 1.
    1. Aiming to lift your leg moderately above the ground is appropriate, but you can decide specifically how high you lift it based on your preference and what enables you to keep your hip bones symmetrical.
    2. Focus on keeping your right and left hip bones level with each other as you avoid letting the hip bone on the side of your free leg tilt down or up. You can imagine balancing a mug of water on the middle of your waist. If laser beams were shooting out from the center of your chest and your imaginary belt buckle, the two lasers should be parallel to each other throughout your set.
3. Lower your heel to the ground in a controlled manner.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 as you switch which foot remains on the ground each rep.
    1. Prioritize keeping your grounded leg completely in line with your torso while you resist letting your hips gradually lower toward the ground as the set progresses.
5. After completing the lowering phase of your last rep, bend your hips to return to sitting on the ground.
## Description
This exercise can statically train your gluteus maximus, hamstrings, and spinal extensor muscles that run along your entire back.
