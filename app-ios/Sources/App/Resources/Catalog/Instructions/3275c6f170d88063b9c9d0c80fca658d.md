## Setting up
1. After placing the base of a kettlebell on the ground, sit down on the ground with the kettlebell directly in front of your hips with your legs straddling the weight.
    1. For comfort, you optionally can place an exercise mat on the ground where you will lie.
2. Use both hands to grasp the horns (side handles) of the kettlebell and lift it up onto the midline of your pelvis with the handle facing up.
    1. The base of the kettlebell should be resting on your pubic bones, which lie directly below your bladder and just above your genitals. If you situate the weight above or below your pubic bones, applying pressure to those areas can naturally be uncomfortable.
    2. Continue holding the horns of the kettlebell with your palms facing in toward each other throughout the exercise.
3. While keeping the kettlebell resting on your pelvis, lean your torso backward and bend your knees to at least 90 degrees to position the soles of your feet flat on the ground.
    1. You can position your feet shoulder-width apart, but it is up to you whether you would like them closer together or farther apart.
    2. Based on what feels comfortable to you, you can point your toes forward or slightly out to the sides.
    3. Specifically how much you wish to bend your knees (and correspondingly bring your heels toward your buttocks) is also up to your preference and which position feels comfortable to you.
    4. In this position, you can rest the back of your head on the ground.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift your hips up off the ground by driving your heels into the ground and squeezing your glutes while continuing to hold the kettlebell on your pubic bones.
    1. At the top, you should be able to draw a straight line from your knee through your hip to your shoulder when viewed from the side. Your thighs and torso should naturally become aligned when you fully contract your glutes.
    2. Focus on moving your hips and avoid excessively arching your lower back. Rather than flaring up (which occurs from lower back arching), the bottom of your ribs should remain pointing down toward your hip bones.
    3. If you wish, you can hold the full glute squeeze at the top for a moment to help you feel your glutes engage.
    4. As a cue for engaging your glutes, you can think about trying to crush a walnut between them at the top.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering your hips in a controlled manner until you can lightly contact the ground with your buttocks.
    1. You do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, but you should actively control the descent with your glutes rather than letting your hips drop abruptly.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
    1. After your last rep, you can march your knees up toward you and rock forward into an upright torso position while placing the base of the kettlebell onto the ground between your thighs.
## Description
This exercise will chiefly train your gluteus maximus, which may be assisted by your adductor magnus and piriformis to a lesser degree.
