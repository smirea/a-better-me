## Setting up

1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and stand upright with approximately a shoulder width stance while holding the dumbbells at your sides with extended elbows as your palms are facing in toward your sides (i.e., in a neutral grip position).

## Exercise technique

1. Brace your core by tensing the front and sides of your abdominals outward, like you would if you were bracing for someone to strike your stomach with a punch or forcible slap.
   1. Continue bracing your core throughout the set.
2. Raise the dumbbells out in front of you until your upper arms are approximately parallel with the ground.
   1. If you wish to do so, elevating the dumbbells higher up, beyond when your upper arms are parallel to the floor, is a completely viable modification to this exercise’s technique.
3. Reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner back to your sides.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your front delt while also training your upper pec major, lateral delt, and supraspinatus.
