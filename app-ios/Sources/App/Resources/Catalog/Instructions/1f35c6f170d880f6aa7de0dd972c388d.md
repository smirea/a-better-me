## Setting up
1. Strap an ankle weight to each of your ankles, and lie down on your back with straight legs as your feet are hip-width apart.
    1. On the ground, your legs and torso should be in the same position in which you exhibit when normally standing upright.
    2. As an alternative to keeping both knees straight, you have the option of keeping one knee (on the side that you are not about to train) bent with that side’s foot flat on the floor if you are more comfortable in that position.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping one foot on the ground, lift the other leg up off the ground through your hip’s available range of motion. Keep your knee straight and the rest of your body stationary.
    1. How high you are able to lift the leg will be influenced by your hamstring flexibility, since flexing your hip with a straight knee position stretches your hamstrings.
    2. While performing this movement with the appropriate technique, you will likely feel the muscles on the front of your upper thigh contracting.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your working leg to descend in a controlled manner until the back of its heel lightly contacts the ground.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your hip flexor muscles, including your psoas major, iliacus, rectus femoris, and tensor fascia latae.
