## Setting up
1. Adjust the machine’s foot plate to a height that aligns the machine’s pivot points with the height of your shoulders once you are standing on the foot plate.
    1. On each side of the machine, the pivot point is the center about which the lever arm rotates, and it is denoted by a small dot or circle in some machines.
    2. Aligning the center of your shoulders with the pivot points can help this exercise feel more comfortable and natural compared to if the foot plate height resulted in your shoulders being above or below the pivot points.
    3. To confirm whether you selected an appropriate foot plate height, you will have to stand on it and visually check where your shoulders are positioned relative to the pivot points, so you may have to try out different settings before finding one that best suits your body.
    4. A foot plate height that aligns your shoulders with the pivot points will commonly allow you to hold the handles with slightly bent elbows.
2. Add weight plates of your desired load to the machine, and stand on the foot plate with the handles situated just in front of your thighs.
3. Firmly grasp the handles with slightly bent elbows as your palms are facing in toward each other.
4. Optionally lean your torso slightly forward at your hips by sitting your buttocks slightly backward with a slight knee bend.
    1. People commonly find lateral raises to feel more comfortable with a slight forward torso lean, but you can alternatively stand fully upright if you prefer to do so.
    2. Maintain your preferred torso position throughout your set, as you subsequently move your shoulders without moving your lower body or torso.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift the handles by driving your elbows out and away from your sides until your upper arms are close to parallel with the ground.
    1. Allow your shoulder blades to move naturally rather than trying to keep them in any particular position.
    2. Focusing on moving your elbows in an arcing manner throughout this exercise can help you target the appropriate shoulder muscles.
2. Reverse the motion in a controlled manner by lowering your elbows in toward your sides to return the handles to the starting position.
    1. While there is no need to use an extremely slow tempo, you should be actively controlling the weight the entire time. Do not let the weight abruptly swing your arms downward.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your lateral delt and supraspinatus, which are assisted by your front delt and upper trap.
