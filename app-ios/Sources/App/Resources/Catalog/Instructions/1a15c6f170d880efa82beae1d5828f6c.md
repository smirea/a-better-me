## Setting up
1. At a dual cable station, adjust each cable to a height approximately level with your shoulders or slightly above them, clip in a D-handle attachment, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. After standing centered between the cables with your sides facing the cable stacks, take a step backward, raise your arms in front of you until they are approximately parallel to the ground, and side-step toward one cable.
3. Reach across your body with your arm that is opposite to the cable to grasp its handle as your palm is facing the cable stack, before side-stepping toward the other cable and grasping its handle with your free hand.
4. Return to being centered between the cables with your arms crossed in front of your torso, with slightly bent elbows, as the cable stacks are situated a little in front of you.
    1. In this position, your arms should resemble an “X-shape” with each other.
    2. Maintain slightly bent elbows throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Pull the handles out and away from each other as you move your arms backward until they form a “T-shape” with your torso.
2. Reverse the motion by bringing the handles inward and toward each other in a controlled manner until they have crossed and returned to their starting position.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your rear delt, which is assisted by your traps and rhomboids, along with your rotator cuff’s infraspinatus, teres minor, and supraspinatus.
