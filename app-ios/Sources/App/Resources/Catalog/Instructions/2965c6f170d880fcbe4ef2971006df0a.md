## Setting up
1. Position a cable station’s pulley to its lowest height, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. Connect a rope attachment or a straight bar attachment to the cable.
    1. Except for specifically how you grasp the attachment, you can perform this exercise with the same technique with either implement, so you can select whichever one you prefer and feel free to try each one out to determine if one feels more comfortable than the other for you.
3. In a shoulder width stance, stand with the cable station directly behind your back as the attachment is just in front of your feet, and the pulley is centered between your feet.
4. Squat down and firmly grasp the attachment with both hands as your wrists are mostly straight.
    1. If you are using a rope attachment, grasp the ends of the rope with a neutral grip as your palms are facing in toward each other. Place the outside of your index fingers in contact with the flat surface of the hard plastic end caps of the rope attachment.
    2. If you are using a straight bar attachment, grasp it with a near shoulder width overhand grip as your palms are facing backward.
5. Stand upright while holding the attachment close to your body with straight elbows and your arms pointing down toward the ground.
6. For additional stability, sit your buttocks slightly backward to adopt a very mild forward torso lean.
    1. While performing a very small hip hinge motion that results in your knees barely bending, keep your chest up and lower back near a neutral position, like when you are normally standing upright.
    2. In the starting position, you should feel tension from the cable to provide resistance through this exercise’s full range of motion. If you do not currently feel tension from the cable, take a step forward to prompt the weight stack to separate.
## Exercise technique
1. Brace your core by tensing the front and sides of your abdominals outward, like you would if you were bracing for someone to strike your stomach with a punch or forcible slap.
    1. Continue bracing your core throughout the set.
2. While keeping your elbows straight, sweep your arms forward and upward in an arcing manner until your arms are approximately parallel to the ground, outstretched in front of you.
    1. While your arm moves from motion at your shoulders, your legs and torso should remain stationary throughout your set.
    2. Allow your shoulder blades to move naturally rather than trying to keep them in any particular position.
3. Reverse the movement by allowing your arms to sweep downward and backward in a controlled arcing manner.
    1. You should finish with your arms pointing down toward the ground with your hands just in front of your legs.
    2. While you do not need to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, avoid letting the cable’s resistance abruptly yank your arms downward.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your front delt while also training your upper pec major, lateral delt, and supraspinatus.
