## Setting up

1. Face the middle of an EZ bar loaded with your desired weight, whether that be a fixed-weight EZ bar or an adjustable plate-loaded EZ bar
2. With a slightly outside shoulder-width overhand grip, firmly grasp the bar at a point where the grips angle downward as they move out to the sides of the bar.
   1. This angled grip should result in your hands being mildly tilted toward the thumb-side of your forearms.
   2. With the zig-zag-like shape of the EZ bar, you may have to rotate it before this grip is accessible on the bar.
   3. A slightly outside shoulder-width grip works well for many lifters, but feel free to try out somewhat different grip widths to determine if any particular hand positions feel best to you as an individual.
3. Stand upright with the bar, and hold it just in front of your legs with straight elbows.

## Exercise technique

1. Lift the bar upward by driving your elbows upward and out to your sides through a range of motion that feels comfortable to your shoulders while bending your elbows.
   1. Your mobility will affect how high you are comfortably able to lift the bar, and there is no need to force your shoulders into a range that provokes any discomfort.
   2. While different individuals may be able to lift higher up and others not quite as high, people often can lift their upper arms until their elbows are near shoulder height for a comfortable and effective range of motion.
   3. The bar should remain close to your torso throughout the exercise as it moves vertically.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your elbows to descend in toward your sides as you lower the bar in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with straight elbows.
   1. There is no need to lower the bar with an extremely slow tempo, but you should actively control the descent rather than letting the weight abruptly pull your arms down.
   2. At the end of the lowering phase, the bar should be situated in front of your thighs.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your lateral delt, upper trap, and supraspinatus, which are assisted by your front delt.
