## Setting up
1. Prepare the barbell with your desired load by adding weight plates to it or leaving the barbell unloaded, which is recommended if performing this exercise for the first time.
    1. You can initially set the bar up on the floor or from an elevated position, such as from a power rack.
2. While facing the middle of the bar, squat down or hinge at your hips with extended elbows.
3. Grasp the bar with approximately a shoulder width grip and an underhand grip such that your palms are directed forward.
4. While keeping your elbows extended, stand upright while gripping the bar.
## Exercise technique
1. Curl the bar upward by bending at your elbows while simultaneously driving your upper arms back behind your torso.
    1. The combined motion at your shoulders and elbows should result in the bar moving vertically upward in a straight line (as opposed to the arcing bar path that occurs with a typical barbell biceps curl).
2. After your elbows have fully flexed and your upper arms are positioned behind your torso, reverse the motion by lowering the bar in a controlled manner by simultaneously bringing your upper arms forward and extending your elbows.
    1. After finishing the lower phase of this exercise, your elbows should have returned to a fully extended position, and your upper arms should once again be positioned at your sides.
3. Alternate between curling the bar up and lowering it down until you have completed your set.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
