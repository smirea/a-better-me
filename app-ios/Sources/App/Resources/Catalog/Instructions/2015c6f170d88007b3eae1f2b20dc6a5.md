## Setting up

The key goal of your setup is to adjust the machine and position yourself in a manner that results in your knee joint being aligned with the machine’s pivot point, which is the center about which the machine’s lever arm rotates, that is denoted by a small dot or circle in some machines. After setting up appropriately, you should be in a position that results in you feeling comfortable and stable during the exercise. The features you can adjust will vary based on the specific machine model you use, so certain setup steps described in the instructions may not be applicable to a particular machine model. As you set up, some trial and error may be needed while you adjust the machine and position yourself to find what works best for your individual body proportions.

1. Adjust the seat’s back pad to a position that aligns your knees with the machine’s pivot point while sitting up against the back pad with your thighs resting on the seat.
2. Adjust the machine’s lever arm (that will align with your shins) to its highest position that allows you to begin each rep with your knees fully straightened.
   1. Certain machines can be used for leg extensions or hamstring curls based on how the lever arm is positioned.
3. Adjust the lower leg pad to a position that places it slightly above the back of your heels (i.e., on your Achilles tendons) when seated against the backrest, with your legs on top of the lower leg pad and your knees straightened.
   1. Adjusting this setting exclusively moves the lower leg pad without moving the lever arm, in contrast to step 2, where the machine lever arm and shin pad move together.
4. After selecting your desired weight with the machine’s loading pin, sit down on its seat with your back against the back pad and your lower legs on top of the lower leg pad.
5. Adjust the thigh pad to press down snugly against your lower thighs.
   1. In this starting position, your knees should be straight and aligned with the machine’s pivot point.

## Exercise technique

1. For additional stability, firmly grasp the handles and continue holding them throughout your set.
2. Pull your toes and feet back toward your shins by moving at your ankles, and maintain this ankle position throughout your set.
   1. You should feel the muscles on the front of your shins contract while doing so.
   2. This places your ankles in the same position in which they would be at the bottom of a calf raise on a step. It is the opposite of the ankle motion you would perform when lifting your heels up off the ground during a calf raise.
   3. Holding your ankles in this position can increase your hamstring curl strength (as explained in detail under the “notes” section).
3. Drive both heels down and back toward you as you bend your knees to pull the lower leg pad under your thighs through the available range of motion that the machine allows.
   1. At some point, the machine will stop you from bending your knees any further, but most machines will allow you to bend your knees by at least 90 degrees.
   2. While your lower legs move through motion at your knees, you should keep the rest of your body stationary throughout your set.
4. After you have fully bent your knees, fully straighten one knee to lift that side’s leg off the lower leg pad, leaving only one leg in contact with the pad.
5. With the leg still in contact with the lower leg pad, allow that side’s knee to straighten progressively in a controlled manner while resisting the weight on the way up. Continue doing so until you have returned to the starting position with both knees fully straightened.
   1. While there is no need to use an extremely slow tempo for this movement, you should be actively controlling the weight the entire time, rather than letting the weight abruptly swing your lower leg up.
6. Repeatedly perform steps 3-5 as you alternate each rep which leg you keep in contact with the lower leg pad during steps 4 and 5 until you have completed your set.
   1. If you wish to do so, you can alternatively complete one set where you perform step 5 of each rep exclusively with one side, rest, and then complete another set where you perform step 5 of every rep with your opposite side. 
   2. During each rep, continue to move the leg pad down with both legs as you control it on the way up with only one leg.

## Description

While emphasizing eccentric strength training, this exercise can effectively train all your hamstrings, which are assisted by your gracilis, sartorius, and gastrocnemius.
