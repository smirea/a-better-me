## Setting up

1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to near hip height, connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your weight with the loading pin.
2. Stand with the pulley by the side of your body you will train during this set, and firmly grasp the handle with that side’s hand.
3. With your palm directed forward, take a step out to the side away from the pulley until the weight stack separates and you feel resistance from the cable.
   1. Allow the cable’s resistance to lift your arm out to the side with a straight elbow, which should result in your arm being angled diagonally outward and downward.
   2. Situate your free arm wherever you prefer throughout the exercise, since all the instructions apply to the side on which you are holding the handle.
4. Take a step forward with your foot further away from the cable to adopt a staggered stance that allows you to feel stable and balanced.
5. As you are standing with an upright torso, pull your shoulder blades back together with a “proud chest” posture that lifts your chest slightly up toward the ceiling.
   1. In the starting position, your shoulders should be level as your chest is facing the same direction as your imaginary belt buckle, rather than being twisted toward the pulley.

## Exercise technique

1. Bend your elbow to curl the handle in toward your shoulder while keeping your upper arm stationary.
   1. You should finish with your elbow fully bent as your forearm is contacting your biceps or very close to doing so.
   2. While you move your forearm through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain still throughout your set.
   3. Resist the urge to swing your elbow forward through shoulder motion, and keep your wrist mostly straight.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your elbow to straighten in a controlled manner as you let your forearm arc outward toward the pulley.
   1. Finish this step with your elbow fully straightened.
   2. While you do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, avoid letting the cable abruptly yank the handle back in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
