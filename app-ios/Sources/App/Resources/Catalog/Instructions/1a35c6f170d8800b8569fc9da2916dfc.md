## Setting up

1. Lie down with your back on the ground near an exercise bench or similar sturdy elevated surface, such as a plyometric box or bed.
2. Lift up your legs to position the back of your heels on top of the bench with straight knees.
   1. If you are too close to keep your knees straight on top of the bench, you can scoot backward until you can do so.
   2. You can situate your feet near shoulder-width apart from each other, but it is up to your preference if you would like to position them somewhat closer together or wider apart.
   3. In this position, you can rest the back of your head on the ground and leave your arms on the ground by your sides.
   4. As a modification, you can alternatively perform this exercise with your knees slightly bent if you wish to do so.

## Exercise technique

1. Lift your hips up off the ground by driving your heels into the ground until your hips are fully extended.
   1. At the end of the lifting phase, you should be able to draw a straight line from your thighs through your hips to your torso.
   2. Focus on moving your hips and avoid arching your lower back excessively.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering your hips in a controlled manner until you are able to lightly contact the ground with your buttocks.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise will primarily train your gluteus maximus and three of your four hamstring muscles.
