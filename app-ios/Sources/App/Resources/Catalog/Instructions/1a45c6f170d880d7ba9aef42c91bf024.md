## Setting up

1. Lie down on your back with straight legs as your feet are together.
2. Raise your arms straight back behind your head with straight elbows so that your arms are aligned with your legs and torso.
   1. Except for being rotated by 90 degrees, your body should be in the same position when you are standing upright and reaching your arms straight up overhead.
   2. Your palms should be facing up away from the ground.

## Exercise technique

1. While only allowing your knees to bend mildly, lift both legs up simultaneously with your torso as you crunch up to touch your feet with your hands.
   1. Sweep your arms toward your feet in an arcing manner until you can touch them with your arms reaching forward and upward. Avoid rapidly swinging your arms in an effort to build momentum.
   2. To touch your feet, sweep your arms forward until they are angled upward and forward.
   3. Keep your elbows straight and your knees as close to straight as your hamstring flexibility permits.
   4. Lift the entirety of your back off the ground, resulting in only your buttocks contacting the ground at the top of the movement.
   5. Rather than trying to keep your back flat, you should intentionally curl your spine to train your abs dynamically.
   6. Keep your neck near a neutral position throughout the exercise, like when you are normally standing upright and looking straight ahead.
2. Reverse the movement in a controlled manner by lowering your legs and torso at the same time.
   1. At the bottom, lightly tap your heels to the ground, and keep your abs tensed to avoid excessively arching your lower back.
   2. Fluidly lower your legs and torso at the same rate, resulting in your upper back contacting the ground at the same time as your heels.
   3. Finish with your whole body forming a straight line like the starting position.
   4. While you do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, make sure that you actively control the entire movement rather than letting gravity rapidly pull you down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your rectus abdominis (the “six-pack muscle”) while also training your obliques and hip flexors.
