## Setting up
1. You can select either a fixed weight EZ bar or an adjustable plate-loaded EZ bar, which are functionally interchangeable for this exercise.
2. While facing the middle of the bar, squat down or hinge at your hips with extended elbows.
    1. If the EZ bar, if already positioned in an elevated rack at mid-thigh level or above, you naturally would not have to squat down or hinge at your hips before picking it up.
3. Grasp the bar with a closer than shoulder width grip and a semi-underhand grip (AKA semi-neutral grip) such that your palms are directed at an angle that is facing forward and inward.
    1. With the zig-zag-like shape of the EZ bar, you may have to rotate it before a closer than shoulder width semi-underhand grip is accessible on the bar.
4. While keeping your elbows extended, stand fully upright while gripping the bar.
## Exercise technique
1. Curl the bar upward by bending at your elbows while keeping your upper arms fixed at your sides (rather than allowing your upper arms to swing forward).
2. After your elbows have fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the bar in a controlled manner until your elbows have returned to an extended position.
    1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arms positioned by your sides.
3. Alternate between curling the bar upwards and lowering it down until you have completed your set.
## Description
Like other curls, this exercise can effectively train the biceps and brachialis in your upper arm along with the brachioradialis in your forearm.
