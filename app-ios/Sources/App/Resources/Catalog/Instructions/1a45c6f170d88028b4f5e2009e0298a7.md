## Setting up

1. Position yourself with your palms, knees, and toes on the ground as your elbows are straight, and your knees are bent.
   1. Your upper arms should be directed straight down such that they are approximately perpendicular to the ground.

## Exercise technique

1. Straighten one knee to move your foot behind you and position the bottom of its toes onto the ground before doing the same with your other leg.
   1. You should now be in the same position as the top of a push-up.
2. Tighten your abdominal muscles and squeeze your glutes to maintain a flat back position while keeping your elbows and knees straight.
   1. Doing so keeps your hips held up (but not excessively hiked up) rather than letting them sag downward, which would result in an exaggerated arch of your lower back.
   2. In this position, you should be able to draw a straight line from your legs through your hips to your torso.
   3. Do not allow your chest to sag, which would result in your shoulder blades being pinched back together.
   4. Keep your neck in a neutral position, as you would normally do when standing upright.
3. Statically hold the position you adopted after performing steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise statically trains your rectus abdominis and obliques as they work to maintain a rigid trunk position.
