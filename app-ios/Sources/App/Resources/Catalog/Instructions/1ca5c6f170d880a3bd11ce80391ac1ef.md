## Setting up

1. Connect to the lat pulldown cable an attachment with a pair of parallel handles that are outside shoulder-width apart.
   1. If you do not have access to this type of attachment, you can train the same muscles by selecting a similar lat pulldown variation from this database, such as the overhand wide grip cable lat pulldown, which can be performed with more common attachments.
2. Select your weight with the loading pin, and adjust the thigh pad to a height that lets you position your thighs snugly against it while sitting with your feet flat on the floor.
3. While facing the bar and standing above the seat, firmly grasp the handles with a neutral grip, such that your palms are directed in toward each other.
   1. For the most stable grip, wrap your fingers around the handles with the handles pressed into where the bases of your fingers meet the palm side of your knuckles.
4. While holding the bar, sit down on the machine’s seat with the lower portion of your thighs snugly pressed against the machine’s thigh pad.
   1. When sitting down, allow your elbows to straighten and your arms to raise overhead in line with the machine’s resistance.
5. In the seated position, slightly lean your torso backward at your hips.
   1. Maintain this slight backward lean throughout your set.
   2. From this position, your elbows should be straight with your arms situated overhead and slightly forward (rather than being completely vertical), and you should feel tension from the cable station holding your arms in this position.

## Exercise technique

1. Pull the bar down toward your upper chest by driving your elbows down toward your sides as you progressively bend them.
   1. Simultaneously pull your shoulder blades down away from your ears.
   2. When pulling through your full range of motion, you should finish with the bar touching your upper chest or very close to doing so.
   3. Maintain the slight backward lean of your torso you adopted while setting up, rather than leaning further backward.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your elbows to lift upward and away from your torso as they progressively straighten to raise the bar in a controlled manner.
   1. Let your shoulder blades move naturally as they elevate with your arms.
   2. Finish with your arms overhead and your elbows fully straightened.
   3. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the machine’s resistance yank your arms up in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your lat and teres major while also meaningfully training your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
