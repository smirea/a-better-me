## Setting up

1. At a dual cable station, adjust each cable to a height approximately level with your shoulders or slightly above them, remove any handle attachment if it is clipped onto the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pins.
2. After standing centered between the cables with your sides facing the cable stacks, take a step backward, raise your arms in front of you until they are approximately parallel with the ground, and side step toward one cable.
3. Reach across your body with your arm that is opposite to the cable to grasp the ball at the end of the cable with an overhand grip as your palm is facing down, before side-stepping toward the other cable and grasping its ball with your free hand.
   1. Alternatively, you have the option to grasp the end of the cable directly with your little finger and the adjacent side of your hand contacting the ball.
   2. Use whichever hand placement feels most comfortable and stable to you, but if you decide to grasp the cable directly, be aware to avoid pinching any fingers when returning the cable to its initial position after finishing your set.
4. Return to being centered between the cables with your arms crossed in front of your torso, with slightly bent elbows, as the cable stacks are situated a little in front of you.
   1. In this position, your arms should resemble an “X-shape” with each other.
   2. Maintain slightly bent elbows throughout your set.

## Exercise technique

1. Pull your arms out and away from each other as you move your arms backward until they form a “T-shape” with your torso.
2. Reverse the motion by bringing your arms inward and toward each other in a controlled manner until your arms have crossed and returned to their starting position.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your rear delt, which is assisted by your traps and rhomboids, along with your rotator cuff’s infraspinatus, teres minor, and supraspinatus.
