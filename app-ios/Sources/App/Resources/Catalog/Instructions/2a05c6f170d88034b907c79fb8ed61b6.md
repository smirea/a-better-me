## Setting up

1. Bring a head harness and an elastic band to a flat exercise bench or any object on which you can comfortably sit.
   1. Either a tube elastic band or a long looped elastic band can be used for this exercise.
   2. You can alternatively perform this exercise standing, but this page will describe the seated position.
2. After optionally donning a beanie for comfort, place a head harness on top of your head, and adjust its straps as needed until it feels snug and secure.
   1. The appropriate fit should ensure that the harness does not slide when nodding your head up and down.
3. While sitting down, connect one end of the band to one of the harness's side attachment points before connecting the other end of the band to the other side attachment point.
4. Drape the band behind your body, and grasp the bottom of the band with your hands to tension the band with straight arms.

## Exercise technique

1. Slowly nod your head down as you tilt your chin toward your chest through your neck’s comfortable range of motion.
   1. People can commonly perform this movement until the chin is very close to touching the collarbone, but your neck mobility will also influence the degree to which you can nod down.
   2. It is natural to feel a stretching sensation in some of the muscles on the back of your neck in the bottom position, but you should not experience any neck discomfort.
   3. Prioritize actively controlling the weight throughout the entirety of your set. This is an intentionally slow exercise.
   4. While you move your head through neck motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary as you continue holding the band throughout the set.
2. Reverse the movement by nodding your head up in a controlled manner as you tilt your chin away from your chest through your neck’s comfortable range of motion.
   1. Finishing with the face angled diagonally forward and upward is generally an appropriate end position for many people, but the specific end point will vary among different individuals.
   2. What chiefly matters is finishing in a position that feels comfortable to your neck.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
   1. After completing your last rep, lift the band up with your hands by bending your elbows until the band becomes slack and you can remove the harness.

## Description

This exercise can effectively train several muscles on the front of your neck, the largest and strongest of which is your sternocleidomastoid.
