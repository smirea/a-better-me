## Setting up
1. If using an adjustable bench, position it at a moderate incline angle, which is near 45 degrees.
    1. The available incline angles will vary among different benches, with some benches having several angle configurations, while others may only be set to flat, upright, or a 45-degree angle.
    2. You can also use a fixed moderate incline bench for this exercise.
2. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on the incline bench with your feet flat on the floor while holding the dumbbells at your sides with extended elbows.
    1. Keep your upper back and the back of your head in contact with the bench throughout the set.
3. Direct your palms so that they are facing in toward your sides (i.e., in a neutral grip position).
    1. Maintain this neutral grip on the dumbbells throughout the set.
## Exercise technique
1. Curl both dumbbells upward by bending at your elbows while keeping your upper arms fixed at your sides (rather than allowing your upper arms to swing forward).
2. After your elbows have fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner until your elbows have returned to an extended position.
    1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arms positioned by your sides.
3. Alternate between curling the dumbbells up and lowering them down until you have completed your set.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
