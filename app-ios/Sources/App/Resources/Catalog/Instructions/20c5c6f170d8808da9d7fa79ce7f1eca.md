## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and stand upright with approximately a shoulder-width stance while holding the dumbbells at your sides with straight elbows as your palms face in toward your thighs.
2. While holding the dumbbells by your legs, hinge at your hips by sitting your buttocks back, and mildly bend your knees to position your torso at an angle fairly close to horizontal.
    1. Keep your lower back flat as you hinge at your hips, rather than just rounding your back.
    2. As a cue for hinging at your hips, you can think about closing a car door with your buttocks while your hands are carrying something.
    3. Maintain this bent-over position throughout your set.
3. Drive your elbows backward and upward in an arcing motion until your upper arms are close to parallel to the ground while allowing your elbows to bend and keeping your forearms directed down toward the ground.
    1. After performing this rowing motion, your elbows should be bent by approximately 90 degrees, placing you into the starting position for this exercise.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your upper arms parallel to the ground, progressively straighten your elbows to drive the dumbbells backward and upward in an arcing motion until you have fully straightened your elbows.
    1. As you move your elbows, you should keep the rest of your body stationary.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your elbows to bend in a controlled manner as you bring the dumbbells downward and forward in an arcing fashion until you have returned to the starting position with your forearms directed straight down toward the ground.
    1. Continue keeping your upper arms stationary as they remain parallel to the ground.
    2. At the end of the lowering phase, your elbows should be bent by approximately 90 degrees.
    3. There is no need to bend your elbows beyond 90 degrees (when your forearms are perpendicular to the ground) since that additional bending motion would be training your biceps rather than your triceps.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to set the dumbbells down.
## Description
This exercise trains all three heads of your triceps, but other exercises can likely train the triceps more effectively.
