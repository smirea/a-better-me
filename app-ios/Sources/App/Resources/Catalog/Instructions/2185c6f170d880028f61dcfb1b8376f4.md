## Setting up
1. Add your desired weight to an adjustable plate-loaded EZ bar on top of the preacher curl bench, or place a fixed weight EZ bar on top of the preacher curl bench.
2. Adjust the seat to a height that allows you to rest your elbows and most of the back of your upper arms (i.e., triceps) on the sloped arm pad when sitting on the seat.
    1. The proper setting will typically result in the top of the pad being close to your armpits.
    2. Some trial and error may be needed to find the ideal seat height.
3. Sit down on the seat, and position the back of your upper arms and elbows on top of the arm pad with your feet on the ground.
4. Firmly grasp the bar with a wider than shoulder width grip as your palms are directed at an angle that is facing forward and inward, resulting in a semi-underhand grip (AKA semi-neutral grip).
    1. With the zig-zag-like shape of the EZ bar, you may have to rotate it initially before a wider than shoulder width semi-underhand grip is accessible on the bar.
    2. If the bar is a little out of your reach, you could slightly stand up and lean forward to reach the bar before sitting back down after you have grasped the bar.
    3. Your wrists should be mostly straight throughout your set after grasping the bar.
5. In the starting position, your elbows should be fully straightened with the bar no longer in contact with the bench so that you can train through a full range of motion.
    1. If this is not the case, you can mildly scoot back with an upright torso until you have reached this starting position.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your elbows and the back of your upper arms in contact with the bench, curl the bar upward and back toward you in an arcing motion by bending at your elbows until they are fully bent.
    1. While your forearms move through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
    2. If your preacher bench allows you to curl the bar past the point when your forearms are completely vertical and perpendicular to the ground, you can finish the lifting phase after your forearms are vertical since the biceps would no longer be working beyond this point.
2. After your elbows have fully bent, reverse the motion by lowering the bar in a slow and controlled manner until your elbows have returned to a fully straightened position.
    1. Continue keeping your elbows and the back of your upper arms in contact with the bench.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to set the bar back down onto the preacher bench.
## Description
Like other curls, this exercise can effectively train the biceps and brachialis in your upper arm along with the brachioradialis in your forearm.
