## Setting up

1. While the barbell is on the ground or in an elevated position, such as a power rack, prepare the barbell with your desired load by adding weight plates to it or by leaving the barbell unloaded.
   1. Setting up will be easier when using an elevated position, especially if you are using relatively heavy loads for this exercise.
   2. The bar will be easiest to pick up if positioned at a height slightly lower than the level of your hands when standing upright with your arms relaxed at your sides.
   3. If you have access to one, you can also use a fixed-weight straight barbell, which may be lighter than a typical 20kg (44lb) barbell that has sleeves on which to add weight plates.
2. With your backside facing the bar, stand close in front of the middle of the bar with a hip-width stance and straight elbows before hinging at your hips while bending your knees until your hands are level with the bar.
   1. Keep your lower back near a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright, rather than meaningfully rounding your lower back while doing so.
3. Firmly grasp the bar with a shoulder-width overhand grip such that your palms are facing backward.
4. By extending your hips and knees, stand upright with the bar while continuing to keep your elbows straight and your lower back near a neutral position.
   1. Once upright, the bar should be directly behind your thighs.
   2. Maintain this upright position with straight elbows throughout your set.

## Exercise technique

1. Lift the bar upward by curling your wrists through their available range of motion while keeping your arms stationary.
   1. All of the motion should be coming from your wrists, and the bar will move slightly farther behind your thighs as it simultaneously moves upward.
   2. Your palms should be angled upward once you have fully curled your wrists.
   3. When performing this exercise appropriately, you should feel tension in the muscles on the palm side of your forearms.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the bar in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with your palms directed straight backward.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively train the six wrist flexor muscles on the palm side of your forearm.
