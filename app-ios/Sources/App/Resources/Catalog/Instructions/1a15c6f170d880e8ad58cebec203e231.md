## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to near waist height, connect a rope attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. Grasp the ends of the rope with a neutral grip such that your palms are facing in toward each other.
    1. Your little fingers and the adjacent aspects of your hands should be in contact with the flat surfaces of the attachment’s rubber stoppers as your thumbs wrap over the outside of your other fingers.
3. Pivot your body away from the pulley while simultaneously repositioning your arms such that your upper arms are raised up high with your elbows close to being fully bent as the cable is applying a downward force to the rope behind your head.
    1. To get into position with a heavier load, you can lunge down into a half-kneeling position during this step before standing upright once the rope is behind your head.
    2. The arm movement performed during this step is akin to a controlled swinging-like motion.
    3. How high you can raise your upper arms is influenced by your mobility.
4. Take a step forward, and slightly lean your torso forward by hinging at your hips while standing in a staggered stance with one foot in front of the other.
    1. In this position, you should feel tension from the cable, so if that is not the case, you should step further forward until you feel tension from the cable.
    2. Maintain this position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your elbows high, press the rope upward by extending your elbows until they are straightened.
    1. At the end of this motion, the rope should be situated above your head.
    2. If the cable contacts the back of your head during this motion, you can tuck your chin closer to your chest for clearance.
2. Reverse the motion by bending your elbows through your available range of motion to lower the rope in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position.
    1. Strive to have your elbows as close to fully bent as you can by the time you have finished this motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop the triceps, including the long head, which is not trained particularly well by compound pressing exercises.
