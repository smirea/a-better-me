## Setting up

1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to the height of your chest when you are in a half-kneeling position, which should correspond to being reasonably near hip height when standing.
2. Connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your weight with the loading pin.
   1. As an alternative to the D-handle attachment, you also can perform a Pallof press with a rope attachment, but this page will describe the use of the D-handle.
3. Stand with the side of one shoulder facing the cable station, and grasp the handle with your closer hand.
4. Take a step to the side while keeping your arm (holding the handle) aligned with the cable.
5. Perform a controlled lunging motion into a half-kneeling position by stepping back with your leg closer to the cable and resting that side’s knee on the ground.
   1. In this position, your imaginary belt buckle should be facing about 90 degrees away from the cable station.
6. Turn your chest toward the cable station, and reach across your body with your free hand to firmly grasp the handle, interlocking your hands and facing your palms toward each other.
7. Pull the handle close to your chest with elbows bent and by your sides before turning your chest to face the same direction as your imaginary belt buckle.
   1. Rather than being bent to one side, twisted, rounded, or excessively arched, your torso should be in a symmetrical upright position aligned with your hips, like it would normally be when you are standing upright.
   2. In this starting position, you should be able to form close to a 90-degree angle between the cable and an imaginary laser beam shooting straight forward from the center of your chest. You should also be able to draw a straight line between your shoulders that is parallel to the cable.

## Exercise technique

1. Brace your core by tensing the front and sides of your abdominals outward like you would if you were bracing for someone to strike your stomach with a punch or forceful slap.
   1. Continue bracing in this manner throughout your set. This will help create full-body tension, and allow your body to function as a stable platform from which to press.
2. While keeping the center of your chest facing straight ahead, press the handle straight forward away from your chest.
   1. You should finish with your elbows fully straightened as your arms are outstretched in front of you, close to parallel to the ground.
   2. All the motion should occur from your arms moving at your shoulders and elbows, while the rest of your body remains completely stationary.
   3. Particularly avoid letting the cable's resistance rotate your torso. If your core muscles do not stabilize your trunk sufficiently, the imaginary laser beam shooting out from the center of your chest will start angling closer toward the cable station. The center of your chest should be facing the same direction in line with your imaginary belt buckle throughout your entire set.
3. Reverse the movement in a controlled manner by bringing the handle straight back toward your chest as you bend your elbows.
   1. You should now be back to the starting position with the handle directly in front of your chest as your upper arms are right by your sides.
   2. Continue keeping the center of your chest facing straight forward as your shoulders remain aligned when viewed from the side.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise, this time facing the opposite direction.

## Description

While also engaging your pec major, this exercise will statically train your obliques as they function to resist trunk rotation.
