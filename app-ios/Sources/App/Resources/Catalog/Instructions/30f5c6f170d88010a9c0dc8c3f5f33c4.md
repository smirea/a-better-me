## Setting up

1. Select your desired weight with the machine’s loading pin(s), and adjust the seat to a height that lets you rest your lower chest comfortably against the chest pad once you are seated.
   1. The proper seat height should let the exercise feel smooth and natural while ideally letting you experience resistance from the machine in the starting position with your elbows fully straightened.
   2. An excessively high seat can prevent the machine from providing tension in the starting position and restrict your range of motion.
   3. When using this machine for the first time, trying out different seat heights may be helpful to determine specifically which setting helps this exercise feel best to you based on your body proportions.
2. If your machine has a thigh pad, adjust it to a height that lets you position your thighs snugly against it while sitting down.
3. While facing the chest pad and standing above the seat, firmly grasp the handles with a grip that feels comfortable to you based on the design of your machine.
4. Sit down on the seat with your torso against the chest pad. Let your elbows straighten and your arms raise in line with the machine’s resistance.
   1. Set your feet on the foot bar or the floor, based on your preference and machine design.
   2. If you do not feel tension from the machine in this position with straight elbows, you may need to lower the seat height to experience resistance throughout the full range of motion.

## Exercise technique

1. While pulling your shoulder blades down and back, drive your elbows down and back to pull the handles toward you.
   1. Pull through your full range of motion, which will typically result in your elbows finishing slightly behind your torso.
   2. Strive to keep your forearms aligned with the angle in which you are pulling, which should naturally promote the appropriate degree of elbow bend.
   3. Keep your torso upright against the chest pad throughout your set rather than leaning backward during the row.
   4. As a cue for training your upper back muscles, you can think about squeezing your shoulder blades together at the end of the pull as if you were trying to pinch a pencil between them.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your elbows to move forward and upward while straightening in a controlled manner.
   1. Let your shoulder blades move fully forward as they elevate with your arms.
   2. Finish with your elbows fully straightened.
   3. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the machine’s resistance yank your arms up in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This row primarily trains your lat, teres major, rear delt, traps, and rhomboids, which are assisted by your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
