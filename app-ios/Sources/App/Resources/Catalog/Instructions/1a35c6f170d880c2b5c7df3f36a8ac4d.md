## Setting up
1. Lie down with your back on the ground near a sturdy object with an elevated surface that is no higher than the height of your upper thighs while standing.
    1. Objects like exercise flat benches, stackable aerobic steps, plyometric boxes, stairs, and beds may be used.
    2. The height of the object will affect the range of motion you can use for this exercise, and selecting an object that is near mid-thigh height for you may be preferable over shorter objects to train through a longer range of motion.
2. Lift up your legs to position the back of your heels on top of the elevated surface with your knees straight or slightly bent.
    1. If you are too close to keep your knees straight or slightly bent on top of the elevated surface, you can scoot backward until you can do so.
    2. In this position, you can rest the back of your head on the ground and leave your arms on the ground by your sides.
3. While leaving one foot on the elevated surface, lift the other foot off the elevated surface by marching that side’s thigh up while bending your knee, or by lifting that leg a little above the elevated surface while keeping your knee straight.
    1. Either position for the free leg is viable, so you can go with whichever you prefer, but keeping the knee straight may make the exercise mildly more challenging than keeping it bent.
    2. Keep one foot off the elevated surface in the static position of your choice throughout the set you perform with your other side.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift your hips up off the ground by driving your heel into the elevated surface until your hip is fully extended.
    1. At the end of the lifting phase, you should be able to draw a straight line from your thighs through your hips to your torso.
    2. Focus on moving your hips and avoid arching your lower back excessively.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering your hips in a controlled manner until you are able to lightly contact the ground with your buttocks.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
This exercise will primarily train your gluteus maximus and three of your four hamstring muscles.
