## Setting up

1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to its lowest height, connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your weight with the loading pin.
2. In a mildly outside shoulder width stance, stand with the handle between your feet as your back is facing the cable station.
3. Hinge at your hips or squat down to firmly grasp the handle with the side of your body you will train during this set.
   1. Hold the handle with your palm facing forward and your elbow straight.
4. Take a step or two forward until the weight stack separates and you feel tension from the cable.
   1. You can keep your torso somewhat inclined forward while stepping forward.
5. On the same side of the body in which you are holding the cable, angle your knee and foot out to the side while situating your feet fairly wide apart.
6. Sit your hips back while allowing your knees to bend and torso to incline further forward until you can rest your working side’s elbow against the inside of your thigh. Simultaneously, brace your free arm’s forearm against your other side’s thigh for additional stability.
   1. Your working side’s arm should be directed down toward the ground with a straight elbow as the cable is exerting a pulling force backward.
   2. With the appropriate set-up, you should feel balanced in the starting position.

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping your elbow pinned against your inner thigh, bend your elbow to curl the handle forward and upward in an arcing manner.
   1. You can finish curling once your forearm is approximately parallel to the ground. Resistance from the cable will be minimal when bending your elbow beyond that point.
   2. While you move your forearm through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain still throughout your set.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your elbow to straighten in a controlled manner as you let your forearm arc downward and back toward the pulley.
   1. Finish this step with your elbow fully straightened.
   2. While you do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, avoid letting the cable abruptly yank the handle back in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
