## Setting up

1. With a cable station positioned to its lowest height, clip in a d-handle attachment and select your weight with the loading pin.
2. While facing the cable station, take a step to the side such that the handle is positioned directly in front of one hand (rather than having the midline of your body in front of the handle)
3. Squat down or hinge at your hips with an extended elbow before using your closer hand to grasp the handle with an underhand grip such that your palm is directed forward.
4. While keeping your elbow extended, stand upright while gripping the handle.
5. Make sure tension is present from the cable station while your elbow is fully extended, rather than slack being present in the cable.
   1. Depending on your arm length relative to your height and which specific cable station you are using, you likely will have to take a small step backward to take out the slack of the cable.
   2. When taking a small step backwards, the cable will now have a mildly diagonal line of pull rather than being vertical.
   3. To maintain a fully extended elbow position with a diagonal line of pull, you will position your upper arm slightly in front of your body by flexing at your shoulder instead of keeping your upper arm pinned to your sides (like you would with a curl that has a vertically directed resistance, such as free weights).

## Exercise technique

1. Curl the handle upward by bending at your elbow while keeping your upper arm fixed in the same position that allowed your elbow to be fully extended at the start of the rep (rather than allowing your upper arm to swing further forward).
2. After your elbow has fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the handle in a controlled manner until your elbow has returned to an extended position.
   1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arm in the same position used to start the rep.
3. Alternate between curling the handle upwards and lowering it down until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
