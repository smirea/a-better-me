## Setting up

1. Connect a lat pulldown bar to the cable, select your weight with the loading pin, and adjust the thigh pad to a height that lets you position your thighs snugly against it while sitting with your feet flat on the floor.
   1. While a standard lat pulldown bar attachment is most commonly used, you can perform this exercise with essentially any attachment that you can comfortably hold throughout the set, so it is up to you which specific attachment you use.
2. While facing the bar and standing above the seat, firmly grasp the bar with a slightly outside shoulder-width overhand grip, such that your palms face forward.
   1. For the most stable grip, wrap your fingers around the bar with the bar pressed into where the bases of your fingers meet the palm side of your knuckles.
3. While holding the bar, sit down on the seat with an upright torso and the lower portion of your thighs snugly pressed against the thigh pad.
   1. When sitting down, allow your elbows to straighten as your arms and shoulder blades are pulled upward in line with the machine’s resistance.
   2. In this starting position, you should feel tension from the cable. If you notice the cable remains slack (which may occur for certain individuals who are quite tall and have long arms), widening out your grip may be needed to experience resistance throughout the full range of motion for this exercise.

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping your elbows straight and your torso upright, fully pull your shoulders down away from your ears
   1. You can think about packing your armpits down into your pockets as a technique cue.
   2. While the range of motion for this exercise may vary among different individuals, this exercise involves a low amplitude of total movement.
2. Reverse the motion in a controlled manner by allowing the cable’s resistance to pull your shoulders upward into a fully shrugged position.
   1. Continue keeping your elbows straight.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise will train your lats, lower traps, and pec minor.
