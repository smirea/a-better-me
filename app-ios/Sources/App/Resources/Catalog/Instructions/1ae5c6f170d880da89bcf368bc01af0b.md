## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on a bench with your feet flat on the floor while holding the dumbbells at your sides with slightly bent elbows as your palms are facing back behind you (i.e., in an overhand grip position).
    1. You can opt to sit down on a flat bench without any back support or sit down on a seated bench with back support.
2. Assume a bent-over torso position by flexing forward at your hips and back until your torso is close to parallel with the ground.
    1. How far you are able to bend over will be affected by your mobility.
    2. If your mobility permits, you can bend over until your torso is parallel with the ground, but you can still effectively perform this exercise if you are not able to descend as far.
    3. While bent over, your arms should be approximately perpendicular to the ground with the dumbbells situated in front of your knees.
    4. Maintain this bent-over position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Raise the dumbbells away from each other and straight out to your sides until your arms and torso form a “T-shape.”
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells toward each other in a controlled manner back until they have returned to their starting position in front of your knees.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your rear delt, which is assisted by your traps and rhomboids, along with your rotator cuff’s infraspinatus, teres minor, and supraspinatus.
