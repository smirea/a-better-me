## Setting up

1. Connect a lat pulldown bar to a seated cable row machine, and select your weight with the loading pin.
   1. While a lat pulldown bar is typically used, you can perform this exercise with any attachment you can comfortably hold throughout the set, so it is up to you which specific attachment you use.
   2. If your machine lets you adjust the seat, you can set it to a height that positions the pulley approximately level with your upper chest once you are seated with an upright torso. This height will keep your arms close to parallel to the ground during the exercise. The exercise can still be very effective with a different seat height, so it is not problematic if your machine does not enable this position.
2. Sit down on the seat and place your feet in contact with the foot plates, as your knees are bent enough to position the bar within your reach after leaning forward.
3. Lean forward at your hips and firmly grasp the bar with a slightly outside shoulder-width overhand grip, resulting in your palms facing down.
4. Assume an upright torso position while pressing your feet into the foot plates to scoot your hips back until you are in a position where you feel stable and comfortable.
   1. People commonly find that a mildly bent knee position feels most comfortable.

## Exercise technique

1. Without bending your elbows or leaning your torso back, pull your shoulder blades backward and in toward each other as far as you can.
   1. As a cue, you can imagine pinching a pencil between your shoulder blades, which should prompt you to feel your upper back muscles contracting.
   2. In contrast to a row, the bar will only move a short distance during this variation, since you are isolating motion of your shoulder blades without moving your arms directly.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your shoulder blades to move forward and spread apart from each other as far as they can in a controlled manner.
   1. Avoid bending your elbows, leaning forward at your hips, or rounding your lower back.
   2. At the end of this motion, your shoulder girdles should resemble a slouched posture as they are pulled forward beyond their normal resting position, which often produces a stretching sensation in your upper back muscles.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise effectively trains your rhomboids and all three regions of your trapezius in a more isolated manner than row variations do.
