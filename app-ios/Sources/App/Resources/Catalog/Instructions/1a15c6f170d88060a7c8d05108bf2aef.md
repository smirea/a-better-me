## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to its highest level, select your desired weight with the loading pin, and attach a bar with a straight (or mostly straight) body to the cable.
    1. Any bar that enables you to perform this exercise with an overhand grip can be similarly used, including a standard straight bar, a lat pulldown bar (with a long straight body and angled ends), and a multi-grip EZ bar.
2. While standing directly in front of the cable station and facing away from it, reach up overhead with bent elbows and fairly straight wrists to grasp the bar with an overhand grip such that your palms are facing forward.
    1. Maintain fairly straight wrists throughout your set.
3. While keeping your hands overhead and elbows bent, step forward and adopt a staggered stance with one foot forward in front of the other before moderately leaning forward by hinging at your hips.
    1. Maintain this forward torso lean throughout your set.
    2. In this position, your hands should be behind your head, and you should feel tension from the cable. If that is not the case, walk forward until you feel tension from the cable.
4. Tuck your chin toward your chest and make sure your upper arms are situated parallel to the ground or raised up a little higher.
    1. Doing so will reduce the likelihood that the back of your head obstructs the path of the cable during the exercise.
## Exercise technique
1. Press the bar forward and somewhat downward by extending your elbows until they are straightened, while not moving your upper arms.
    1. At the end of this motion, the bar should be situated in front of you.
    2. If the cable contacts the back of your head during this motion, you can lean forward a little more for additional clearance.
2. Reverse the motion by bending your elbows through your available range of motion to move the bar backward and somewhat upward in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position.
    1. Strive to continue keeping your upper arms stationary while all of the movement occurs from elbow motion rather than also involving shoulder motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop all three heads of your triceps, including its large long head, which may not be trained as well by compound pressing exercises.
