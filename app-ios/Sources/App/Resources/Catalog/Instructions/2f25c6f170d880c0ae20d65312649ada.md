## Setting up

1. Adjust the machine’s seat to a height that allows you to rest your elbows and the backside of your upper arms on the arm pad when sitting on the seat.
   1. The proper setting will typically result in the top of the pad being close to your armpits.
   2. Avoid setting the seat too low to the point where you have to shrug your shoulders up.
   3. Some trial and error may be needed to find the ideal seat height.
2. After selecting your desired weight with the machine’s loading pin, sit down on the seat with your feet on the ground.
   1. If your machine lets you adjust its handle attachment, you can connect a straight bar attachment or an angled EZ-bar attachment, based on your preference.
3. Position the back of your upper arms and elbows on top of the arm pad with your armpits just behind the pad.
4. Firmly grasp the handles with an approximately shoulder-width grip as your palms face upward.
   1. If the handles are a little out of your reach, you could slightly stand up and lean forward to reach the handles before sitting back down after you have grasped the handles.
   2. Your wrists should be mostly straight throughout your set after grasping the handles.
5. In the starting position, your elbows should be fully straightened to allow you to train through a full range of motion.

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping your elbows and triceps in contact with the arm pad, fully bend your elbows to curl the bar toward you.
   1. While your forearms move in an arc through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
2. Reverse the motion by letting the bar move forward in a controlled manner until your elbows have returned to a fully straightened position.
   1. Continue keeping your elbows and triceps in contact with the arm pad.
   2. There is no need to perform this step with an extremely slow tempo, but you should avoid letting the machine’s resistance abruptly yank your forearms forward.
   3. If you lose resistance from the machine when your elbows fully straighten, you can stop just before the point where this loss of tension would occur.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
