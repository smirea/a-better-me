## Setting up

1. Situate a weight plate or kettlebell of your desired load next to a flat exercise bench or any object on which you can comfortably sit.
   1. You can alternatively perform this exercise standing with your torso hinged forward, but this page will describe the seated position.
2. After optionally donning a beanie for comfort, place a head harness on top of your head, and adjust its straps as needed until it feels snug and secure.
   1. The appropriate fit should ensure that the harness does not slide when nodding your head up and down.
3. Connect one end of a chain to one of the harness's side attachment points while sitting down with your legs moderately spread apart.
4. After picking up your weight, slide the free end of the chain through the center hole of a weight plate or under the handle of a kettlebell before connecting that end of the chain to the other side attachment point of the harness.
5. Moderately to mildly lean forward at your hips, and lower the weight until the chain is tensed.
   1. The weight should now be hanging between your legs.

## Exercise technique

1. Slowly nod your head down as you tilt your chin toward your chest through your neck’s comfortable range of motion.
   1. People can commonly perform this movement until the chin is very close to touching the collarbone, but your neck mobility will also influence the degree to which you can nod down.
   2. It is natural to feel a stretching sensation in some of the muscles on the back of your neck in the bottom position, but you should not experience any neck discomfort.
   3. Prioritize actively controlling the weight throughout the entirety of your set. This is an intentionally slow exercise.
   4. While you move your head through neck motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary.
2. Reverse the movement by nodding your head up in a controlled manner as you tilt your chin away from your chest through your neck’s comfortable range of motion.
   1. Based on your preference and what feels best to you, you can finish nodding up once you have returned to a neutral position (like when you are normally looking straight ahead while standing upright), or you can extend a bit beyond that point.
   2. Regardless of which end range you opt to use, your face should be angled forward in the top position. There is no need to nod your head all the way up until you are looking up at the ceiling.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
   1. After completing your last rep, lift the weight up with your hand to unload the harness before unfastening the chain from one side of the harness and removing the chain from the weight.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop the musculature on the back of your neck, including the splenius muscles, semispinalis muscles, and cervical portions of the erector spinae and multifidus.
