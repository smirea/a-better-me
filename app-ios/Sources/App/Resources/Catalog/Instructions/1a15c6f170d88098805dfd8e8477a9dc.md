## Setting up
1. Securely attach your desired weight to your wrist roller.
    1. Depending on the model of your wrist roller, you may have to use weight plates (if your model uses a loading pin), or you may be able to use weight plates, kettlebells, or dumbbells (if your model uses a strap that you can loop around the weight).
    2. Leave enough of the rope initially uncoiled so that you can stand up with the roller without the weight lifting off the ground.
2. While the weight is on the ground, firmly grasp the roller’s handles with your palms facing back toward you (i.e., an overhand grip) and the uncoiled part of the rope on the side of the roller that is closer to you.
    1. If the uncoiled part of the rope is on the side of the roller farther away from you, you would be able to train wrist extension when using an overhand grip, but this is the wrist flexion variation.
3. Stand upright while grasping the roller and bend your elbows by approximately 90 degrees while keeping your upper arms tucked by your sides.
    1. Maintain this bent-elbow position throughout your set.
    2. Your palms should now be facing down toward the ground.
4. Take up the slack from the roller’s rope with alternating twisting motions of your wrists until you feel that the rope is taut from the weight’s resistance.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your elbows bent, bend one wrist to curl that side’s hand toward you as you extend your other wrist in the opposite direction by pointing that hand’s palm down toward the ground.
    1. You should grasp the handle firmly with the hand whose palm is curling toward you, as you grasp the handle loosely with the hand whose palm is pointing toward the ground.
    2. The hand whose palm is curling toward you is what rotates the roller to lift the weight up, while the other side’s wrist is repositioning in preparation to perform the curling motion.
2. Alternate the motions in step 1 with the opposite hands as you lift the weight up by cyclically repeating these twisting movements with your wrists until you have fully coiled the rope around the roller.
3. Lower the weight in a controlled manner by reversing the same motions you just performed until the weight lightly taps the ground or is just about to do so.
4. Repeatedly perform steps 1-3 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively train the six wrist flexor muscles on the palm side of your forearm.
