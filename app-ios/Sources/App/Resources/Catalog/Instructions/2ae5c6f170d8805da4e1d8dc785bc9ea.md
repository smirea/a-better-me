## Setting up
1. After selecting your weight using the machine’s loading pin, adjust the seat or thigh pad height to a level that allows you to position your thighs snugly against the thigh pad while sitting with your feet flat on the floor.
    1. Certain machine models have an adjustable seat, others have an adjustable thigh pad, and some have both an adjustable seat and an adjustable thigh pad.
2. While facing the thigh pads and standing above the seat, firmly grasp the handles with your palms facing in toward each other (i.e., a neutral grip).
    1. If your machine has rotating handles (rather than fixed parallel handles), you naturally can rotate your palms inward for this variation.
3. While holding the handles, sit down on the machine’s seat with the lower portion of your thighs snugly pressed against the machine’s thigh pad. Slightly lean your torso backward at your hips.
    1. When seated, allow your elbows to straighten in a controlled manner and your arms to raise overhead in line with the machine’s resistance.
    2. If you do not feel tension from the machine in this position (which may occur for individuals who are tall and have long arms), lowering the seat height may be necessary to achieve resistance throughout the full range of motion for this exercise.
## Exercise technique
1. Drive your elbows down toward your sides while progressively bending them to pull the handles downward.
    1. Pull through your full range of motion until you can no longer drive your elbows closer to your torso, which often results in the handles finishing below the height of your ears and slightly above the tops of your shoulders.
    2. Maintain the same slight backward lean of your torso that you adopted while setting up, rather than progressively leaning further backward.
    3. Allow your elbows to move naturally in a manner that feels comfortable with the path of your machine’s handles. With a machine whose handles spread apart as you pull them down, it is typically most comfortable to let your elbows flare outward from your sides during the initial portion of the pull. With a machine that has handles that remain closer together throughout the rep, it is typically most comfortable to keep your elbows tucked closer to shoulder width apart rather than meaningfully flaring them outward.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your elbows to lift upward and away from your torso as they progressively straighten in a controlled manner.
    1. You should finish with your arms positioned fully overhead with straight elbows.
    2. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the machine’s resistance swing your arms up in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can train your lat and teres major especially well while also effectively training your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
