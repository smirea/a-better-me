## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to its highest level, connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. While standing with the cable station positioned in front of one of your shoulders, reach up with your closer hand to grasp the handle with an overhand grip such that your palm is facing forward and away from you while keeping your wrist fairly straight.
    1. Keep your wrist in a fairly straight position throughout your set.
3. Take a step backward and slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips.
    1. Maintain this slight torso lean throughout your set.
4. Pull your upper arm down and back until it is positioned at your sides with your elbow being close to fully bent.
    1. Maintain this upper arm position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Push the handle downward by extending your elbow until it is straightened.
    1. At the end of this motion, the handle should be situated by your side.
2. Reverse the motion by bending your elbow through your available range of motion to raise the handle in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position.
    1. Strive to have your elbow as close to fully bent as you can by the time you have finished this motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop the triceps, including the long head, which is not trained particularly well by pressing exercises.
