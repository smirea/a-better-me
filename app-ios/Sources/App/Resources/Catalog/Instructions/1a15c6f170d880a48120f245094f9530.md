## Setting up
1. With a cable station positioned to its lowest height, select your weight with the loading pin, and connect a straight bar attachment.
2. Situate a flat exercise bench or chair a little in front of the cable station.
3. Squat down to pick up the bar attachment with an overhand grip where your palms are facing back toward you.
4. While holding the bar, sit down on a flat exercise bench or chair and position your forearms on top of your thighs with your palms facing down and your wrists situated slightly in front of your knees.
    1. To achieve this position, you will lean forward at your hips when seated, with your elbows bent.
5. Allow the force from the cable to bend your wrists and bring your hands downward such that your palms are directed at an angle down and back toward you.
    1. In this position, the bar should not be contacting your legs, so scoot your forearms forward if it is initially contacting your legs.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift the bar upward by extending your wrists through their available range of motion while keeping your forearms resting on your thighs.
    1. All motion should come from your wrists as your hands move upward.
    2. About halfway through the lifting phase, your palms should be facing straight down toward the floor when your hands are aligned with your forearms.
    3. At the end of the lifting phase, when the bar is fully raised, your palms should be angled forward.
    4. When performing this exercise appropriately, you should feel tension in the muscles on the top side of your forearms.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the bar in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with your palms directed at an angle down and back toward you.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively train the seven wrist extensor muscles on the side of your forearm opposite to your palm.
