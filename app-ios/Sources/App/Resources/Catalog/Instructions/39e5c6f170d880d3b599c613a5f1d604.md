## Setting up

1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to be near head height and select your desired load while leaving the cable without any handle attachment.
2. Reach up with the arm you will train, and firmly grasp the ball-shaped stopper at the end of the cable with a bent elbow while facing away from the base of the cable station.
   1. You can use your non-working side’s arm to help set your grip on the cable and assume the starting position.
3. Take a couple of steps forward, and adopt a staggered stance with one foot further in front than the other.
4. For balance, slightly bend your front knee and slightly lean your torso forward by hinging at your front leg’s hip.
5. If you did not already do so, hold your upper arm approximately parallel to the ground with your elbow bent to assume the starting position.
   1. Situate your non-working arm into your preferred position.

## Exercise technique

1. Drive your forearm forward in an arcing manner by straightening your elbow. Keep your elbow up as your upper arm remains essentially stationary throughout the set.
   1. Finish with your elbow fully straightened.
   2. Strive to have all the movement occurring from your forearm hinging at your elbow without any shoulder motion.
   3. You can think of your elbow as the center of an analog clock, while your forearm is the hand that rotates about this stationary center elbow.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your elbow to bend in a controlled manner as your forearm arcs back toward the pulley.
   1. Let your elbow bend through the full range of motion provided by the cable until your forearm is aligned with it.
   2. There is no need to perform this extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the cables abruptly yank your hands toward the pulley in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
   1. After your last rep, step backward and bring the cable end toward the pulley until the cable loses tension and you can release it.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop the triceps, including the long head, which is not trained particularly well by compound pressing exercises.
