## Setting up

1. Stand tall upright with an upright posture and your feet about hip width apart.
   1. Position your arms wherever you prefer throughout your set.

## Exercise technique

1. Take a deep breath, and then fully exhale until you feel that you have released all the air you can.
2. Without inhaling, suck your belly button in toward your spine with the intent to narrow your waist fully.
3. Statically hold this position for your target duration (e.g., 10-15 seconds) before inhaling and relaxing your abdomen.
   1. Regardless of whether you reached your target duration, you should resume breathing if you start feeling lightheaded when holding your stomach vacuum.
4. Alternate between steps 1-3 until you have completed your set.

## Description

While engaging your transversus abdominis, training stomach vacuums can help improve posing for bodybuilders.
