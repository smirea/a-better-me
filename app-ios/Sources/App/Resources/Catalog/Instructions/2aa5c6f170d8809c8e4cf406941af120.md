## Setting up

1. Position a cable station’s pulley near shoulder height or slightly higher, connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your weight with the loading pin.
2. Stand with the side of your body that you will not train during this set facing the cable station.
3. Reach across your body (with your hand further away from the pulley), and grasp the handle with your palm facing backward. To assist with getting into position, you can use your other hand to grasp the ball of the cable next to the pulley.
4. Take a step out to the side, and lunge backward into a half-kneeling position with your knee further away from the pulley resting on the ground.
   1. Keep your torso upright with the center of your chest and your imaginary belt buckle facing straight ahead in the same direction.
   2. Allow the cable’s resistance to keep your arm (that is holding the handle) pulled across your body.
   3. Your knee on the same side as the pulley should now be in front of you, while your other knee is under your torso.
5. While holding the handle with your working side’s hand, let go of the cable’s ball with your other hand. Brace that free hand against your lower thigh for additional stability.
   1. Continue bracing that hand against your thigh throughout the exercise to help avoid twisting your chest toward the pulley.
   2. All the subsequent instructions will refer to the arm whose hand is holding the cable.

## Exercise technique

1. Brace your core by tensing the front and sides of your abdominals outward, like you would if you were bracing for someone to strike your stomach with a punch or forcible slap.
   1. Continue bracing your core throughout the set.
2. Drive your elbow diagonally outward and down toward your side while keeping your forearm aligned with the cable and palm facing backward.
   1. You should finish with your elbow bent by your side, as the handle is in front of your torso.
   2. Continue keeping the center of your chest and imaginary belt buckle pointing straight ahead throughout the set, as you avoid twisting your chest to either side.
3. Reverse the movement in a controlled manner by allowing the cable to pull your elbow diagonally inward and upward across your body.
   1. While letting your elbow straighten, strive to move through your shoulder’s full range of motion with your arm fully across your body, angled toward the pulley.
   2. At the end of this step, it is common to feel a stretching sensation in your back muscles with your elbow in front of your breastbone or slightly further across your body.
   3. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the cable yank your arm across your body in an uncontrolled fashion.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side while facing the opposite direction.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop train your lat, teres major, and rear delt while also training your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
