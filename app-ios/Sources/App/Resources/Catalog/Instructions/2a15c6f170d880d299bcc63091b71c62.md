## Setting up
1. Lie down with your back and head on a flat bench, with your legs straight and feet together.
    1. As an alternative to a bench, you can lie down on the ground with something sturdy (like a couch or bed) behind you that you can grasp for stability. If you select this option, you can optionally place an exercise mat under where you will lie for comfort.
2. Reach behind your head and grasp the bottom of the bench’s pad with both hands.
    1. If you opted to lie down on the ground, you can alternatively reach behind you and grasp whatever sturdy object you are positioned against.
    2. Continue holding onto the bench (or alternative object) for stability throughout the exercise.
## Exercise technique
1. Tighten your abdominal muscles to press your lower back down into the ground. Simultaneously lift your legs up off the ground until your thighs are approximately vertical while keeping your knees mostly straight.
    1. Strive to keep your knees as close to straight as your hamstring flexibility comfortably permits.
2. While keeping your legs directed straight up, lift your buttocks, lower back, and middle back off the floor.
    1. You should now be bearing your weight predominantly through your upper back.
3. In a controlled manner, allow your legs to arc forward and downward while keeping your buttocks and most of your back off the bench until your thighs are about to contact the bench.
    1. In contrast to the lifting phase of this exercise that you perform during steps 1 and 2, this lowering phase should be a continuous movement as you lower your legs back together. Correspondingly, your buttocks should only contact the bench when your legs are angled mostly forward rather than upward.
    2. At the bottom of this movement, your legs should be directed straight forward with your back contacting the bench.
    3. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should strive to control the descent to the best of your ability rather than just dropping down abruptly.
    4. As you descend further, controlling the movement will become progressively more challenging.
4. Alternate between steps 1-3 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise will primarily train your rectus abdominis, with assistance from your obliques and hip flexors.
