## Setting up

1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on a bench with an upright posture and your feet flat on the floor while holding the dumbbells at your sides with extended elbows.
   1. You can opt to sit down on a flat bench without any back support or sit down on a seated bench with back support.
2. Direct your palms so that they are facing forward.
   1. Maintain this underhand grip on the dumbbells throughout the set.

## Exercise technique

1. Curl both dumbbells upward by bending at your elbows while keeping your upper arms fixed at your sides (rather than allowing your upper arms to swing forward).
2. After your elbows have fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner until your elbows have returned to an extended position.
   1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arms positioned by your sides.
3. Alternate between curling the dumbbells up and lowering them down until you have completed your set.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
