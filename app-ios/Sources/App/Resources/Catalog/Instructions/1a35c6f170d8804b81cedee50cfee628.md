## Setting up
1. Lie down on your side with the side of your body that you are about to train facing up.
    1. In this side-lying position, you can rest the bottom side of your head on the hand of your bottom arm for comfort by situating your upper arm out to the side with your elbow bent.
    2. For extra stability, you can bend the elbow of your top arm and place that side’s hand on the ground in front of your torso.
2. Bend your knees by approximately 90 degrees and bring your thighs forward so they form near a 45-60-degree angle with your torso.
    1. Based on preference and what feels comfortable to you, you can position your thighs further forward so that they are closer to forming a right angle with your torso, or you can position them further backward so that they are closer to being aligned with your torso.
    2. In this position, the inner aspects of your knees and feet should be in contact with each other.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift your top leg’s knee upward away from your bottom knee while keeping the inner aspect of your feet in contact with each other and the rest of your body stationary.
    1. Move through your hip’s available range of motion, and avoid rolling your hips backward during this exercise, which is a common technical error.
    2. To help prevent your hips from rolling, you can imagine that you are wearing a belt with a buckle that has a laser beam shooting out of it. Strive to keep the laser beam pointing straight forward, parallel to the ground, throughout the movement.
    3. If you start to roll your hips backward, your belt buckle’s laser beam will start to angle upward, the exercise will feel easier, and you will be able to lift your knee higher up than if you had avoided rolling backward.
    4. While performing this movement with the appropriate technique, you will likely feel the muscles on the side of your top hip contracting.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your top knee to descend in a controlled manner until it is low enough to contact the inner aspect of your lower knee.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side by lying on your opposite side.
## Description
This exercise primarily trains the upper region of your gluteus maximus and the short hip external rotator muscles situated underneath your glutes.
