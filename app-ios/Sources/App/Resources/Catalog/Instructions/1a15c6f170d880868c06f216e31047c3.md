## Setting up

1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and stand upright with approximately a hip-width stance while holding the dumbbells at your sides with extended elbows.
2. Direct your palms so that they are facing in toward your sides (i.e., in a neutral grip position).
   1. Maintain this neutral grip on the dumbbells throughout the set.

## Exercise technique

1. Curl both dumbbells upward by fully bending your elbows while keeping your upper arms fixed at your sides (rather than allowing your upper arms to swing forward).
2. After your elbows have fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner until your elbows have returned to an extended position.
   1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arms positioned by your sides.
3. Alternate between curling the dumbbells up and lowering them down until you have completed your set.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
