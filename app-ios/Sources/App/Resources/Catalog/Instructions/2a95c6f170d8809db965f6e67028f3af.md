## Setting up

1. Slide a pair of fat grip attachments onto two dumbbells, and pick them up while firmly grasping them.
2. Sit down on a bench with your torso upright and feet flat on the floor while holding the dumbbells by your sides with straight elbows as your palms are facing in toward your sides (resulting in a neutral grip that is also called a hammer grip).
   1. You can opt to sit down on a seated bench with your back resting against the back support, or you can sit on a flat bench without any back support.

## Exercise technique

1. Curl the dumbbells upward in an arcing manner by bending your elbows while turning your palms to face up and then back toward you at the top.
   1. Keep your upper arms fixed by your sides rather than allowing them to swing forward through shoulder motion.
   2. While your forearms move through elbow joint motion and your palms rotate, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
   3. Maintain a tight grip on the dumbbells with straight wrists throughout the entirety of this exercise.
   4. At the top of the curl, your elbows should be fully bent with your forearms touching your biceps as you are holding the dumbbells with an underhand grip rather than the neutral grip you used to start.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner and turning your palms inward.
   1. You should finish with your elbows straightened and your palms facing in toward your sides.
   2. You do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, but you should avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the dumbbells down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

While dynamically training your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis, this exercise can statically train your forearm’s extrinsic finger flexors and your hand’s intrinsic finger flexors.
