## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to near waist height, connect  a V-bar attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. While facing the cable station, grasp the V-bar with your palms facing in toward each other.
3. Pivot your body 180 degrees while simultaneously repositioning your arms such that your upper arms are raised up high with your elbows close to being fully bent with fairly straight wrists as the cable is applying a downward force to the V-bar behind your head.
    1. The arm movement performed during this step is akin to a controlled swinging-like motion.
    2. How high you can raise your upper arms is influenced by your mobility.
4. Adopt a staggered stance by stepping forward with one foot, and slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips
    1. In this position, you should feel tension from the cable, so if that is not the case, you should step further forward until you feel tension from the cable.
    2. Maintain this position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your elbows high, press the V-bar upward by extending your elbows until they are straightened.
    1. At the end of this motion, the V-bar should be situated above your head.
    2. If the cable contacts the back of your head during this motion, you can tuck your chin closer to your chest for clearance.
2. Reverse the motion by bending your elbows through your available range of motion to lower the V-bar in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position.
    1. Strive to have your elbows as close to fully bent as you can by the time you have finished this motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop the triceps, including the long head, which is not trained particularly well by compound pressing exercises.
