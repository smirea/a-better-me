## Setting up
1. At a cable station, adjust the cable to its lowest height, clip in a D-handle attachment, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. While standing to the side of the cable, grasp the handle with your hand on the side opposite the cable, stand up, and take a step out to the side away from the cable station.
    1. Your palm should be approximately facing the cable station as your hand is situated just in front of your thigh.
3. Slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips, slightly bend your elbow, and move your arm mildly across the front of your body until your hand is positioned approximately in front of your opposite thigh.
    1. Maintain this slightly hinged position and slightly bent elbow throughout your set.
    2. With your arm in this diagonal position, you will likely feel a stretching sensation on the side of your shoulder.
    3. In this position, you should feel tension from the cable, so if that is not the case, take another step away from the cable stack. 
## Exercise technique
1. Raise the handle out and away from your side until your upper arm is close to parallel with the ground.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the handle in a controlled manner back to the starting position with your hand just in front of your opposite thigh.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your lateral delt and supraspinatus, which are assisted by your front delt and upper trap.
