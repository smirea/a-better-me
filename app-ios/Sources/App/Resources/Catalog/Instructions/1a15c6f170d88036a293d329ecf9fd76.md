## Setting up

1. Approach a surface (e.g., hand rail, wall, power rack) where you can place one hand to assist with maintaining your balance while performing this exercise.
2. Place your hand onto the nearby surface for balance assistance.
   1. Maintain this hand contact throughout the set.
3. On the side you are not about to train, lift the foot up off the ground by bending that knee.
   1. Keep this foot in this position off the ground throughout the set.

## Exercise technique

1. With the leg that is standing on the ground, raise your heel into the air through your ankle’s available range of motion while keeping that side’s knee straight and your trunk upright.
2. In a controlled manner, allow your heel to descend toward the ground while continuing to keep the knee straight on your working side.
   1. Gently tap the ground with your heel at the bottom.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat this exercise with your other side.

## Description

This exercise offers you a convenient way to train your gastrocnemius and soleus in nearly any environment.
