## Setting up

1. Adjust a cable to a height that is approximately level with your elbows when your arms are resting at your sides while standing.
2. Connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
3. While facing the cable station, grasp the handle with one hand and direct your palm downward as you raise your arm to be parallel with the cable.
4. With your elbow in an extended position, step backward until the machine’s weight plates separate and you feel tension from the cable
   1. In this position, your arm should be outstretched in front of your torso.

## Exercise technique

1. While bending your elbow, pull the handle backward and upward simultaneously until your upper arm is in line with your torso and approximately parallel with the ground while your palm is facing forward.
   1. While doing so, the cable should move in a diagonal motion that involves more horizontal motion than vertical motion.
   2. At the end of this motion, your elbow should be bent to near a 90-degree angle.
2. Reverse the motion by moving the handle forward and downward simultaneously as your elbow extends until you have returned to the starting position.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.

## Description

While also meaningfully training your rear delt, traps, and rhomboids, this exercise can effectively develop your rotator cuff’s infraspinatus and teres minor, along with the supraspinatus to a lesser degree.
