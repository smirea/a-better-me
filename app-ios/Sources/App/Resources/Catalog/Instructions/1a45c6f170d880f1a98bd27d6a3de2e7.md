## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable station to approximately chest height, connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your weight with the loading pin.
    1. As an alternative to the D-handle attachment, you also can perform a Pallof press with a rope attachment, but this page will describe use of the D-handle.
2. Stand with the side of one shoulder facing the cable station as the handle is just in front of your chest.
3. Firmly grasp the handle with your hands interlocked and palms facing each other. Your elbows should be bent with your upper arms by your sides and the handle in front of your chest.
    1. One palm should be facing in toward the cable as the other palm faces straight out to the side away from the cable.
4. While keeping the handle close to your chest, side-step away from the cable station until you feel tension from the cable.
    1. Prevent your torso from rotating when side-stepping.
5. Position your feet near shoulder-width apart, and slightly sit your buttocks backward to bend your hips and knees slightly.
    1. Rather than being excessively arched or rounded, your back and neck should be near a neutral position like when you are normally standing upright and looking straight ahead.
    2. In this starting position, you should be able to form a 90-degree angle between the cable and an imaginary laser beam shooting straight forward from the center of your chest. You should also be able to draw a straight line between your shoulders that is parallel to the cable.
    3. The purpose of the slight hip and knee bend is to help you feel stable and balanced during your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Brace your core by tensing the front and sides of your abdominals outward like you would if you were bracing for someone to strike your stomach with a punch or forceful slap.
    1. Continue bracing in this manner throughout your set. This will help create full body tension, and allow your body to function as a stable platform from which to press.
2. While keeping the center of your chest facing straight ahead, press the handle straight forward away from your chest.
    1. You should finish with your elbows fully straightened as your arms are outstretched in front of you close to parallel to the ground.
    2. All the motion should occur from your arms moving at your shoulders and elbows while the rest of your body remains completely stationary.
    3. Particularly avoid letting the resistance from the cable rotate your torso. If your core muscles do not stabilize your trunk sufficiently, the imaginary laser beam shooting out from the center of your chest will start angling closer toward the cable station. The center of your chest should be facing the same direction throughout your entire set.
3. Reverse the movement in a controlled manner by bringing the handle straight back toward your chest as you bend your elbows.
    1. You should now be back to the starting position with the handle directly in front of your chest as your upper arms are right by your sides.
    2. Continue keeping the center of your chest facing straight forward as your shoulders remain aligned when viewed from the side.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise while facing the opposite direction.
## Description
While also engaging your pec major, this exercise will statically train your obliques as they function to resist trunk rotation.
