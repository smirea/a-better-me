## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of kettlebells and stand upright with approximately a shoulder-width stance while holding the kettlebells with straight elbows.
2. Position your hands by the front of your outer thighs and direct your palms so that they are facing backward.
3. Slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips.
    1. Maintain the slight forward lean throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift the kettlebells upward by driving your elbows upward and out to your sides through a range of motion that feels comfortable to your shoulders while bending your elbows.
    1. Your mobility will affect how high you are comfortably able to lift the kettlebells, and there is no need to force your shoulders into a range that provokes any discomfort.
    2. While different individuals may be able to lift higher up and others not quite as high, people often can lift their upper arms until they are close to parallel to the ground for a comfortable and effective range of motion.
    3. The kettlebells should remain close to your torso throughout the exercise as they move vertically.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your elbows to descend in toward your sides as you lower the kettlebells in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with straight elbows.
    1. At the end of the lowering phase, the kettlebells should be situated in front of your thighs.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your lateral delt and supraspinatus, which are assisted by your front delt and upper trap.
