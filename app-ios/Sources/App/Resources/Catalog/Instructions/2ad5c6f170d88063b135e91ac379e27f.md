## Setting up

1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to its highest level, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. If you have access to one, connect a long rope attachment to the cable. Otherwise, connect two standard rope attachments to the cable, and pull each rope fully to one side of the attachment.
   1. Whatever type of equipment you select should be long enough that you can spread your hands about shoulder width apart at the bottom of the rep to perform this specific variation.
   2. In addition to the attachments that are just longer versions of the standard rope attachments, long rope attachments functionally include the long strap attachments (that can be used for cable crunches) that have a pair of looped ends into which you can place your hands.
   3. If you only have a single standard rope attachment, you can train your triceps just as effectively with the standard cable rope triceps pushdown.
3. Stand in front of the cable station facing the rope.
4. Reach up and grasp each end of the rope with your little fingers and adjacent aspects of your hands contacting the flat surfaces of the attachment’s rubber stoppers. Wrap your thumbs over the outside of your other fingers.
   1. If you are using a long strap attachment with looped ends rather than ropes, you naturally will situate your hands within the loops and grasp the inner part of each loop.
   2. Your palms should be facing inward.
5. Take a step backward and slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips.
   1. Maintain this slight torso lean throughout the set.
6. Pull your upper arms down and back until they are at your sides, with your elbows close to fully bent, so your hands are close together in front of your chest.
   1. In this starting position, you should feel tension from the cable. If you do not, take another step back until the weight stack separates.

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping your upper arms and elbows aligned with your torso, push the rope downward and spread it apart by straightening your elbows.
   1. Allow your elbows to flare slightly out away from your sides while spreading the rope apart, which can result in your hands finishing slightly out to the sides of your torso.
   2. At the end of this motion, your elbows should be fully straightened, and you can optionally emphasize squeezing your triceps for a moment if you wish to do so.
   3. Throughout the exercise, avoid swinging your elbows backward or forward through shoulder motion.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your elbows to bend in a controlled manner to raise the rope as your hands move closer together.
   1. Strive to have your elbows as close to fully bent as you can by the time you have finished this movement to train your triceps through a full range of motion.
   2. You do not have to bend your elbows extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the cable abruptly pull your forearms upward in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop all three heads of your triceps, which constitute the majority of muscle mass in your upper arms.
