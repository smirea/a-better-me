## Setting up
1. Lie down with your back on the ground, knees straight out in front of you, and your arms straight by your sides.
    1. For comfort, you optionally can lie down on an exercise mat.
2. Lift your arms slightly off the ground, pointing forward, and keep them pointing forward throughout the exercise.
## Exercise technique
1. As you tighten your abdominal muscles, curl your back to lift it off the ground while keeping the backs of your hip bones and legs on the ground.
    1. To train your abdominal muscles dynamically, your back should progressively round rather than remain flat (which could happen if you predominantly lift your torso through your hip flexors). With the appropriate focus on curling your back, you should feel meaningful tension in your abs rather than in your hip flexors on the front of your upper thighs.
    2. As a cue, you can think about driving the bottom of your ribs down toward the top of your hip bones, which is the action that contraction of your abdominal muscles can produce.
    3. You have reached the end of the lifting phase once you feel that you have fully curled your back and crunched the bottom of your ribs down toward the top of your hips.
    4. In contrast to a sit-up, your shoulders should remain clearly behind your hips at the top of this lift, since you should exclusively be moving through back motion without any hip motion.
    5. Based on what feels most comfortable to you, you can mildly tuck your chin toward your chest at the start of the lifting phase, or you can keep your neck near a neutral position like you would normally do when standing upright.
2. Reverse the movement by uncurling your back in a controlled manner as you lower your back to the ground.
    1. When transitioning from having a rounded back to a straighter back, your ribs should flare away from your hip bones.
    2. While you do not need to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, you should avoid dropping onto the ground in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your rectus abdominis, which is assisted by your obliques.
