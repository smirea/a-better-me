## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on a flat exercise bench or chair.
2. While holding the dumbbells, lean forward at your hips and position your forearms on top of your thighs with your palms facing down toward the floor and your wrists situated slightly in front of your knees.
3. Allow the force from the dumbbells to bend your wrists and bring your hands downward such that your palms are directed at an angle down and back toward you.
    1. In this position, the dumbbells should not be contacting your legs, so scoot your forearms forward if they are initially contacting your legs.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift the dumbbells upward by extending your wrists through their available range of motion while keeping your forearms resting on your thighs.
    1. All of the motion should be coming from your wrists as your hands move upward.
    2. About halfway through the lifting phase, your palms should be facing straight down toward the floor when your hands are aligned with your forearms.
    3. At the end of the lifting phase, when the dumbbells are fully raised, your palms should be angled forward.
    4. When performing this exercise appropriately, you should feel tension in the muscles on the top side of your forearms.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with your palms directed at an angle down and back toward you.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively train the seven wrist extensor muscles on the side of your forearm opposite your palm.
