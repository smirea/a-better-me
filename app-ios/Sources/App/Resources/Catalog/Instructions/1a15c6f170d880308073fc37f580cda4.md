## Setting up
1. Position an adjustable cable station’s pulley to its highest setting, or approach a cable lat pulldown machine.
    1. Either type of equipment can be used interchangeably for this exercise.
2. Select your desired weight with the loading pin, and connect a straight bar attachment to the cable.
    1. As an alternative to a standard straight bar or a lat pulldown bar (with a long straight body and angled ends), you can use a multi-grip EZ bar if you wish to do so.
3. While facing the cable station, reach up and firmly grasp the bar with an approximately shoulder-width overhand grip, such that your palms are angled forward and downward.
    1. Keep your elbows and wrists straight.
4. While keeping your arms outstretched with straight elbows in line with the cable, walk backward until you feel tension from the cable as the weight stack separates.
5. Sit your buttocks back to hinge at your hips and moderately incline your torso while keeping your arms in line with the cable and your back flat.
    1. The appropriate degree of torso incline should allow you to draw a mostly straight line from your arms through your shoulders to your hips when viewed from the side. From this position, the cable’s resistance should situate your arms fully overhead relative to what your shoulder mobility comfortably permits.
    2. As a cue for hinging at your hips, you can think about closing a car door with your buttocks while your hands are carrying something.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your elbows straightened and your torso inclined, sweep your arms down and back toward your sides in an arcing motion until your hands are right by your thighs.
    1. During the initial half of this movement, you can focus on driving down, while you can focus on pulling back during the latter half of this movement.
    2. As your arms move through motion at your shoulders, the rest of your body should be stationary.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your arms to sweep forward and upward in a controlled arcing manner until you have returned to the starting position with your arms fully overhead in line with the cable.
    1. Continue keeping your elbows straightened and your torso inclined.
    2. While you do not have to reverse the movement with an extremely slow tempo, you should avoid letting the cable abruptly swing your arms upward in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
    1. After your last rep, you can stand upright and walk forward to return the cable to its initial position.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your lats, teres major, and rear delt, which are assisted by the lowest portion of your pec major and the long head of your triceps in the overhead position of each rep.
