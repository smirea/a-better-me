## Setting up

1. With a cable station positioned to its lowest height, select your weight with the loading pin, and connect a straight bar attachment.
2. Situate a flat exercise bench or chair a little in front of the cable station.
3. Squat down to pick up the bar attachment with an underhand grip where your palms are facing forward away from you.
4. While holding the bar, sit down on a flat exercise bench or chair and position your forearms on top of your thighs with your palms facing up and your wrists situated slightly in front of your knees.
   1. To achieve this position, you will lean forward at your hips when seated, with your elbows bent.
5. Allow the force from the cable to bend your wrists and bring your hands downward such that your palms are facing forward.
   1. In this position, the bar should not be contacting your legs, so scoot your forearms forward if it is initially contacting your legs.

## Exercise technique

1. Lift the bar upward by curling your wrists through their available range of motion while keeping your forearms resting on your thighs.
   1. All motion should come from your wrists.
   2. About halfway through the lifting phase, your palms should be facing straight up when your hands are aligned with your forearms.
   3. At the end of the lifting phase, when the bar is fully raised, your palms should be angled back toward you.
   4. When performing this exercise appropriately, you should feel tension in the muscles on the palm side of your forearms.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the bar in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with your palms directed forward away from you.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively train the six wrist flexor muscles on the palm side of your forearm.
