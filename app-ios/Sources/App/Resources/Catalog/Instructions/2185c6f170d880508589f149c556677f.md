## Setting up
1. Adjust the machine’s seat to a height that allows you to rest your elbows and most of the back of your upper arms (i.e., triceps) on the sloped arm pad when sitting on the seat.
    1. The proper setting will typically result in the top of the pad being close to your armpits.
    2. Some trial and error may be needed to find the ideal seat height.
2. After adding weight plates to the machine, sit down on the seat with your feet on the ground.
3. Position the back of your upper arms and elbows on top of the arm pad as your elbows are aligned with the machine’s pivot point, which is the center about which the machine’s lever arm rotates that is denoted by a small dot or circle in some machines.
4. Firmly grasp the handles with your hands approximately shoulder width apart from each other as your palms are angled upward.
    1. If the handles are a little out of your reach, you could slightly stand up and lean forward to reach the handles before sitting back down after you have grasped the handles.
    2. Your wrists should be mostly straight throughout your set after grasping the handles.
    3. Machines commonly have angled handles that allow you to grasp them with your palms angled inward and upward (resulting in a hybrid between an underhand and neutral grip), but certain models may have horizontal handles that will allow you to use a purely underhand grip.
5. In the starting position, your elbows should be fully straightened to allow you to train through a full range of motion.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your elbows and the back of your upper arms in contact with the arm pad, curl the handles upward and back toward you in an arcing motion by bending at your elbows until they are fully bent.
    1. While your forearms move through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
2. After your elbows have fully bent, reverse the motion by lowering the handles in a slow and controlled manner until your elbows have returned to a fully straightened position.
    1. Continue keeping your elbows and the back of your upper arms in contact with the arm pad.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
