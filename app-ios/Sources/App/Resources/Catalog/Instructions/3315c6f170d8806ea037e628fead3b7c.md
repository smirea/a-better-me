## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of kettlebells and stand upright in a near hip-width stance while holding the kettlebells at your sides with your palms facing in toward your thighs.
2. Slightly bend your elbows and slightly lean forward at your hips.
    1. While you do not need to have an exaggerated “proud chest” posture with your shoulders fully pulled back, avoid standing with your shoulders rolled forward into a slouched upper back position.
## Exercise technique
1. Sweep your arms out and away from your sides until they are approximately parallel to the ground.
    1. While you raise your arms through shoulder motion and allow your shoulder blades to move naturally, keep the rest of your body stationary as you maintain a slight elbow bend and slight forward lean throughout your set.
    2. Let the handles rotate in your hands to keep the base of each kettlebell facing the ground.
    3. At the top of the raise, your palms should be directed down toward the ground with mostly straight wrists.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering your arms in toward your sides in a controlled arcing manner until the kettlebells are directly next to your legs.
    1. While you do not need to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, avoid letting the weights abruptly yank your arms downward.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your lateral delt and supraspinatus while also training your front delt and upper trap.
