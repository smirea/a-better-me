## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to its lowest height, connect a D-handle attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. Stand with the side of your shoulder (that you will not train during this set) facing the cable.
3. Squat down, and reach across your body to grasp the handle with your arm on the side opposite the cable.
4. After standing upright, use your free hand to grasp a stable part of the cable station.
    1. Throughout the exercise, continue using this arm to help stabilize yourself.
    2. All the subsequent directions will apply to the arm holding the handle.
5. Take a step forward with your foot that is further away from the pulley. Allow the cable’s resistance to pull your arm across your body and in toward the pulley with your elbow straightened.
    1. You should feel tension from the cable and will likely feel a stretching sensation on the side of your shoulder.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your elbow straight, sweep your arm in a smooth arcing motion diagonally upward and outward away from the pulley.
    1. After sweeping your arm as if you were drawing a sword, you should finish with your arm angled out to the side and mildly upward.
    2. In contrast to a typical lateral raise, your arm should initially arc forward in front of you as it moves across your body. This will result in the movement functioning like a hybrid between a lateral raise and a front raise, with it involving a greater proportion of lateral raise motion than front raise motion.
    3. While you raise your arm through shoulder motion and allow your shoulder blade to move naturally, the rest of your body should remain stationary. This includes keeping your chest and imaginary belt buckle directed straight ahead throughout your set rather than rotating either one to the side.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering your arm in toward the pulley and across your body in a controlled arcing manner through your full range of motion.
    1. While you do not need to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, avoid letting the cable abruptly yank your arm downward.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop your lateral delt, front delt, and supraspinatus, which are assisted by your upper trap.
