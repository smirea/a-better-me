## Setting up
1. With a cable station’s pulley positioned near hip height, connect a d-handle attachment, and select your weight with the loading pin.
2. Position yourself to one side of the cable station while facing away from it.
3. Squat down or hinge at your hips with an extended elbow before using your closer hand to grasp the handle with an underhand grip such that your palm is directed forward.
4. While keeping your elbow extended, stand upright while gripping the handle.
5. Step forward while keeping your elbow fully extended and allowing the cable’s resistance to pull your upper arm backward at an angle behind your torso.
    1. In this position, your forearm and upper arm should form a straight line with the cable that is diagonally angled forward and upward, and you should also feel tension from the cable rather than slack being present in the cable.
    2. How far you step forward from the cable station will be based on your personal preference, since you can effectively perform this exercise with variable shoulder positions.
        1. If you step forward further, you will be able to have your upper arm pulled further behind your body, which will place your biceps into a more lengthened position.
## Exercise technique
1. Curl the handle upward by bending at your elbow while keeping your upper arm fixed in the same position that allowed your elbow to be fully extended at the start of the rep (rather than allowing your upper arm to swing forward).
2. After your elbow has fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the handle in a controlled manner until your elbow has returned to an extended position.
    1. During the lowering phase, continue to keep your upper arm in the same position used to start the rep.
3. Alternate between curling the handle upwards and lowering it down until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
While also meaningfully developing your brachialis and brachioradialis, this exercise can effectively train biceps from a notably lengthened position.
