## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable to approximately hip height, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. Based on your preference, either connect a rope attachment to the cable or leave the cable without any attachment.
    1. Either option allows you to perform this exercise with essentially the same technique.
3. Stand facing the cable station with the cable just in front of one of your arms.
4. With the hand in front of the cable, firmly grasp the rope or the ball-shaped stopper with a neutral grip such that your palm is facing in toward your other side.
    1. If you decide to use the rope attachment, pull the rope to one side of the attachment and grip its end with your little finger and the adjacent aspect of your hand contacting the flat surface of the attachment’s rubber stopper as your thumb wraps over the outside of your other fingers.
    2. If you decide not to use any cable attachment, grasp the ball-shaped stopper at the end of the cable in the palm of your hand by wrapping your four smaller fingers around it and positioning your thumb on top of these fingers.
5. Take a step backward so that you feel tension from the cable as the weight stack separates.
6. As you keep your upper arm aligned with your torso, hinge at your hips to sit your buttocks back, and incline your torso so that it is fairly close to being in a horizontal position.
    1. If you wish to do so, you can place your free hand up against the cable station’s beam for additional stability.
    2. Maintain this bent-over-torso position throughout your set.
7. Drive your elbow backward and upward until your upper arm is approximately parallel to the ground while you allow tension from the cable to keep your elbow bent.
    1. Your elbow should now be somewhat above the side of your torso (which would look like behind your torso if you were standing upright) rather than being aligned with your torso.
    2. In this starting position, your elbow should be bent by at least 90 degrees.
## Exercise technique
1. While keeping your upper arm parallel to the ground, progressively straighten your elbow to drive the cable backward and upward in an arcing motion until you have fully straightened your elbow.
    1. As you move your elbow, you should keep the rest of your body stationary.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your elbow to bend in a controlled manner as you bring the cable downward and forward in an arcing fashion until you have returned to the starting position with your elbow bent by at least 90 degrees.
    1. Allow your elbow to continue bending as long as you feel tension from the cable pulling you toward a greater degree of bending.
    2. Continue keeping your upper arm parallel to the ground.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.
## Description
This exercise trains all three heads of your triceps, which together constitute the majority of muscle mass in your upper arms.
