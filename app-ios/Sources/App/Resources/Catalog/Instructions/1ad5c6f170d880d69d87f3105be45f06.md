## Setting up

1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on a bench with your feet flat on the floor while holding the dumbbells at your sides as your palms are facing in toward your sides (i.e., in a neutral grip position).
   1. You can opt to sit down on a flat bench without any back support or sit down on a seated bench with back support.
2. Bend your elbows to approximately right angles.
   1. Maintain this position throughout your set.

## Exercise technique

1. Raise the dumbbells out and away from your sides until your upper arms are close to parallel with the ground.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner back to your sides.
3. Alternate between raising the dumbbells up and lowering them down until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your lateral delt and supraspinatus, which are assisted by your front delt and upper trap.
