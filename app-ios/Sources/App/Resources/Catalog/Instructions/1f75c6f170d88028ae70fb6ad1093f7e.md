## Setting up

1. Lie down on your back next to your weight with your knees bent as the soles of your feet are flat on the ground and approximately hip width apart.
   1. In addition to using a weight plate, a medicine ball, a dumbbell, or a kettlebell is a viable option that you can use.
   2. You optionally can hook the tops of your feet under a sturdy object like a couch (while your soles are still flat on the ground) to help stabilize your body during this exercise.
2. Pick up the weight, firmly grasp each side of it, and press it up until your arms are directed up toward the ceiling with straight elbows.
   1. After doing so, you can rest your head on the ground.

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping your arms directed straight up, tighten your abdominal muscles to press your lower back down into the ground while you lift your head and shoulder blades up off the ground by curling your middle back.
   1. Given that this is a crunch rather than a sit-up, the lower half of your back should remain in contact with the ground while you only lift up the upper half of your back mildly off the floor.
   2. Based on what feels most comfortable to you, you can mildly tuck your chin toward your chest at the start of the lifting phase, or you can keep your neck near a neutral position like you would normally do when standing upright.
   3. As a cue, you can think about driving the bottom of your ribs down toward the top of your hip bones, which is the action that contraction of your abdominal muscles can produce.
   4. You have reached the end of the lifting phase once you feel that crunching up any further would require the lower half of your back to start lifting up off the floor.
   5. At the end of the lifting phase, your chest should be facing at an angle between forward and upward as opposed to only facing upward as it did in the starting position.
2. Reverse the movement by allowing your middle back to uncurl in a controlled manner as you lower your head and shoulder blades back to the starting position on the ground.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can dynamically train your rectus abdominis, which is assisted by your obliques.
