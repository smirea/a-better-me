## Setting up

1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and stand upright with approximately a hip width stance while holding the dumbbells at your sides with straight elbows.
2. Direct your palms so that they are facing in toward your sides (i.e., in a neutral grip position).

## Exercise technique

1. Brace your core by tensing the front and sides of your abdominals outward, like you would if you were bracing for someone to strike your stomach with a punch or forcible slap.
   1. Continue bracing your core throughout the exercise, which will help you maintain a rigid trunk position while you use hip motion instead of lower back motion to help lift the dumbbells.
2. While keeping your elbows straight, sit your buttocks back while allowing your knees to bend mildly and your torso to incline slightly forward.
   1. Throughout the set, keep your lower back flat or mildly arched rather than rounding it or excessively arching it.
3. Thrust your hips forcefully forward to generate momentum from your lower body. Simultaneously curl the dumbbells upward by bending your elbows while turning your palms to face back toward you at the top.
   1. Allow your elbows to swing forward mildly rather than keeping them pinned to your sides, like during a strict curl.
   2. The hip drive motion, which is similar to the lockout of a deadlift, will bring your torso into a vertical position with your knees straightened.
   3. Refrain from leaning back through excessive lower back arching, which can be easier to avoid if you think about squeezing your glutes.
   4. While you are intentionally using momentum to help your arm muscles lift the weight, you should not be “cheating” to the extent that you feel out of control.
   5. You should finish the lifting phase with your elbows fully bent as your forearms are contacting your biceps and you are holding the dumbbells with an underhand grip (rather than the neutral grip with which you started).
4. Lower the dumbbells in a controlled manner as you progressively allow your elbows to straighten and return near your sides. Simultaneously turn your palms in toward each other.
   1. You should finish with your elbows straight while you are holding the dumbbells in a neutral grip by your sides.
   2. Keep your torso upright and hips stationary as only your arms move during the descent.
   3. Your muscles are noticeably stronger during the lowering phase of an exercise compared to the lifting phase, so “cheating” during the lifting phase allows your biceps to experience greater loading during the more controlled lowering phase.
   4. You do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, but you should not let gravity just swing the dumbbells back down. If you are unable to control the lowering phase to any reasonable degree, you are likely using a weight that is excessively heavy relative to your current ability.
5. Alternate between steps 2-4 until you have completed your set.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
