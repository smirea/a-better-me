## Setting up

1. At a cable station, adjust the cable height to near the top of your head when standing, clip in a D-handle attachment, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. While facing away from the cable station and standing slightly to its side (such that the back of one shoulder is in line with the cable station), use your hand on the side closer to the cable to grasp the handle and take a step forward.
3. On the side holding the handle, raise your upper arm approximately 90 degrees out to your side (so that it is parallel to the ground) while bending your elbow to approximately 90 degrees and keeping your wrist in a fairly straight position.
   1. Maintain this position throughout your set.
4. Rotate your forearm through your available range of motion so that it is near a vertical position (or as close to that position as is comfortable for your shoulder), which will result in your palm facing forward.
   1. In this position, you should feel tension from the cable, so if that is not the case, take another step backward away from the cable station.
   2. Your shoulder mobility will affect how far you can rotate.

## Exercise technique

1. Pull the handle downward and forward by rotating your forearm away from the cable station until your forearm is situated parallel to the ground, as you continue to keep your upper arm held out to your side and parallel to the ground.
   1. All of this movement should occur from shoulder rotation as the bony prominence on the back of your elbow continues pointing straight out to the side throughout the rep.
   2. When thinking about this technique, it can be helpful to view your forearm as a clock’s hand, while your elbow is the center about which rotation occurs without any side-to-side motion.
2. Reverse the motion by rotating your forearm toward the cable station as you move the handle upward and backward in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set with one side and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your rotator cuff’s subscapularis, while also training your pec major, lat, teres major, and front delt.
