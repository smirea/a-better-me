## Setting up
1. After selecting your weight using the machine’s loading pin, adjust the seat or thigh pad height to a level that allows you to position your thighs snugly against the thigh pad while sitting with your feet flat on the floor.
    1. Certain machine models have an adjustable seat, others have an adjustable thigh pad, and some have both an adjustable seat and an adjustable thigh pad.
2. While facing the thigh pads and standing above the seat, firmly grasp the handles with your palms facing forward (i.e., an overhand grip) as they are fairly wide apart.
    1. Your hands should be clearly outside shoulder width, resulting in your arms forming a “Y-shape” with your torso.
    2. While being influenced by the length of your machine’s handles, specifically how wide a grip you use is up to your preference.
3. While holding the handles, sit down on the machine’s seat with the lower portion of your thighs snugly pressed against the machine’s thigh pad. Slightly lean your torso backward at your hips.
    1. When sitting down, allow your elbows to straighten in a controlled manner and your arms to raise up overhead in line with the machine’s resistance.
    2. If you do not feel tension from the machine in this position (which may occur for individuals who are tall and have long arms), lowering the seat height or widening your grip may be necessary to achieve resistance throughout the full range of motion for this exercise.
## Exercise technique
1. Drive your elbows down toward the sides of your torso while progressively bending them to pull the handles downward.
    1. Maintain the same slight backward lean of your torso that you adopted while setting up, rather than progressively leaning further backward.
    2. Allow your elbows to naturally flare outward from the midline of your body in a manner that feels comfortable to you during the initial portion of the pull.
    3. Pull through your available range of motion until you can no longer drive your elbows closer to your torso, which often results in the handles finishing below the height of your ears and slightly above the tops of your shoulders.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your elbows to lift upward and away from your torso as they progressively straighten in a controlled manner.
    1. You should finish with your arms positioned fully overhead with straight elbows.
    2. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the machine’s resistance swing your arms up in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can train your lat and teres major especially well while also effectively training your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
