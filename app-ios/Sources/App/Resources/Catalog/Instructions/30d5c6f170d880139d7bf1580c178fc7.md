## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on a flat exercise bench on a flat exercise bench while lifting the dumbbells onto your upper thighs near your abdomen, with your palms facing in toward each other.
    1. Position yourself so that there is enough room on the bench behind you so that you can lie your head down onto the end of the bench.
2. While marching your thighs up to help lift the dumbbells back toward your chest with your elbows bent, lean back to lie down with your back and the back of your head in contact with the bench as your head is right by the end of the bench.
    1. Once you are lying down, your feet should be flat on the ground, approximately shoulder width apart from each other, in a stable position stacked under your bent knees.
3. Tuck your chin toward your chest to lift your head off the bench. Simultaneously perform a slight crunching motion to lift the uppermost part of your upper back just off the bench without rolling your shoulders forward into a slouched position.
    1. Maintain this position throughout your set.
4. Point your palms backward to assume an underhand grip.
## Exercise technique
1. Straighten your elbows in a controlled manner and let the weights pull your arms down toward the ground through a range of motion comfortable to your shoulders.
    1. If your shoulders feel comfortable doing so, you can finish letting your arms descend once the dumbbells are barely above the ground.
    2. To prevent the dumbbells from contacting the bench, you may have to spread your arms slightly out away from your sides.
    3. It is normal to feel a stretching sensation in your biceps and the front of your shoulders in the bottom position, but you should not experience any shoulder discomfort when moving into the position appropriate for you.
2. Curl the dumbbells upward by fully bending your elbows without moving your upper arms.
    1. While your forearms move through elbow joint motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary as you avoid swinging your elbows upward through shoulder motion.
    2. Once you have fully bent your elbows at the top of the curl, your palms should be facing back toward you, since you should maintain an underhand grip with straight wrists throughout the set.
3. Reverse the movement by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner until you have fully straightened your elbows.
    1. Continue keeping your upper arms still as all the motion occurs from your forearms hinging about your elbows like the hand of an analog clock rotating about its center.
    2. To prevent your elbows from drifting down, you will have to perform a static front raise effort, since the force from the dumbbells will pull your upper arms down through the shoulder if you do not resist this force.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set.
    1. Following your last full rep, you can repeat step 2 to curl the dumbbells again before turning your palms inward to face each other and lifting your elbows up to be tucked by your sides. Then march your thighs up toward the dumbbells, and rock forward to sit up with the dumbbells on your upper thighs as you would when following a set of dumbbell bench pressing.
    2. If your mobility and arm length allow you to do so comfortably, you can alternatively set the dumbbells on the ground while lying down on the bench after resting your head and upper back fully on the bench and letting your arms drift downward. In contrast to the first approach described, setting the dumbbells down onto the ground is not recommended for everyone because it requires a greater degree of shoulder mobility than some people have. However, the first technique described requires you to curl the dumbbells up, which may not be feasible if you reach true muscular failure during the set.
## Description
Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
