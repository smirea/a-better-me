## Setting up
1. Load an adjustable EZ bar with your desired weight, or approach a fixed-weight EZ bar.
    1. Either type of EZ bar can be used interchangeably for this exercise.
2. While facing the middle of the bar, squat down and firmly grasp it with your palms angled backward and in toward each other (resulting in a semi-overhand grip) as your hands are about shoulder width apart.
    1. With the zig-zag-like shape of the EZ bar, you may have to rotate it before a shoulder width grip is accessible on the bar.
3. While keeping your elbows straight, stand fully upright and hold the bar directly in front of your legs with your arms by your sides.
## Exercise technique
1. Curl the bar upward by bending your elbows while keeping your elbows and upper arms fixed by your sides.
    1. Finish lifting the bar once your elbows are fully bent with your biceps contacting your forearms.
    2. While your forearms lift upward in an arcing manner through elbow motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary throughout your set.
    3. Avoid swinging your elbows and upper arms forward through shoulder motion.
    4. Since you should not be leaning backward, your shoulders should remain vertically aligned with your hips and knees when viewed from the side.
    5. Keep your wrists straight rather than letting the resistance from the weight bend them.
2. Reverse the movement by lowering the bar in a controlled manner until you have fully straightened your elbows.
    1. Nothing else should move except for your forearms lowering in an arcing manner through elbow motion.
    2. While you do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, you should avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the bar down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can develop your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis, but it may train your biceps somewhat less effectively than a curl with an underhand or neutral grip.
