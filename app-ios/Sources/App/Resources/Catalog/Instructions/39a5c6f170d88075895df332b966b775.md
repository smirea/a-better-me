## Setting up

1. Adjust the height of either the machine’s foot plate or the machine’s pivot points to a level that aligns your shoulders with the pivot point once you are standing on the foot plate.
   1. Which part of the machine can be adjusted in height varies based on the machine model you have.
   2. On each side of the machine, the pivot point is the center about which the lever arm rotates, and it is denoted by a small dot or circle in some machines.
   3. Aligning the center of your shoulders with the pivot points can help this exercise feel more comfortable and natural compared to if the foot plate height resulted in your shoulders being above or below the pivot points.
   4. To confirm whether you set up the machine appropriately, you will have to stand on it and visually check where your shoulders are positioned relative to the pivot points, so you may have to try out different settings before finding one that best suits your body.
2. If you are using a multi flight machine that lets you adjust the starting position of its handles, rotate the handles as high up as your machine allows.
   1. Standing pec fly machines typically do not let you adjust the starting position of the handles, but they can be used for this variation similarly to multi flight machines.
3. Select your desired load with the machine’s loading pin, and stand on the foot plate.
4. While facing sideways relative to the weight stack and toward the handle you will use, align the side of your shoulder (that you will train during this set) with the machine’s pivot point.
5. With your arm closer to the handle, reach up and firmly grasp the handle with your palm facing backward (i.e., overhand grip) while standing with a “proud chest” posture.
   1. Based on your preference, you can keep your elbow fully straight or maintain a slight bend in your elbow throughout the exercise.
   2. In the starting position, you should be standing upright with your arm directed down toward the ground.
   3. Situate your free hand in a comfortable position, such as resting by your side.

## Exercise technique

1. Sweep your arm down and back in an arcing manner to pull the handle toward you until it is right by your thigh.
   1. All the movement should occur from shoulder motion while you keep your legs and torso stationary throughout the exercise.
2. Reverse the motion by letting your arm move forward and upward toward the starting position in a controlled manner.
   1. To confirm you have moved through your full range of motion, you can lightly tap the weights you selected against the rest of the weight stack, or you can stop just before this point.
   2. While there is no need to use an extremely slow tempo, you should be actively controlling the weight the entire time. Do not let the weight abruptly swing your arm upward.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your lats, teres major, and rear delt.
