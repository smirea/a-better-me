## Setting up

1. With a cable station positioned to its lowest height, select your weight with the loading pin, and connect a D-handle attachment to the cable.
2. While standing with the side of one shoulder facing the cable station, squat down to grasp the handle firmly with the hand closer to the cable station as you keep your elbow straight and your palm facing in toward your side (i.e., a neutral grip).
   1. When squatting down, keep your lower back near a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright, rather than meaningfully rounding your lower back.
3. By extending your hips and knees, stand upright with the handle while continuing to keep your elbow straight and your lower back near a neutral position.
   1. When upright, you should feel resistance from the cable.
4. Take a step to the side away from the cable station and allow the cable’s tension to raise your arm somewhat out away from your side, such that your arm is directed in a straight line with the cable.

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping your elbow, hips, and knees extended, slowly lower the handle toward the base of the cable station in a controlled manner as you bend your torso toward the side of the cable station.
   1. Move through your comfortable full range of motion, which will normally result in you experiencing a stretching sensation in the muscles on the opposite side of your torso (including your lower back).
   2. Continue to keep your arm directed in a straight line with the cable, which should naturally occur and result in the handle moving in a diagonal path involving motion directed both downward and out away from your side.
   3. Your mobility will affect how far you can side bend.
   4. Avoid bending your lower back forward or arching it backward during this exercise.
   5. The tops of your hip bones should remain level throughout this movement, like they do when you are normally standing upright.
2. Reverse the motion by bending your torso away from the cable station in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with your torso upright and your shoulders approximately level with each other.
   1. Doing so will pull the handle diagonally out and away from the cable station.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to repeat the exercise with your other side.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your obliques and quadratus lumborum, while also training your erector spinae and intertransversarii lumborum.
