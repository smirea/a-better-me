## Setting up

1. Approach a surface (e.g., hand rail, wall, power rack) where you can place one hand to assist with maintaining your balance while performing this exercise.
2. Place your hand onto the nearby surface for balance assistance.
   1. Maintain this hand contact throughout the set.

## Exercise technique

1. Raise your heels into the air through the available range of motion of your ankles while keeping your knees straight and your trunk upright.
2. In a controlled manner, allow your heels to descend toward the ground while continuing to keep your knees straight.
3. After your heels touch the ground, alternate between raising and lowering your heels until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise offers you a convenient way to train your gastrocnemius and soleus in nearly any environment.
