## Setting up

1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and stand upright in a hip-width stance while holding the dumbbells at your sides with straight elbows.
2. Direct your palms so that they are facing in toward your sides (i.e., using a neutral grip position).

## Exercise technique

1. While keeping one dumbbell resting at your side with an extended elbow, curl the other dumbbell upward and across the front of your torso toward your opposite shoulder by bending at your elbow.
   1. As you start curling across your body, you will naturally rotate your shoulder inward such that your palm is directed toward the front of your torso.
   2. To allow the dumbbell to clear your torso, you will have to position your upper arm slightly in front of your body while curling (rather than keeping your upper arm right next to your side like you could with a standard hammer curl).
2. After your elbow has fully flexed, reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbell in a controlled manner until your elbow has returned to an extended position with the dumbbell and your upper back at your side.
3. With your other arm, repeat steps 1 and 2.
4. Alternate between curling with each side until you have completed your set.

## Description

Like other curl variations, this exercise can effectively train your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
