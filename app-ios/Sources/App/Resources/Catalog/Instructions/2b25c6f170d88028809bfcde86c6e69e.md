## Setting up

1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells, and sit down on a bench with your torso upright, feet flat on the floor, and arms hanging straight down.
   1. You can opt to sit down on a seated bench with your back resting against the back support, or you can sit on a flat bench without any back support.

## Exercise technique

1. While holding the dumbbells by your sides with straight elbows, direct your palms forward to adopt an underhand grip.
2. Curl the dumbbells upward in an arcing manner by bending your elbows without rotating your palms.
   1. Keep your wrists straight and your elbows by your sides, rather than swinging your elbows forward through shoulder motion.
   2. While your forearms move through elbow joint motion, the rest of your body should remain stationary.
   3. Since you will maintain an underhand grip during this lifting phase, your palms should be facing upward when your forearms are parallel to the ground.
   4. At the top of the curl, your elbows should be fully bent with your forearms touching your biceps and your palms facing back toward your shoulders.
3. Rotate your palms forward away from you to adopt an overhand grip, which is also called a reverse grip.
4. While keeping your wrists straight, lower the dumbbells in a controlled manner by allowing your elbows to straighten progressively without rotating your palms.
   1. Since you will maintain a reverse grip during this lowering phase, your palms should be facing down when your forearms are parallel to the ground before facing backward once you have fully straightened your elbows.
   2. You do not have to perform an extremely slow lowering phase, but you should avoid letting gravity abruptly swing the dumbbells down.
5. Alternate between steps 1-4 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can develop your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis while also training your wrist extensor muscles more effectively than most curl variations.
