## Setting up
1. Lie down with your abdomen contacting the ground (i.e., in the prone position) and your head lifted slightly off the ground as you maintain a tucked chin position with your face directed toward the ground
2. With extended elbows, raise your arms straight out to your sides so that they form a “T-shape” with your torso while resting on the ground.
3. Rotate your arms so that your palms face forward.
    1. If you make a thumbs-up gesture in this position, your thumb should be pointing up toward the ceiling.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift your arms up off the ground through your available shoulder range of motion while maintaining the “T-shape” and keeping your chest in contact with the ground.
    1. Your shoulder mobility will affect how high you can raise your arms.
    2. If you notice your chest lifting up off the ground during this movement, that indicates that you most likely are dynamically extending your lumbar spine, which can occur as a compensation pattern instead of lifting your arm only with your shoulder girdle muscles.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering your arms in a controlled manner until your hands lightly contact the ground while maintaining the “T-shape.”
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise trains your rear delt, traps, and rhomboids, which are assisted by your rotator cuff’s infraspinatus, teres minor, and supraspinatus.
