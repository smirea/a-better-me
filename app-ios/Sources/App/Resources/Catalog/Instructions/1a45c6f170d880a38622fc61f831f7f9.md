## Setting up
1. Position yourself on your hands, knees, and toes, with your elbows straight and your knees bent.
2. Prop your forearms onto the ground with your upper arms directed straight down such that they are approximately perpendicular to the ground.
    1. Based on your preference, you can either make a fist with each hand and direct your palms in toward each other or place your palms flat on the ground.
## Exercise technique
1. Straighten one knee to move your foot behind you and position the bottom of its toes onto the ground before doing the same with your other leg.
    1. You should be bearing your bodyweight into the ground through your elbows and feet.
2. Tighten your abdominal muscles and squeeze your glutes to maintain a flat back position while keeping your knees straight and elbows bent.
    1. Doing so keeps your hips up (but not excessively hiked) rather than letting them sag, which would result in an exaggerated arch in your lower back.
    2. In this position, you should be able to draw a straight line from your legs through your hips to your torso.
    3. Do not allow your chest to sag, as this can cause your shoulder blades to pinch together.
    4. Keep your neck near a neutral position like you would normally do when standing upright.
3. Press through your feet to rock your body back forward as you move at your ankles like you would during the lifting phase of a calf raise (also known as a heel raise).
    1. Doing so will prompt your elbows to bend further.
    2. Continue keeping your abdominal muscles and glutes tightened to maintain the same rigid trunk position you adopted in step 2.
4. Reverse the motion you just performed by rocking your body backward at your ankles as you continue keeping your abdominal muscles and glutes tightened.
5. Alternate between steps 3 and 4 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can statically train your core muscles, including your rectus abdominis (AKA  the “six-pack muscle”) and your abdominal obliques as they function to maintain a rigid trunk position.
