## Setting up

1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on a bench with an upright posture and your feet flat on the floor while holding the dumbbells at your sides with extended elbows and your palms facing in toward you (i.e., a neutral grip).
   1. You can opt to sit down on a flat bench without any back support or sit down on a seated bench with back support.
   2. If you are using dumbbells that you consider fairly heavy, reaching forward from a bent-over position to pick up both dumbbells simultaneously from a rack may be awkward and challenging.
   3. For an easier setup, you can pick up one dumbbell, carry it, place it on a flat bench (or sturdy box), and move the other dumbbell onto the bench before picking up both dumbbells from a mechanically advantaged position.
   4. Pick up the dumbbells with a hip hinge or squat motion, where you lift them by extending your hips and knees while keeping your lower back flat.

## Exercise technique

1. Shrug your shoulders upward through your available range of motion while keeping your elbows extended, which will lift the dumbbells upward.
   1. While doing so, keep your neck in a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright and looking straight ahead.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your shoulders to descend in a controlled manner through your available range of motion, which will lower the dumbbells.
   1. At the end of this motion, your shoulders should ideally be positioned a little lower than where they are normally situated while standing at rest due to the downward stretch that the weight can provide.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise primarily trains your upper traps and levator scapulae.
