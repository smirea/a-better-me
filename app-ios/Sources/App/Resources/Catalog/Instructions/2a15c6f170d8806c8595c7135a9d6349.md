## Setting up

1. Place a pair of furniture sliders on the ground hip-width apart from each other.
2. Lie down with your back on the ground, and situate the bottom of your heels on top of the sliders with your knees fully bent.
   1. You can rest the back of your head on the ground and leave your arms on the ground by your sides or in whichever position you prefer.

## Exercise technique

1. Lift your hips up off the ground by driving your heels down into the sliders, squeezing your glutes, and tightening your abs.
   1. After performing this glute bridge motion, you should now be able to draw a straight line from your knees through your hips to your shoulders when viewed from the side.
   2. Focus on moving your hips and avoid arching your lower back excessively.
   3. As a cue for engaging your glutes, you can think about trying to hold onto a hundred-dollar bill by tightly squeezing it between your glutes.
2. Without bending your hips, move your feet forward by progressively straightening your knees in a controlled manner until they are almost fully straightened.
   1. Continue keeping your glutes squeezed, abs tensed, and pressure concentrated through your heels throughout the exercise.
   2. All the motion should occur from your shins moving at your knee joints, while you are still able to draw a straight line from your knees through your hips to your shoulders.
   3. At the end of this lowering phase, your buttocks should just barely be above the ground.
   4. You do not have to perform this movement with an extremely slow tempo, but you should avoid letting your feet abruptly slide forward in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Reverse the movement by driving your heels toward your buttocks to bend your knees and pull the sliders backward.
   1. You should finish with your knees close to being fully bent as the sliders are vertically under your knees.
   2. If you experience a notably uncomfortable cramping sensation in your hamstrings when your knees are fully bent, you can stop bending your knees just before reaching that point.
4. Alternate between steps 2 and 3 until you have completed your set and are ready to lower your buttocks to the ground by bending at your hips.

## Description

While statically engaging your gluteus maximus, this exercise can train all your hamstrings, which are assisted by your gracilis, sartorius, and gastrocnemius.
