## Setting up

1. Select your desired load at each weight stack with the machine’s loading pins, and adjust the seat to a height that lets you rest your chest comfortably against the chest pad once you are seated.
   1. It is generally appropriate to situate the seat where your upper chest can be positioned slightly above the top of the chest pad, while your middle and lower chest contact the pad once you are seated.
   2. The proper seat height should let the exercise feel smooth and natural, so if you think your arm path feels somewhat awkward during the set, it is recommended to adjust the seat height. When using this machine for the first time, trying out different seat heights may be helpful to determine specifically which setting helps the row feel best to you based on your body proportions.
2. Adjust the thigh pad to a height that lets you position your thighs snugly against it while sitting with your feet flat on the floor.
3. While facing the chest pad and standing above the seat, firmly grasp the handles with an underhand grip that points your palms facing backward.
4. Sit down on the seat with the lower portion of your thighs snugly pressed against the machine’s thigh pad. Simultaneously let your elbows straighten as your arms raise overhead in line with the machine’s resistance.
   1. If you do not feel tension from the machine in this position (which may occur for certain individuals who are quite tall and have long arms), you may need to lower the height of the seat and thigh pad to experience resistance throughout the full range of motion for this exercise.

## Exercise technique

1. Pull your shoulder blades down away from your ears. Simultaneously drive your elbows down and back in an arcing manner to pull the handles toward you.
   1. Pull through your full range of motion, which will typically result in your elbows finishing slightly behind your torso.
   2. When focusing on driving your elbows down and back, they should naturally bend to the appropriate degree.
   3. Keep your torso upright against the chest pad throughout your set rather than leaning backward during the pull.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your elbows to lift upward and away from your torso as they progressively straighten to raise the handles in a controlled manner.
   1. Let your shoulder blades move naturally as they elevate with your arms.
   2. Finish with your elbows fully straightened.
   3. There is no need to perform this step extremely slowly, but you should avoid letting the machine’s resistance yank your arms up in an uncontrolled fashion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop your lat, teres major, and rear delt while also meaningfully training your biceps, brachialis, and brachioradialis.
