## Setting up
1. Pick up a pair of dumbbells and sit down on a flat exercise bench or chair.
2. While holding the dumbbells, lean forward at your hips and position your forearms on top of your thighs with your palms facing upward and your wrists situated slightly in front of your knees.
3. Allow the force from the dumbbells to bend your wrists and bring your hands downward such that your palms are facing forward.
    1. In this position, the dumbbells should not be contacting your legs, so scoot your forearms forward if they are initially contacting your legs.
4. While maintaining your grip on them, let the dumbbells roll down your fingers.
    1. This downward motion will occur from movement at the joints formed by your knuckles and the finger bones closest to your knuckles.
    2. After they roll down your fingers, the dumbbells should no longer be fully contacting the palm side of your knuckles, in contrast to when you typically maintain a firm grip on dumbbells with a fist-like hand position.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift the dumbbells upward by curling your wrists and fingers simultaneously through their available range of motion while keeping your forearms resting on your thighs.
    1. All of the motion should be coming from your wrists and the joints your fingers form with your knuckles.
    2. About halfway through the lifting phase, your palms should be facing straight up when your hands are aligned with your forearms.
    3. At the end of the lifting phase, when the dumbbells are fully raised, your palms should be angled back toward you, and your fingers should be fully wrapped around the handles in a fist-like hand position.
    4. When performing this exercise appropriately, you should feel tension in the muscles on the palm side of your forearms.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the dumbbells in a controlled manner as they roll down your fingers until you have returned to the starting position with your palms directed forward away from you.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively train the six wrist flexor muscles on the palm side of your forearm in addition to the hand muscles involved in gripping.
