## Setting up

1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to its highest level, connect a straight bar attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. While facing the cable station, reach up and grasp the bar with an underhand grip such that your palms are facing back toward you.
3. Take a step backward and slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips.
   1. Maintain this slight torso lean throughout your set.
4. Pull your upper arms down and back until they are positioned at your sides with your elbows close to fully bent.
   1. Maintain this upper arm position throughout your set.

## Exercise technique

1. Push the bar downward by extending your elbows until they are straightened.
   1. At the end of this motion, the bar should be situated in front of your thighs.
2. Reverse the motion by bending your elbows through your available range of motion to raise the bar in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position.
   1. Strive to have your elbows as close to fully bent as you can by the time you have finished this motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.

## Description

This exercise can effectively develop the triceps, including the long head, which is not trained particularly well by pressing exercises.
