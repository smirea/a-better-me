As an alternative to performing this exercise with the front of your body facing the machine’s weight stack, you can also set up facing the opposite direction, with your back directed toward the machine’s weight stack. With this approach, you would not be able to keep your thighs pressed under the machine’s thigh pad, which could reduce the stability of this variation. However, as long as you are not using a weight heavy enough to lift your whole body from the seat, the exercise remains sufficiently stable to effectively train the same muscles without the thigh pad. This modification may be appropriate if your head continues to obstruct the bar's path when facing the cable station, even when you are fully scooted forward on the seat with your chin tucked to your chest. Because a less flexed neck position may be required for bar clearance, the oppositely directed sitting position can also be preferable for people who experience symptom aggravation of pre-existing conditions when meaningfully flexing their neck forward.
