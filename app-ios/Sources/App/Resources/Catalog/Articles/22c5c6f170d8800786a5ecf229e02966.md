While performing a single arm dead hang, your torso may have a tendency to start rotating to one side. This rotation will not detract from the grip training stimulus the exercise provides, but if you intentionally keep your chest facing the same direction as when you start the exercise, you may be able to resist the rotation and maintain a static body position. Alternatively, you may be able to use your free hand to help stabilize yourself and limit any inadvertent rotation. To do so, you could place your hand on a nearby surface, such as a beam from a power rack or a wall, and use that hand to counteract any rotation of your torso. This approach would not be viable if you do not have an elevated surface nearby. However, you can use a similar technique by contacting with one leg a box, step, or exercise bench that you place underneath you. When using either your free hand or a leg to limit torso rotation, press horizontally with that limb to stabilize yourself, rather than pressing downward into the nearby surface to support your bodyweight, which would reduce the intensity of the hang.
