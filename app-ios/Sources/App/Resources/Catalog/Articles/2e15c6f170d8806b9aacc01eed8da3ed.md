If you notice that your hinge range of motion is constrained by the bar contacting the bottom of the machine before you feel that you have finished sitting your hips fully backward, you can select the Smith machine deficit Romanian deadlift from this database. Compared with the barbell Romanian deadlift, the deficit variation may be appropriate for a greater proportion of lifters when using the Smith machine. This is due to the lowest height to which a bar can descend in a Smith machine, resulting in the bar being situated higher up than a barbell would be on the ground, even when loaded with large-diameter weight plates.
