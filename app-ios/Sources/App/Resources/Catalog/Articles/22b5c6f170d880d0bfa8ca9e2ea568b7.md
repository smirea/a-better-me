People sometimes perform a dumbbell exercise that is quite similar to this cable shoulder internal rotation exercise in terms of body position and joint motion. While holding a dumbbell with a 90-degree elbow bend and keeping the upper arm by the side, dynamic shoulder external and internal rotation can be performed. However, the dumbbell variation provides no meaningful resistance to shoulder rotation in either direction because the dumbbell's resistance is directed vertically downward, as gravitational force acts on it. The elbow flexor muscles must function isometrically to maintain a 90-degree elbow joint angle while holding the dumbbell, but the dumbbell provides no additional resistance to shoulder rotation. In contrast, the horizontally oriented line of resistance from this cable variation directly resists rotational shoulder motion and enables effective training of the shoulder internal rotator muscles.
