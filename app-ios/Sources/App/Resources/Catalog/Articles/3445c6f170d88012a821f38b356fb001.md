The D.Y. row machine is named after Dorian Yates, widely recognized as one of the greatest bodybuilders of all time, with his crowning achievement being six consecutive Mr. Olympia titles from 1992 to 1997. Dorian’s total number of Mr. Olympia wins has only been exceeded by four bodybuilders in history, namely Arnold Schwarzenegger, Phil Heath, Lee Haney, and Ronnie Coleman.

Gary Jones, the founder of Hammer Strength, said he initially designed a prototype of what later became the D.Y. row machine as a custom machine for Yates after he sustained a wrist injury that interfered with his ability to perform the barbell rows, which served as a staple of his back training. Demand subsequently grew for the machine, and Jones refined the machine into its commercially available version. While Hammer Strength developed the first D.Y row machine, other manufacturers have since released their own similarly designed models.

In contrast to most other plate-loaded row machines, D.Y. row machines have handles designed to enable an underhand grip while also having their pivot points positioned overhead. Typical plate-loaded row machines have handles designed to be grasped with an overhand and/or neutral grip while having their pivot points situated closer to the ground.
