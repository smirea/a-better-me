Kipping proficiently involves transitioning efficiently from an arched body position to a hollow body position. Learning how to perform kipping pull-ups can be a meaningfully easier task if you have initially developed competency in achieving these two positions independently, which training the hollow body hold and the superman exercise can provide. If you have a difficult time performing either exercise by itself in a controlled manner, it can be rather challenging to integrate them together into a coordinated, higher velocity movement.
