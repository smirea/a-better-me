Because greater elbow flexion torque can be generated with a supinated forearm position than a pronated forearm position, the dumbbell Zottman curl can function as an eccentric overload variation of the typical reverse curl, where a reverse grip is used for both the concentric and eccentric phases. For the same number of reps, a greater weight can most likely be used for a Zottman curl than a reverse curl due to a Zottman curl facilitating greater elbow flexion torque during its concentric phase using a supinated forearm position.
