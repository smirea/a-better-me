A marching dead bug serves as a regressed version of a standard dead bug, which involves the simultaneous motion of one leg and the opposite arm. As an alternative to the technique described on this page, you have the option of keeping both arms directed straight up to the ceiling, which is the starting position for a standard dead bug. However, the hand placement instructed on this page can provide immediate tactile feedback on whether you are appropriately bracing your core and resisting lumbar spine motion. When you are new to this type of movement, receiving this feedback can help you learn what it feels like to move your extremities independently of your trunk. Once you have learned how to do so, you can be better prepared to replicate this bracing ability during other exercises that lack this feedback.
