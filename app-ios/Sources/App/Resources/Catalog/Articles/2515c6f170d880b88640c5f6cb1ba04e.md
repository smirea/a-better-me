The JM press is named for JM (John Mark) Blakely, who is an acclaimed American bench press specialist who has bench pressed 710lb (322kg) in the 308lb (140kg) weight class of multi-ply powerlifting. Blakely popularized this exercise as a hybrid between a lying triceps extension and a close grip bench press. He reported using this exercise as a way to develop triceps strength for the bench press with lower shoulder loading than a close grip bench press provides. When putting his bench press strength into perspective, it is worth noting that he primarily competed from 1992-2001. Multi-ply bench press shirts have evolved considerably since then, so the equipment Blakely used did not provide nearly as much assistance for bench pressing as modern multi-ply lifters use. For reference, the equipped bench press world record was 802lb (364kg) in 2001 when Blakely completed his remarkable 710lb (322kg) bench press, but it now exceeds 1,400lb (635kg) as the bench press shirts have evolved.
