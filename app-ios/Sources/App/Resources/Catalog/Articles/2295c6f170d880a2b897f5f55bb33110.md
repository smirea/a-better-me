The adage “use it or lose it” can readily apply to the available range of motion you have in your body. If you chronically do not perform any activities where you raise your arms up fully overhead, your shoulders can gradually lose the range of motion required to do so, especially as you age beyond young adulthood. Alternatively, performing overhead activities can help you improve your shoulder range of motion if it is limited or allow you to maintain your range of motion if you already have a full range of motion. Vertical pulling exercises like lat pulldown and pull-up variations may assist with improving your shoulder range of motion in an overhead position, but dead hangs can be even more efficient for doing so since you spend the entire duration of each set in a fully overhead position.
