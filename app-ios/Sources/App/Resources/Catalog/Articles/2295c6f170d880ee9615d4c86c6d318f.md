As you increase your grip strength over time, you can progress your dead hangs in a few ways. Just like you could increase the number of reps you perform with a particular load during a dynamic exercise, you naturally can increase the duration in which you perform your dead hang sets. Rather than simply performing progressively longer sets, you can alternatively perform a more intense variation, such as a single arm dead hang. However, switching from a standard dead hang to a one arm dead hang can provide a considerable increase in difficulty that may be a more dramatic change in challenge than you wish. As an intermediate option, you can perform a dead hang with both arms, but you grip lightly with one side as the other side supports more than half of your bodyweight. Over time, you can progressively shift more of your bodyweight into one arm until you feel ready to dead hang with only one arm. You can also perform a weighted dead hang with both hands as you decide specifically how much more challenging you wish to make the movement based on how much weight you add to your pull-up/dip belt. As another way to intensify the standard dead hang, you can additionally add fat grip attachments to the bar and perform a dead hang with both arms, or you can wrap a thick towel around the bar and hang while gripping the towel.
