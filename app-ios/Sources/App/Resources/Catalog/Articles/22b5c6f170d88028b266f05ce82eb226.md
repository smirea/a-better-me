The overhand grip machine reverse fly has been found to yield lower mean EMG activity of the rear delt and the rotator cuff’s infraspinatus compared to the neutral grip machine reverse fly. The difference for EMG activity of the rear delt was found to be of a small effect size, while the difference for EMG activity of the infraspinatus was found to be of a moderate effect size. When comparing these two machine reverse fly variations, the mean EMG activity of the lateral deltoid also trended toward being greater with the neutral grip, but the difference was not statistically significant.
