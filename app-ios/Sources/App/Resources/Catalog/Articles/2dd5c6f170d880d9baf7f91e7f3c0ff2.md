When crossing and uncrossing your arms over each other during each rep, your arms will naturally be slightly uneven, resulting in a very small difference in how your right and left sides move during a set. There is little reason to think that such a small difference is likely to result in the development of a meaningful muscle size or strength asymmetry. Still, you have the option of alternating which arm crosses in the higher position from set to set if you wish to do so. If you wish to have every rep train each side with the identical technique, you can also train the single arm cable rear delt fly.
