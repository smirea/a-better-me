The Pendlay row is named in reference to Glenn Pendlay, who was a renowned United States weightlifting coach. The technique for the Pendlay row is quite similar to the bent over overhand grip barbell row, but in contrast to Pendlay row, the plates do not touch the ground in between reps of the bent over overhand grip barbell row. Coupled with this difference, the Pendlay row typically uses a torso position closer to horizontal relative to the ground.
