Compared to the standard biceps curl, where you use an underhand grip, you will most likely not be able to perform as many reps with the same weight when performing the reverse curl, since your forearm position influences how much elbow flexion torque can be generated. Relative to using an underhand grip or a neutral grip, less elbow flexion torque can be generated with an overhand grip, likely in part due to the biceps having poorer leverage to flex the elbow with an overhand grip. Multiple studies have also found that the biceps exhibit lower EMG activity during curling (or resisted isometric elbow flexion) with an overhand grip than with an underhand grip, suggesting that overhand grip curls may not be the optimal means of developing the biceps.
