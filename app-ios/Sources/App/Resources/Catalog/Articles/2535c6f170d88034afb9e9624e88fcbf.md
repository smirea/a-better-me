Flat bench pressing is not inherently injurious to the shoulders, but some lifters with pre-existing shoulder issues report that their shoulders feel more comfortable when pressing from a slight decline. Performing a flat bench press with greater back arching can place the shoulders in a position similar to that of a slight decline press with less back arch. However, some people with limited mobility or pre-existing lower back issues cannot comfortably arch to that degree. Compared to using a flat bench, a slight decline is unlikely to detract from the stimulus provided to the upper pec major to any meaningful extent. Consequently, it is reasonable for lifters to use the slight decline bench press as a substitute for flat bench pressing if they are more comfortable with the slight decline.
