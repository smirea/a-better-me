When performed as a maximal isometric action against manual resistance, the prone T raise has demonstrated very high EMG activity in the middle trapezius, high EMG activity in the lower trapezius, rhomboids, and levator scapulae, and moderately high EMG activity in the upper trapezius. While the other muscles can contribute to retraction of the shoulder girdle, the levator scapulae functions as an elevator and downward rotator of the scapula, so its high EMG activity would not necessarily be expected during this exercise.
