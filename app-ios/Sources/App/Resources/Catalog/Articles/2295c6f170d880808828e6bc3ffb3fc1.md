In this database and in common usage, standing exercises, including “overhead press,” are strict variations in which the legs remain stationary throughout the rep, requiring only the upper body to lift the weight upward. The push press is also a standing overhead press variation, but it differs from strict variations by utilizing leg drive to assist the upper body in propelling the weight upward. Because of the leg-drive contribution, which is especially impactful during the first half of the pressing phase, push presses allow lifters to utilize heavier loads than strict overhead presses. A push press is also a more technically complicated exercise than a strict overhead press, so it would be helpful for someone to develop competent technique with a strict overhead press before starting to train push presses.
