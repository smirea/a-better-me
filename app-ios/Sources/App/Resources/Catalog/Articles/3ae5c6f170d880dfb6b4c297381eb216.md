The standard thickness for a single bench press board corresponds to the thickness of a 2x4 piece of wood, which is 1.5in (3.8cm). A 2x4 piece of wood receives its name from its dimensions before the wood is further processed from having an initial thickness of 2in (5.1cm) and width of 4in (10.2cm) to its final thickness of 1.5in (3.8cm) and width of 3.5in (8.9cm). While wooden boards were exclusively used when board presses were first performed, bench block attachments are now commonly used as alternatives to boards. While they do not enhance the training response of board pressing, bench block attachments can be more convenient, since they do not require a training partner to use and can also be readily transported in a gym bag due to their more compact size.
