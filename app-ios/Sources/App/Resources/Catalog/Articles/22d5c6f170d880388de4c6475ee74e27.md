Rather than alternating between steps 1-4 described under the exercise technique (which may be considered the standard version of the reverse crunch), some people perform a reverse crunch by exclusively alternating between steps 2 and 3. Neither technique is inherently right nor wrong, but each variation could have a somewhat different training effect. Using an abbreviated range of motion would reduce the stimulus to the hip flexor muscles and result in a variation that predominantly trains the abdominal muscles dynamically, rather than training them both statically (during steps 1 and 4) and dynamically (during steps 2 and 3) per rep.
