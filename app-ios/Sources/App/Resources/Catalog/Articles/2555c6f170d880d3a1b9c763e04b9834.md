With a certain amount of weight, getting into and out of position for a decline dumbbell press without a spotter may be more challenging than performing the exercise itself. Performing higher rep sets with a lighter load can reduce this challenge to a degree, but you can alternatively select a different decline variation, such as the decline single arm dumbbell bench press, which will naturally be easier to get into and out of position with the lighter load. The slight decline dumbbell bench press can additionally allow you to train decline pressing with a meaningfully lower difficulty for getting into and out of position.
