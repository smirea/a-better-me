To help you use a standardized foot placement you can reliably replicate from set to set, you can use an alternative approach instead of the stepping method described in the “setting up” section. For this method, sit on the edge of your elevated surface and extend one leg in front of you by straightening your knee. Wherever that foot reaches determines how far from your elevated surface you position your front foot. Regardless of the setup approach you select, it can be beneficial to use a method that promotes a consistent technique in an appropriate posture, reducing the likelihood that you unintentionally end up in a less-than-ideal position.
