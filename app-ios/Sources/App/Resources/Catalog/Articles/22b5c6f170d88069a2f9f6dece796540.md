Based on muscle size data reported by Holzbaur et al. (2007), the wrist extensor muscles trained by this exercise constitute approximately one fifth of total forearm muscle volume. These muscles are noticeably smaller than the wrist flexor muscles on the opposite side of the forearm, which constitute approximately half of total forearm muscle volume. Primarily due to this discrepancy in muscle size, wrist extension strength is typically lower than wrist flexion strength; therefore, it is common to use lighter loads for wrist extension exercises than for wrist flexion exercises.
