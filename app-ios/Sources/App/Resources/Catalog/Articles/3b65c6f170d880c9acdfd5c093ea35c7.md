Prior to starting your single leg leg press set, you have the option of initially setting up with both feet on the foot plate to press the sled off the safety stoppers, or you can situate only your working side’s foot on the foot plate from the start. After finishing your set, you similarly have the option to lower the sled onto the safety stoppers with both legs, or you can lower it only with the leg you just trained. Both approaches are viable, so you can proceed whichever one you personally prefer.

Using both legs to get into the starting position will naturally be easier for what will be the working leg during the upcoming set, and returning the sled onto the safety stoppers will also be less taxing to the already fatigued working leg after the set. Some people may find that initially placing both feet on the foot plate can provide a reference point that helps achieve the ideal foot position as well.
