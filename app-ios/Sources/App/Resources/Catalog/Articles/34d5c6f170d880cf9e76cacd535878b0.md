To move into the starting position for this exercise, you are essentially performing the lowering phase of a rep with some shoulder motion. If you end your set by curling the dumbbells up before sitting up from the bench (instead of just setting the dumbbells down onto the ground), you are also finishing with half of a rep. While each full rep of the lying dumbbell curl starts with the elbows extended and finishes with the elbows flexed, you can consider the two half reps you perform to get into and out of position for the exercise to together add up to a whole rep that you can include for how many reps you log per set. Whether you do so is completely up to your preference, since what matters is that you track your reps in a consistent manner from set to set to help you reliably measure your progress.
