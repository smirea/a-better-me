If you notice that your weight contacts the ground before you have reached what is your body’s full available range of motion, you can perform the Jefferson curl while standing on top of a sturdy elevated surface, such as an aerobic step or bumper plate, to facilitate a greater range of motion than could be reached on the ground. Alternatively, you may be able to select smaller diameter weight plates to extend the range of motion if you initially used larger diameter weight plates that restrict how far you can descend.
