Most lifters do not perform any exercises that effectively train the ankle dorsiflexor muscles, so including an ankle dorsiflexion exercise in your program can allow you to train muscles that would otherwise most likely be neglected. However, the ankle dorsiflexor muscles are fairly small as they have been measured to have a combined muscle volume that is about one fifth of the ankle plantar flexor muscles, which are trained by calf raise variations. Correspondingly, the ankle dorsiflexor muscles have a limited contribution to your lower body muscularity, and increasing their size by a particular percentage will have a considerably lower effect than if you increased the size of your calf muscles by the same percentage. Nonetheless, the tibialis anterior (which has the fourth largest volume among the lower leg muscles) is one of the most visible muscles because it lies superficial to other muscles on the front of the lower leg, making its definition quite apparent when you wear shorts.
