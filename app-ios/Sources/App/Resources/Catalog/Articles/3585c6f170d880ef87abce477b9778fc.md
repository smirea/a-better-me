If your goal is to stimulate increases in your strength and/or muscle size, moving from point A to point B in whatever manner minimizes muscle demand is not the most efficient means of doing so. However, if you were competing in a competition with the goal of maximizing how many times you can touch the bar with your toes, the strict form described by this exercise’s technique section would not be the best means of achieving that particular objective. In a competition like the CrossFit Games, optimizing the likelihood of success involves expending the least amount of energy and effort to complete each rep within the specific parameters dictated by the rules. When performing max toes-to-bar reps in a competitive environment, that will involve meaningfully using momentum in a swinging manner and explosively kicking the feet up to finish each rep while minimizing the muscular challenge from each rep.
