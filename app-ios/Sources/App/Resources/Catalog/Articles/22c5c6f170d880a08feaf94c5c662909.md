If you are not accustomed to performing shoulder girdle depression exercises, it may be difficult to learn the appropriate movement pattern at the intensity required when you are fully hanging from the bar. Even if you have developed a proficient technique, you may simply wish to perform a similar variation with a lower absolute intensity that allows you to perform more reps per set. If either scenario applies to you, you can perform the regressed scapular pull-up from this exercise library, which involves keeping the balls of your feet and your toes on the ground.
