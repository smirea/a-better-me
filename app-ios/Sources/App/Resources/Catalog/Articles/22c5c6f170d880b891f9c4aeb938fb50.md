Compared to the cable straight bar reverse curl (using an overhand grip) and the cable rope hammer curl (using a neutral grip), greater EMG activity of the biceps and the brachioradialis has been measured to occur during the lifting phase of the cable straight bar biceps curl (using an underhand grip). The size of this effect was greater for the biceps than the brachioradialis, and EMG activity of these muscles during the eccentric phase of the curls was similar regardless of which grip was used. During the lifting phase, the cable rope hammer curl has also been reported to yield greater EMG activity of the biceps than the cable straight bar reverse curl, but these two variations have been found to result in similar brachioradialis EMG activity.
