If you have very strong ankle dorsiflexor muscles, you may be able to reach the limit of small-diameter plates that you can add to the machine’s loading sleeves. If you still wish to progress the load over time, switching from the double leg variation to the single leg machine tib raise may be appropriate. Alternatively, you may be able to elevate the machine onto an object like an aerobic step to provide clearance above the ground for you to be able to load larger diameter plates without the plates touching the ground and limiting your range of motion. On a per-pound (or per-kilogram) basis, typical 10lb (or 5kg) weight plates can be meaningfully thicker than heavier plates, so using heavier plates will not require as much loading sleeve length to provide the same load.
