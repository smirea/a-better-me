Compared to the shoulder width grip that is used for the standard barbell Jefferson curl, using a snatch grip for this variation can allow you to descend through a greater range of motion before the weight contacts the ground, since the snatch grip results in the weight being held higher off the floor. However, if you have a rather high degree of mobility and notice that your weight contacts the ground before you have reached what is your body’s full available range of motion, you can perform this exercise while standing on top of a sturdy elevated surface such as an aerobic step or bumper plate. Doing so can facilitate a greater range of motion than could be reached on the ground, but you may alternatively be able to select smaller diameter weight plates to extend the range of motion if you initially used larger diameter weight plates that restrict how far you can descend.
