Relative to other exercises that train the same muscle groups, the single-leg bodyweight Romanian deadlift imparts a low level of resistance to the gluteus maximus and other hip extensor muscles. If training with the objective of increasing muscle size and or strength, Romanian deadlifts that use additional load, such as single-leg dumbbell Romanian deadlifts, will likely be more efficient for most able-bodied individuals. Nonetheless, if you are new to single-leg Romanian deadlifts, starting with the bodyweight variation can be quite helpful for learning the appropriate technique before progressing to using weights. Even if your hip and thigh muscles are not meaningfully challenged during this bodyweight exercise, it can still serve as a dynamic exercise that trains balance and hamstring flexibility, and it can be performed for these purposes or as part of a warm-up before higher-intensity exercises.
