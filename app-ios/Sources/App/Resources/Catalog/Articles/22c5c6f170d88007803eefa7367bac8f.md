When performed as a maximal isometric action against manual resistance, the prone Y raise has demonstrated high EMG activity in all three regions of the trapezius (particularly the lower region) and in the rhomboids. Moderately high EMG activity of the serratus anterior and levator scapulae has also been measured to occur during this exercise. The levator scapulae functions as an elevator and downward rotator of the scapula, so its high EMG activity would not necessarily be expected during this exercise, which resists upward rotation of the scapula.
