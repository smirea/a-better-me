A low-to-high cable fly can train the pec major and front delt in a manner that is quite similar to incline dumbbell fly and incline press variations. In terms of shifting the emphasis to different muscle regions, lowering the cables also increases the angle for an incline fly or press. When positioned near hip level, the low-to-high cable fly likely results in a training effect that is similar to a low incline (e.g., near 30 degrees) fly for many individuals, while situating the cables near knee height likely results in a training effect that is more similar to a moderate incline (e.g., near 45 degrees) fly.
