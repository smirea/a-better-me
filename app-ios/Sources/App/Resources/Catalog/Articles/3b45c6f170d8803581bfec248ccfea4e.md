A cable triceps press functions as a hybrid between a triceps pushdown, where the shoulders are in a neutral position with the elbows by your sides, and an overhead triceps extension, where the shoulders are fully flexed with the elbows raised fully upward. Since the long head of the triceps lengthens as the angle of shoulder flexion increases, a cable triceps press will correspondingly train the long head of the triceps at longer lengths than a triceps pushdown but at shorter lengths than an overhead triceps extension.
