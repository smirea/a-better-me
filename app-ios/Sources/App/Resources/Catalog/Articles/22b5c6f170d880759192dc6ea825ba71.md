While reverse curls are often cited as training the brachialis more effectively than biceps curls with an underhand grip, evidence supporting this claim is lacking. The limited research that has measured EMG activity of the brachialis during resisted elbow flexion with different grips has not found the pronated grip to elicit greater brachialis EMG activity than a supinated or neutral grip. The biceps have been found to exhibit lower EMG activity when curling with a pronated grip than with a supinated grip, whereas a corresponding increase in brachialis EMG activity with a pronated grip has not yet been measured.
