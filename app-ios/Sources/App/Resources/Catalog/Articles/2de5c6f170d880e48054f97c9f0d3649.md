With the obvious exception of sitting as opposed to standing, the seated dumbbell lateral raise may be performed with a technique that is essentially identical to that of the standing dumbbell lateral raise. However, some people may be naturally inclined to perform the standing dumbbell lateral raise with a less strict technique that uses lower body muscles to generate momentum, assisting with dumbbell movement. Using momentum from other muscle groups to assist the shoulder muscles in moving the dumbbells will not necessarily reduce the hypertrophy stimulus to the shoulders compared with a stricter technique. However, if someone uses a stricter technique that does not involve generating momentum with additional muscles during seated dumbbell lateral raises, less weight will be required to complete the same number of reps as with a looser technique during standing dumbbell lateral raises.
