Based on muscle size data reported by Holzbaur et al. (2007), the wrist flexor muscles trained by this exercise constitute approximately half of the total forearm muscle volume. Nearly two-thirds of the total wrist flexor muscle volume is situated within the muscle bellies of the flexor digitorum superficialis and the flexor digitorum profundus, which together have a volume exceeding the volume of the upper arm’s biceps brachii. Besides flexing the wrist, these two flexor digitorum muscles flex the joints of the four long fingers and are the two strongest gripping muscles. Correspondingly, increasing the size and strength of these muscles through wrist curl variations may improve grip strength potential.
