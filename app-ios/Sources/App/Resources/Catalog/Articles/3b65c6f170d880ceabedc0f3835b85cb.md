Typical incline fly machines position the torso similarly to a dumbbell incline fly, but they train the pec major and front delt through the same plane of shoulder motion that a flat dumbbell fly could. Incline fly machines have handles that provide resistance that is directed backward and downward at an angle, in contrast to dumbbells that provide resistance directed purely downward due to gravity acting on them. The angle of the resistance that an incline fly machine provides aligns with the angle to which the bench is inclined, so the arm motion relative to the torso winds up being the same as what could be performed with a flat dumbbell fly.
