### **What to do if you are in between step heights for your step-down**

Depending on the equipment that is available to you, you may find that you are in between step heights relative to the rep range in which you wish to train for this exercise. In some situations, the height of the step you are currently using may not be sufficiently challenging, but the smallest increment by which you can increase the height results in the step being excessively tall. If you find yourself in this scenario, you can use the taller step and simply perform your step-down through the range of motion you can appropriately control without tapping the ground with your heel, or you can tap the ground with your toes pointed down (which would reduce the range of motion compared to if you tapped the ground with your heel). If you do not have access to a taller step, you can alternatively reach your free leg forward to increase the range of motion before your heel taps the ground, or you can progress the intensity by performing a weighted step-down variation.
