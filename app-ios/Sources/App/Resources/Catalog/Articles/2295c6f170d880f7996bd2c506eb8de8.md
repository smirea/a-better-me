The grip used for the dumbbell pullover has been successfully used by many people and helps you hold the dumbbell securely throughout the exercise. However, some people may not feel comfortable holding a weight above their head with this grip and may prefer to perform a pullover with the weight held differently. If you would personally prefer to do so, you can select from this database the plate pullover or barbell pullover (which can be performed with fixed weight bars and EZ curls bars if you wish to use a weight that is lighter than a standard unloaded barbell) to use a different grip to train the same muscles targeted by a dumbbell pullover.
