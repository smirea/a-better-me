As you pull the cable toward you, you can consciously think about pinching your shoulder blades back together in an effort to increase potential engagement of the trapezius and rhomboids, since these muscles function to retract (pull back and together) the shoulder blades. While focusing on doing so may have an effect, shoulder blade retraction is naturally coupled with end range shoulder horizontal extension, so your shoulder blade retractor muscles will be active during this exercise even if you do not focus on shoulder blade motion while performing it.
