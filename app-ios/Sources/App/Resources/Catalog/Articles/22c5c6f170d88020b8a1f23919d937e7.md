While it serves as a dynamic stretching exercise, the absence of external loads (e.g., dumbbells, kettlebells, barbells) precludes bodyweight Jefferson curls from meaningfully increasing strength and muscle size in able-bodied individuals. Nonetheless, trying out bodyweight Jefferson curls before loaded Jefferson curls can provide insight into how your back feels with this type of movement before considering whether higher intensity variations are appropriate for you at this time. Based on personal factors such as prior injuries, certain individuals may find the Jefferson curl movement to be quite uncomfortable with bodyweight alone, so it is sensible to find out how you, as an individual, feel while performing the bodyweight version before moving on to any loaded variations.
