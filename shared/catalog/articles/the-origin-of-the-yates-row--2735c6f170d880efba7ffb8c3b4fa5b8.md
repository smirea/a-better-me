The Yates row is named after Dorian Yates, widely recognized as one of the greatest bodybuilders of all time, with his crowning achievement being six consecutive Mr. Olympia titles from 1992 to 1997. Dorian’s total number of Mr. Olympia wins has only been exceeded by four bodybuilders in history, namely Arnold Schwarzenegger, Phil Heath, Lee Haney, and Ronnie Coleman. Yates received international acclaim for the remarkable size of his back, which he developed by using rows as a foundational component of his training routine. Dorian reported that he previously performed his barbell rows with an underhand grip but switched to an overhand grip after sustaining a biceps tendon rupture. While an underhand grip can promote greater biceps engagement, note that this does not mean that rowing with an underhand grip is inherently dangerous to the biceps.
