While simple from a technique standpoint, the single arm dead hang can be a challenging exercise that many people cannot perform. If this variation is too intense for your current level of strength, you can readily select the standard dead hang from this database, where you hang with both arms. Progressively increasing the time you perform for your sets of standard dead hangs can develop your grip strength and transfer to better single arm dead performance, but you can also perform variations that are more intense than the standard dead hang but less intense than the single arm dead hang. As an intermediate option, you can perform a dead hang with both arms, gripping lightly with one arm while the other supports more than half your bodyweight. Over time, you can progressively shift more of your bodyweight into one arm until you feel ready to dead hang with only one arm. You can also perform a weighted dead hang with both hands as you decide specifically how much more challenging you wish to make the movement based on how much weight you add to your pull-up/dip belt. As another way to intensify the standard dead hang, you can additionally add fat grip attachments to the bar and perform a dead hang with both arms, or you can wrap a thick towel around the bar and hang while gripping the towel. If you have very advanced levels of grip strength, you can apply the aforementioned strategies to progress beyond the conventional single arm dead hang by using a weight belt, fat grip attachments, or a towel while performing single arm dead hangs.
