You can perform a single arm dumbbell row with your free hand and knee supported on a bench, or you can perform it in the tripod (AKA three-point stance) with both feet on the ground and one hand supported on a bench (or other object). A strict or “cheating” technique can be used with either setup, but Kroc has personally used the standing tripod approach whenever demonstrating the technique for what is now known as the Kroc row. When striving to maximize how much weight can be lifted while using momentum to achieve that objective, the tripod stance may be more efficient than the kneeling position, which likely influenced Kroc’s preferred way to row.
