After completing a set of dumbbell presses, it is not uncommon for people to drop the dumbbells forward while still lying on the bench. If one end of the dumbbell contacts the ground before the other end, the dumbbell can ricochet off the ground and potentially injure someone nearby or damage equipment. While you can proceed as you wish if you are training alone with your own equipment in a home gym, dropping the dumbbells this way is inappropriate and strongly discouraged. While lying on the bench after the last rep, some individuals may set the dumbbells on the ground by straightening their elbows and allowing the dumbbells to pull their arms down below the bench until they contact the ground. Doing so can rapidly pull the shoulder into a position of end-range shoulder extension that can be quite stressful to the shoulder when using loads that are sufficiently challenging for a press, so this maneuver should also be avoided.
