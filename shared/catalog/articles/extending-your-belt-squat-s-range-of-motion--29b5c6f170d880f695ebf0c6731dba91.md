If you notice that the belt squat machine bottoms out when squatting down before you have reached the full range of motion you wish to perform regardless of how you set up, you have the option of elevating your feet on the platform. To do so, you can place a pair of sturdy elevated objects that will not slide, like deadlift blocks or bumper plates, onto the platform where you will stand. This approach may be more relevant for highly mobile lifters, along with shorter lifters, since machines are often designed for individuals within the average range for height.
