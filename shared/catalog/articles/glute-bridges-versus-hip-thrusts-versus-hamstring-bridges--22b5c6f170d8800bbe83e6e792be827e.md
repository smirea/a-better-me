Glute bridge, hamstring bridge, and hip thrust variations are similar in that they primarily train hip extension using similar techniques, but they employ different body positions. Glute bridges and hamstring bridges are both performed while lying down with your upper back contacting the ground, but hamstring bridges involve an extended or slightly flexed knee position, while glute bridges are performed with the heel closer to the buttock, resulting in a moderately high to high degree of knee flexion. Like glute bridges, hip thrusts are conventionally performed with a relatively flexed knee, but they differ in that the upper back rests on an elevated surface, such as a bench, which allows greater hip range of motion during the exercise.
