When performing a vertical pulling exercise with a fixed bar, the movement is known as a chin-up if your palms face backward, while it is known as a neutral grip pull-up if your palms face in toward each other. Because gymnastic rings permit your palms to rotate freely, a consensus does not exist in terms of what specifically constitutes a ring chin-up or a ring pull-up. This database describes a ring chin-up as finishing the lifting phase with an underhand grip and starting the pull with a neutral grip, while it describes a ring pull-up as starting the lifting phase with an overhand grip and typically finishing the pull with a neutral grip. While it is rational to view a ring chin-up as requiring you to have an underhand grip at some point during the rep, other valid interpretations of ring chin-ups and ring pull-ups exist beyond what is discussed in this database.
