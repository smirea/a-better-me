Even when using chalk, the load you can use or the distance you can complete with a given load will often be limited by grip strength during this loaded carry, so this exercise can be a viable means of training static grip strength. However, if you wish to increase the stimulus provided to muscles besides the forearm and hand muscles, you have the option of performing this carry with lifting straps. With the heavier loads or longer distances that straps typically facilitate, the exercise can be more challenging to your core and upper back muscles as they support your trunk and shoulder girdle in addition to your lower body muscles that stabilize your pelvis and propel you forward.
