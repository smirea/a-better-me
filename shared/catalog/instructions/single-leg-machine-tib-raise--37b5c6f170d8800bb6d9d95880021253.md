## Setting up
1. If your machine is not currently next to a seat, place it near a seat on which you can comfortably sit while your foot is on the machine’s foot platform.
    1. Having the seat be close enough to the machine where your knee can be bent by a bit less than 90 degrees once your foot is on the foot is commonly a comfortable position for this exercise.
2. Add weight plates of your desired load to the machine using plates with diameters that do not touch the ground.
    1. Most machines have loading sleeves that are close enough to the ground that plates larger than typical 10lb (or 5kg) plates may touch the ground and restrict your range of motion for the exercise.
3. Sit down on the seat, and place the sole of your working leg’s foot onto the machine’s foot platform.
    1. Keep your other foot in a comfortable position on the ground throughout your set.
    2. All the subsequent directions apply to your working leg.
4. Slide your foot forward on the platform until the top of your foot is snugly pressed against the foot pad and your ankle is aligned with the machine’s pivot points.
    1. On each side of the machine, the pivot point is the center point about which the platform rotates. When your ankle is aligned with the pivot points (such that a straight line can pass from one pivot point through the center of your ankle and to the other pivot point), the exercise will feel the most comfortable and natural.
5. If you did not already do so, allow the machine’s resistance to fully point your toes down away from you to assume the exercise’s starting position.
## Exercise technique
1. Fully pull your toes and the balls of your foot upward and back toward your shin by moving at your ankle.
    1. As the balls of your foot and toes lift up, your heel will correspondingly arc downward.
    2. How high you will be able to lift up will be affected by your ankle mobility, but people with normal ankle mobility should be able to finish with the balls of their feet and toes at least slightly higher than the bottoms of their heels.
    3. Throughout the set, keep the sole of your foot against the foot platform while the top of your foot remains against the foot pad.
    4. While motion occurs at your ankle, the rest of your body should remain stationary during this exercise.
2. Reverse the movement in a controlled manner by letting the balls of your foot and toes arc down away from your shin to lower the weight.
    1. Descend through the full range of motion your ankle mobility comfortably permits, which often results in a stretching sensation in the muscles on the front of your shin when your foot is fully pointed down with your toes and the balls of your foot lower than your heel.
    2. There is no need to perform this step with an extremely slow tempo, but you should avoid letting the weight abruptly yank your foot down.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set and are ready to slide your foot back off the platform before repeating the exercise with your other side.
## Description
This exercise can train the four muscle ankle dorsiflexor muscles on the front of your lower leg, the largest of which is the tibialis anterior.
