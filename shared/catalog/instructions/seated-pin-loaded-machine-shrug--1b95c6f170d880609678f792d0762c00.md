## Setting up
1. Adjust the machine’s seat to a height that situates its handles slightly below the level of your hands when seated with your arms resting at your sides.
2. After selecting your desired amount of weight with the machine’s loading pin, sit down facing away from the machine with your feet flat on the floor.
3. With extended elbows, lean forward by hinging at your hips until your hands are low enough to grasp the handles firmly with a neutral grip such that your palms are facing in toward each other.
    1. While leaning forward, keep your lower back near a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright, rather than meaningfully rounding your lower back into a slouched posture.
4. Sit upright by moving about your hips while continuing to keep your elbows extended and your lower back near a neutral position.
## Exercise technique
1. Shrug your shoulders upward through your available range of motion while keeping your elbows extended, which will lift the handles upward.
    1. While doing so, keep your neck in a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright and looking straight ahead.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your shoulders to descend in a controlled manner through your available range of motion, which will lower the handles.
    1. At the end of this motion, your shoulders should ideally be positioned a little lower than where they are normally situated while standing at rest due to the downward stretch that the weight can provide.
    2. If you lose resistance from the machine (from its mobile arms contacting its base beams) when your shoulders are fully depressed, you can set the handles down, elevate the seat height, and resume your set with resistance throughout the full range of motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise primarily trains your upper traps and levator scapulae.
