## Setting up
1. Adjust a cable station’s pulley to its highest level, connect a rope attachment to the cable, and select your desired weight with the loading pin.
2. Stand in front of the cable station facing the rope.
3. Reach up and grasp each end of the rope with your little fingers and adjacent aspects of your hands contacting the flat surfaces of the attachment’s rubber stoppers. Wrap your thumbs over the outside of your other fingers.
    1. In this position, your palms should be facing inward.
4. Take a step backward and slightly lean forward by hinging at your hips.
    1. Maintain this slight torso lean throughout the set.
5. Pull your upper arms down and back until they are at your sides, with your elbows close to fully bent, so your hands are close together in front of your chest.
    1. Maintain this upper arm position throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Push the rope downward and spread it apart by extending your elbows until they are straightened.
    1. At the end of this motion, your hands should be near the outer aspects of your thighs, and they should be farther apart from each other compared to when you were in the starting position.
2. Reverse the motion by bending your elbows to raise the rope in a controlled manner as your hands move closer to each other until you have returned to the starting position.
    1. Strive to have your elbows as close to fully bent as you can by the time you have finished this motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively develop the triceps, including the long head, which is not trained particularly well by compound pressing exercises.
