## Setting up
1. With a cable station positioned to its lowest height, select your weight with the loading pin, and clip in a straight bar attachment or a multi-grip curl bar attachment.
2. While standing directly in front of the middle of the bar, squat down with extended elbows to grasp the bar firmly with approximately a shoulder-width grip as your palms are directed back toward you (i.e., an overhand grip).
    1. When squatting down, keep your lower back near a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright, rather than meaningfully rounding your lower back.
3. By extending your hips and knees, stand upright with the bar while continuing to keep your elbows extended and your lower back near a neutral position.
    1. When upright, you should feel resistance from the cable, so take a step backward if you do not initially feel any resistance.
## Exercise technique
1. Shrug your shoulders upward through your available range of motion while keeping your elbows extended, which will lift the bar upward.
    1. While doing so, keep your neck in a neutral position, like you have when you are normally standing upright and looking straight ahead.
2. Reverse the motion by allowing your shoulders to descend in a controlled manner through your available range of motion, which will lower the bar.
    1. At the end of this motion, your shoulders should ideally be positioned a little lower than where they are normally situated while standing at rest due to the downward stretch that the weight can provide.
    2. If you lose resistance from the cable machine when your shoulders are fully depressed, you can take a step backward and resume your set with resistance throughout the full range of motion.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise primarily trains your upper traps and levator scapulae.
