## Setting up
1. Prepare the barbell with your desired load by adding weight plates to it or leaving the barbell unloaded, which is recommended if performing this exercise for the first time.
    1. If you have access to one, you can also use a fixed-weight straight barbell, which may be lighter than a typical 20kg (44lb) barbell that has sleeves on which to add weight plates.
2. Stand close in front of the middle of the bar with a hip-width stance and straight elbows before hinging at your hips while bending your knees until your hands are level with the bar.
    1. Keep your lower back near flat, rather than meaningfully rounding it while doing so.
3. Firmly grasp the bar with a shoulder-width underhand grip such that your palms are facing forward away from you.
4. By extending (straightening) your hips and knees, stand upright with the bar while continuing to keep your elbows straight.
    1. Once upright, the bar should be directly in front of your thighs.
    2. Maintain this upright position with straight elbows throughout your set.
## Exercise technique
1. Lift the bar upward by curling your wrists through their available range of motion while keeping your arms stationary.
    1. All motion should come from your wrists while you keep the rest of your body stationary.
    2. Your palms should be angled upward once you have fully curled your wrists.
    3. When performing this exercise appropriately, you should feel tension in the muscles on the palm side of your forearms.
2. Reverse the motion by lowering the bar in a controlled manner until you have returned to the starting position with your palms directed forward away from you.
3. Alternate between steps 1 and 2 until you have completed your set.
## Description
This exercise can effectively train the six wrist flexor muscles on the palm side of your forearm.
