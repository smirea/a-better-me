# MacroFactor (nutrition): research spec

Source: MacroFactor 5.8.9 (`/Applications/MacroFactor.app`, bundle id `com.sbs.diet`, process "Runner"), a Flutter iPad build running on macOS, logged into the owner's account. Studied on 2026-10-26. This is the nutrition counterpart of [`../mf-workouts/README.md`](../mf-workouts/README.md) and uses the same structure.

Companion files written from the app bundle by a sibling unit: [`app-strings.md`](app-strings.md) (every UI string), [`data-model.md`](data-model.md) (the persisted model), and [`nutrients.md`](nutrients.md) (the nutrient catalog with ids, units, and default goals). This file covers behavior and layout; read those for exhaustive strings and fields.

**Layout caveat.** The macOS window is fixed at 788 x 1083 pt and cannot be resized, so every screenshot shows the tablet layout: a left navigation rail instead of a bottom tab bar, and More as a split view. On iPhone, assume the five destinations sit in a bottom tab bar (Dashboard, Food Log, center `+`, Strategy, More). Bottom sheets taller than the window are clipped at the bottom edge and do not scroll, so a few long option lists were only partly visible; those are listed in the backlog.

**Screenshots** are in the `screens` folder: JPEG, about 728 x 1000, dark theme unless named `theme-light-*`. **The owner's data is boxed out in purple** on every screenshot that showed it (weights, intake, dates, food names, the profile name). Screens that showed only today's empty log or test data are unredacted.

**Tools** (in `tools/`):

- `mfinput.swift` posts mouse, scroll, and keyboard CGEvents to the window. Coordinates are in screenshot space (1000 px tall). It brings MacroFactor to the front through System Events, refuses to post if MacroFactor is not frontmost, and hands focus and the cursor back afterwards.
- `mf` wraps it: `mf click x y`, `mf scroll x y dy`, `mf drag x1 y1 x2 y2`, `mf type text`, `mf key code`, `mf shot`, `mf save <name> [x1,y1,x2,y2 ...]` (save the last capture with boxes painted over owner data).
- `redact.swift` paints those boxes.
- `export-schema.py` prints an export's sheets and columns with inferred types, never values.

## 1. Visual design language

The app shares its design system with MacroFactor Workouts (same fonts, card shapes, sheets, and chart components), so everything in the Workouts spec §1 applies. Nutrition-specific additions:

- **Theme.** System, Light, Dark (More > Theme, three preview tiles; light samples `theme-light-01-more.jpg`, `theme-light-02-food-log.jpg`, `theme-light-03-dashboard.jpg`). Dark: app background ~#141414, header bands and cards ~#1F1F1F, input and pill fills ~#333, primary text white, secondary ~#9A9A9A.
- **Nutrient colors** (sampled from the dark theme):

  | Role                                                   | Color          | Used for                                                            |
  | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
  | Calories / energy                                      | blue #68A0FA   | calorie bars, gauge arc, bars in Energy Balance, Food Logging habit |
  | Protein                                                | salmon #FF8660 | protein bars, chips, protein amino acids group                      |
  | Fat                                                    | yellow #FFD05E | fat bars, fat breakdown group                                       |
  | Carbs                                                  | green #5EB987  | carb bars, carb breakdown group                                     |
  | Vitamins                                               | lilac #B28DF7  | vitamin group swatches                                              |
  | Minerals                                               | pink #FF7EA2   | mineral group swatches                                              |
  | Other (alcohol, caffeine, cholesterol, choline, water) | blue #669FFA   | "Other" group swatches; "Other" in the stacked Macros chart         |

- **Insight colors.** Expenditure salmon-orange #F9845D (line, plus a translucent "flux range" band). Weight trend lilac (trend line light lilac, scale-weight line darker purple). Goal Progress and Weigh-In habit green #5EBB89. Tutorial cards are tinted with the insight's color (brown-red for Expenditure, dark green for Scale Weight and Goal Progress).
- **Calorie glyph.** Calories are written with a custom flame-like glyph after the number ("149ઠ" in the screenshots), not "kcal", in compact rows. Macros use letter suffixes: "7P 5F 20C".
- **Target bars.** Every nutrient bar is drawn on a track with a small tick at the target. A shaded band in the nutrient's color marks the goal range (floor to ceiling). The filled part is the amount consumed. A nutrient with only a ceiling (sugars) has the band from zero to the ceiling; one with only a floor (fiber) has the band from the floor to the end of the track. See "About Nutrient Goals" in §3.6.
- **Charts.** Same chart shell as Workouts: a header with the period's summary values, a fit-to-data button, a right-side axis, range pills 1W / 1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y / All, a D / W / M averaging dropdown ("Chart Averaging": D - Daily, W - Weekly, M - Monthly), and a legend bar under the chart. Bars for intake, lines for targets and trends, dotted lines for expenditure and targets on bar charts.
- **Day pills.** Rounded capsules with the day number over a two-letter weekday ("26 / Mo"). Today is filled gray with a dot. In the expanded calendar, a day's outline becomes a blue progress ring that fills with that day's calories as a fraction of target.
- **Sheets** have a grab handle, X at the left, a bold centered title with optional subtitle, and a hairline. Option sheets list rows with radio buttons and a one-line description. Date pickers are a month grid sheet ("Select Date") with month arrows and a "Go to Today" button; future months are disabled in month pickers ("Select Month").
- **Keypad.** Food quantities use a custom keypad, not the system keyboard: digits, ".", "/" (fractions) and a space key (mixed numbers like "1 1/2"), backspace, a keyboard-switch key, and a white Done key. Above it are the quantity field with its unit and a row of unit chips with a ⇅ toggle. See `log-10-edit-entry-sheet.jpg`.
- **Tutorials.** Detail screens carry a dismissible tutorial card ("Dismiss Tutorial", "Next"). The graduation-cap icon in a detail header opens the matching article on `help.macrofactorapp.com` in the browser. Both are onboarding; cut.

### 3.2 Expenditure detail

`dashboard-14-expenditure-detail.jpg`, `dashboard-15-expenditure-insights.jpg`, `dashboard-16-expenditure-sources.jpg`

- Header: **Average** (kcal) for the range, **Difference** (last minus first in the range), the date range, fit button.
- Chart: the expenditure line with a translucent **Flux Range** band (the estimate's uncertainty) and **Holding** markers: hollow squares on days when the estimate was held constant.
- **Insights & Data**:
  - **Expenditure Changes**: 3-day, 7-day, 14-day, 30-day, 90-day rows, each with a sparkline, the change in kcal, and "→ No Change", "↗ Increase", or "↘ Decrease".
  - **Current Expenditure** tile: "The latest estimate of your daily energy expenditure based on your weight trend and nutrition data."
  - **Current Strategy** tile, for example **Holding**: "Holding safeguards your expenditure estimate against insufficient data. Updating requires at least 3 days of nutrition data and 1 day of weight data per 7-day period."
- **Data Sources**: Nutrition Data Manager (Manage Data) and Scale Weight (Manage Data).
- A month-grouped list of daily values ("2207 kcal / Mon, Oct 26" with a "Holding" tag).

**Algorithm** (MacroFactor help, "Expenditure"): energy balance solved for output. Expenditure = intake − change in stored energy, where stored-energy change comes from the rate of trend-weight change and an assumed fat/lean composition that depends on the rate (slower loss or faster gain is assumed to be more fat, which is more energy-dense). It is adherence-neutral: it uses what was eaten, not what was planned. Partial logging lowers the estimate, which is why a day can be flagged incomplete (§3.3). Exact constants are not published; see the backlog.

### 3.4 Weight Trend detail

`dashboard-19-weight-trend-detail.jpg`, `dashboard-20-weight-trend-insights.jpg`

- Chart: **Scale Weight** (dark purple, smoothed line through weigh-ins) and **Trend Weight** (light lilac with dots). After the last weigh-in, the trend line keeps easing toward the last scale value day by day (seen with the V1 algorithm, which the owner uses; see §9).
- **Weight Changes**: 3-, 7-, 14-, 30-, 90-day rows with sparkline, change in lb, and Increase / Decrease / No Change.
- Tiles: **Current Weight** ("Our estimate of your true weight after smoothing out day-to-day fluctuations."), **Weekly Weight Change** (lb per week, "Your typical weekly rate of weight loss over the past three weeks."), **Energy Deficit** (kcal per day, "…based on your rate of weight loss over the past three weeks."), **30-Day Projection** ("Your projected weight in 30 days if your current rate of weight loss is met.").
- Derived values are consistent with: energy deficit ≈ weekly change × 3500 / 7 kcal per lb, and projection = current trend + weekly change × 30 / 7.
- Data Sources: Scale Weight. Then a daily list of trend values with a change arrow.

Help article: the trend is a moving average weighted toward recent weigh-ins, with missing days filled by linear interpolation, recalculated on each weigh-in.

## 4. Food Log

All `foodlog-*` screenshots, named per item below.

There are **no meals**. The log is a single **timeline of hours**: every entry has a timestamp, and entries group under their hour. Meal names, meal counts, and meal-based grouping do not exist anywhere in the app or the export.

**Header** (`foodlog-01-today-timeline.jpg`):

- Title "TODAY", or the date ("FRI, JUL 31") on other days, plus three round buttons: **calendar**, **sliders** (Food Log settings, §9.2), **⋮** (More).
- **Week strip** of day pills. It scrolls freely (Daily Scroll) or by week (Weekly Swipe). Future days are shown and selectable. On a past day a white "›" capsule at the right end jumps back to today.
- **Macro summary**, two pages with dots: page 1 "ઠ 0 / 1600, P 0 / 170, F 0 / 48, C 0 / 170" with thin bars; page 2 "1600 left", "P 170 left" … with full-color bars (`foodlog-02-summary-remaining.jpg`). With Show Overages on, remaining goes negative ("P −12 left"). Tapping it opens **Nutrition Overview** (below).
- Scrolling the timeline collapses the strip so only the title and summary stay pinned.

**Calendar** (`foodlog-06-calendar-expanded.jpg`): the calendar button expands the strip into a continuous scrolling grid of day pills, nine per row, months on alternating background shades with a floating month label ("Dec", "Jan '27"). Each logged day's pill outline is a blue progress ring proportional to calories against target. A round "⌄" button returns to today. Tapping a day opens it.

**Timeline** (`foodlog-07-day-with-entries.jpg`, `foodlog-10-today-test-entries.jpg`):

- One row per hour in the configured range (default 8 AM to 9 PM; hours with entries outside the range still show). Each hour is a gray pill ("2 PM"); the current hour has a dot. Next to it a round **+** adds food at that hour (or long-press the pill, per settings).
- Hours with entries show **hourly macro totals** on the right: "510 ઠ 33 P 18 F 56 C" with letter badges.
- **Food tile**: an emoji-style icon (recipes show a composite of their ingredients' icons), the name ("ZZ Test Food By ZZ Brand"), "200ઠ 12P 8F 20C • 1 portion", the timestamp ("2:48") in the gutter, and a pencil that opens the editor.
- **Notes** section under the timeline: "+" adds a note; "1 / 5" shows the limit of five notes per day. A note card has a title, body, time, and a color dot (`foodlog-13-notes.jpg`). The note editor (`foodlog-11-new-note.jpg`, `foodlog-12-note-colors.jpg`) is a full-height sheet: X, a palette button (red, blue, green, yellow, purple tabs), the date, a ✓, a "Note Name" title and a body. ✓ requires a title. An existing note adds a trash button with a "Delete Note" confirmation.

**Select mode** (`foodlog-08-select-mode.jpg`): tapping a tile (not its pencil) enters selection. Each entry gets a check circle in the gutter and the hour pill of the selection turns white. A bottom bar shows "✕ Select · N Foods" and actions:

- 1 food: Edit, Copy, Move, Remove, Modify Timestamp.
- 2+ foods: View, Copy, Move, Remove, Modify Timestamp, **Create Recipe** (turns the selection into a recipe).
- **Copy** and **Move** switch the bar to targets (`foodlog-09-copy-options.jpg`): To Now, To Today, To Tomorrow, To Dates (multi-date), To Date & Time, **Paste Inline** (each hour's + becomes a paste button), Multi-Paste. The bar scrolls horizontally; it could not be scrolled further under automation.
- Remove asks "Remove Foods · 3 food entries will be removed from the timeline. Are you sure you want to continue? This action cannot be undone." (`dialog-remove-foods.jpg`).

**⋮ More** (`foodlog-05-more-menu.jpg`): Nutrition Overview, Copy Day, Select All, Clear Day, and two toggles, **Hide Food Details** (collapses tiles to names) and **Hide Empty Hours**.

**Nutrition Overview** (`foodlog-03-nutrition-overview.jpg`, `foodlog-04-nutrition-overview-contributors.jpg`): tabs Today, 1 Week, 1 Month, 3 Months, 1 Year. Every visible nutrient in groups (Calories, Macros, Carb Breakdown, Fat Breakdown, Protein Breakdown, Vitamins, Minerals, Other), each a row with "value / target", percent, and a target bar, or "No Target". A **Contributors** toggle (Today tab) adds "Top 3 Contributors" under each nutrient with a chevron to the full list. Longer tabs show daily averages.

**Not present:** water tracking as its own log (water is a nutrient, and Siri has a "Log Water" intent that quick-adds water), a "mark day complete" button (completeness is set in the Nutrition Data Manager, §3.3, and asked during check-in), and fasting outside the data manager and check-in.

## 5. Logging flows

All logging happens in one full-height **logger** sheet (`log-01-logger-search-latest.jpg`). Its top banner (configurable, §9.2) shows:

- X (exit; asks "Exit the Plate? Foods you added to your plate will be cleared." with Exit and Stay when the plate is not empty, `dialog-exit-plate.jpg`),
- a **time chip** ("2 PM", or "2 PM / Jul 31" for another day) that opens **Select Date and Time** (`log-05-select-date-time.jpg`): a wheel of days (Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow, dates) × hour × AM/PM, with buttons "Select Date and Time", "Log to Date and Time", Cancel. Hour granularity only; minutes come from the current time.
- remaining-after-plate bars for the chosen nutrients ("1350 left", "150 P left"),
- the **plate**: the icons of foods added so far ("+3" when overflowing). Tapping it expands **Your Plate** (`log-04-your-plate.jpg`): each item with a quantity chip, a **Plate | Day** nutrition toggle, "Show all nutrients", and **Log Foods**.
- ↓ collapses the logger to the plate view, ↑ restores it.

**Tabs:** Scan, Search, AI, Quick Add, Library, Describe. The default is set by how the logger was opened.

**Search** (`log-02-search-results.jpg`), network for everything but history: an empty query shows **Latest** (recent foods). Typing shows groups **From History**, **Common** (generic foods, "See 15 More"), **Branded**, and Open Food Facts when enabled. Matched text is bold. Rows: icon, name ("Banana, Fresh", "… By Brand"), "105ઠ 1P 0F 27C • 1 medium – 7" to 7 7/8" long", and a round **+** that adds the default serving to the plate without leaving search (Multi-Add). Tapping the row opens food detail. The bottom field has a **Log Foods** button.

**Food detail** (`log-03-food-detail.jpg`, with Optimize for Speed):

- Name, big Calories, Protein / Fat / Carbs with a colored chip showing each macro's share of calories ("3%", "94%").
- Action buttons, which depend on the food kind: database food: To Custom, Favorite. Custom food: Edit, Share, Favorite, Delete, Duplicate (`food-07-custom-food-detail.jpg`). Recipe: Prepare, Edit, Share, Favorite, Delete, Explode, To Custom, Duplicate (`recipe-06-detail.jpg`). An item already on the plate: Remove, To Custom.
- **Impact on Targets**: four rings (Calories, Protein, Fat, Carbs) with the percent of today's target this serving uses.
- The full nutrient breakdown, as in Nutrition Overview.
- The **quantity keypad**: the field shows "1 | large – 8" to 8 7/8" long • 136 g". Unit chips: the two favorite weight units (g, oz) and two favorite volume units (ml, cup, only when the food has a density), a divider, then the food's own servings (large, medium, slice, serving, lb …). ⇅ swaps. Keys: digits, ".", "/", space, ⌫, **Log Foods** (log the plate now), **Add** (add to plate and return).
- The plate preview in the banner updates live as the quantity changes.

**Quick Add** (`log-06-quick-add.jpg`): Energy (kcal or kJ dropdown) with a live helper "Macro sum is 250 kcal" (4/9/4 kcal per gram, alcohol 7), Protein, Fat, Carbs, Alcohol (g), Name (optional). **Quick Add** puts it on the plate as "1 serving" (exported with a nominal 100 g weight); **Log Foods** logs the plate.

**Describe** (`log-07-describe.jpg`, `log-08-describe-result.jpg`, network): a 500-character "Describe your meal" field marked **Deprecated**, and **Find Foods**. "one apple and two boiled eggs" put Apple (1 medium) and Boiled Eggs (2 large) on the plate as database foods with servings.

**AI** (`log-09-ai-describe.jpg`, `log-11-ai-snap.jpg`, network and camera): a **Snap | Describe** toggle. Snap is a full camera view with a shutter, an upload button, and a flash toggle. Describe takes up to 3 photos (Capture or upload) plus a 500-character description, then **Analyze**. Not run (needs a real meal photo).

**Scan** (`log-12-scan-barcode.jpg`, `log-13-barcode-result.jpg`, camera): a **Barcode | Label** toggle, a keyboard button for **Manual Barcode** ("Enter the barcode number", Lookup Barcode), and a flash toggle. A found barcode opens the food detail. Label mode scans a nutrition label into a new custom food (not run: needs a physical label).

**Library** (`log-14-library-recipes.jpg`, `log-15-library-foods.jpg`, `log-16-library-favorites-empty.jpg`): **Recipes | Foods | ♥** segments, a **+** (Create Recipe or Create Food), a sort ("Sort": A–Z, Z–A, Created, Modified), and a bottom "Filter recipes" field. Favorites is empty for the owner: "No favorites yet. Search for a food, open the Food Detail view, and press the heart to save it as your favorite." Its header icon is a sort/reorder control.

**Editing a logged entry** (`log-10-edit-entry-sheet.jpg`, `log-17-edit-entry-test-food.jpg`, `log-18-edit-entry-time.jpg`): the pencil opens the same detail sheet over the timeline, with the entry's own time chip ("2:48 PM", opens an hour : minute : AM/PM wheel with Done and Cancel) and Done at the top. Actions: Remove, Favorite, Share, Duplicate, plus Explode and To Custom for recipes. Changing the quantity or unit and pressing Done updates the entry. There is no meal field.

**Explode** (`foodlog-14-after-explode.jpg`) replaces a logged recipe with its ingredients scaled to the logged amount ("Banana, Fresh · 0.5 large"), immediately and without confirmation.

**Network and hardware:** Search beyond history, barcode lookup, Describe, AI, Import from Link, and recipe/food AI import need the network. Snap, barcode, and label scanning need the camera. Quick Add, Library, history, and editing work offline.

### 7.1 New Program

A full-screen wizard with a progress bar ("NEW PROGRAM · 1 Set program preferences"). Step 1, **program style** (`strategy-03-program-style.jpg`):

- **Coached**: "We'll design your Calorie targets and macro program based on your goal and preferences."
- **Collaborative**: "Set your own macro targets and we'll adjust your Calorie budget based on your goal and progress."
- **Manual**: "Set your own macro and Calorie targets using manual entry."

**Coached** asks, one screen each:

1. **Diet** (`strategy-04-diet.jpg`): Balanced ("Standard distribution of carbs and fat"), Low-fat, Low-carb, Keto ("Carbs will be very restricted to allow for higher fat intake").
2. **Calorie floor** (`strategy-05-calorie-floor.jpg`): **Standard Floor** ("never go below 1361 kcal" for the owner; help says the standard floor is 1200 kcal, so it is individualized) or **Low Floor** (907 kcal, "proceed with caution", two thirds of the standard floor).
3. **Training** (`strategy-06-training.jpg`): None or Relaxed Activity, Lifting, Cardio, Cardio & Lifting. Mainly moves the protein recommendation.
4. **Calorie distribution** (`strategy-07-distribution.jpg`): Distribute Evenly or **Shift Calorie Targets**, then **"Which days would you like to have higher Calorie targets?"**, a Monday-to-Sunday checklist (`strategy-08-high-days.jpg`).
5. **Protein** (`strategy-09-protein.jpg`): Low, Moderate (default), High, Extra High.
6. A generating animation (`strategy-10-generating.jpg`, orbiting macro-colored planets, "Cranking up the success likelihood.").
7. **"Your macro program is ready"** (`strategy-11-program-ready.jpg`, `strategy-12-program-extra-high-protein.jpg`): the 7-day chart, then **How was your program designed?** in five numbered steps: Estimated Expenditure, Average Target ("Your goal is to lose weight at a rate of 0.76% of body weight per week, so your daily average Calorie goal should be around … kcal"), Calorie Shift, Target Protein (g/lb), Diet Type. **What's Next** explains weekly monitoring. **Done** saves.

Measured calculations (one profile, Balanced, lifting, Fri and Sat high days):

- Average daily target = expenditure − goal rate × body weight × ~500 kcal per lb per week (3,500 kcal per lb / 7).
- **Calorie shift** keeps the weekly total: 5 normal days at 1421 plus 2 high days at 1564 equals 7 × 1462. The high days get about +10%.
- **Protein** g per lb of body weight: Low 0.63, Moderate 0.77, High 0.93, Extra High 1.08. The grams are the same on every day.
- **Balanced** fat is about 29–30% of each day's calories; carbs take the remainder.
- **Fat floor** about 44 g: when protein plus the minimum fat leaves too little room, the program says "we had to switch you over to a ketogenic program. If you'd like a higher-carb program, please reduce your goal rate of weight loss."
- Low-carb caps carbs at ~30% of calories or under 200 g (help article).

**Collaborative** (`strategy-13-collaborative-editor.jpg`, `strategy-14-collaborative-rebalance.jpg`) goes straight to an editor: the 7-day chart with day buttons M to S, then for the selected day **Calorie targets** ("set between 0 and 2865 kcal", about 1.3 × expenditure), a **Protein** slider in g per lb ("Set between 0.0 and 1.85 g per lb of body weight") with a numeric field, and a **Carb to Fat Ratio** slider (percent of remaining calories given to carbs, end labels in g per lb of body weight). **Reset All Days**, and a **lock** that pins a day. Changing one day's calories spreads the opposite change evenly over the unlocked days so the weekly budget stays fixed (Tuesday +538 → each of five unlocked days −108; Monday locked).

**Manual** asks **"Do you want to follow the same targets all week?"** (Same targets all week, Different targets per day; `strategy-15-manual-same-or-different.jpg`), then **"Set your targets for the week"**: Energy (kcal), Protein, Fat, Carbs (`strategy-16-manual-targets.jpg`). Nothing checks that macros add up to energy (the owner's targets do not). **Edit Program** on a manual program opens "Edit Manual Program" with the same four fields (`strategy-21-edit-manual-program.jpg`).

### 7.2 Goals

**New Goal** (`strategy-17-new-goal-intro.jpg`, `strategy-18-goal-type.jpg`, `strategy-19-goal-weight-rate.jpg`, `strategy-20-goal-summary.jpg`): "NEW GOAL · 1 Set new goal · 2 Set new program", so a new goal always continues into the New Program wizard.

1. **"What is your goal?"**: Lose Weight, Maintain Weight, Gain Weight.
2. Weight and rate screen: header tiles **initial daily budget** and **projected end date**; **"What is your target weight?"** a horizontal ruler you drag (starts at the current weight); **"What is your target goal rate?"** a slider with a green recommended zone and a label that changes: **Slower**, **Standard (Recommended)**, **Faster (Use Caution)** (with a warning knob). ### 7.3 Weekly check-in

The owner is on a Manual program, so no check-in was offered and its screens were not seen. What the settings and help say (More > Strategy, `more-14-strategy.jpg`):

- **Check-In Day**: a weekday (owner: Sunday). Coached and Collaborative programs recompute the weekly calorie budget from the latest expenditure on that day.
- **Fast Check-In**: "Skips all optional coaching modules".
- **Coaching Modules** that can appear in a check-in: Introduction (Default), **Partial Logging** (mark days that were incompletely logged so they are excluded), **Weigh-In** (prompt for a weight), **Fasting** (mark fasting days), **Logging Break** (declare a break), **Program Update** (Default; shows the new targets).
- Widgets warn "But that's based on a plan last updated … Open MacroFactor to check-in and refresh your goals."
- The export's "Nutrition Program Settings" sheet has one row per program update (date, weekday, calories, macros, expenditure, daily average, weight, mode), so each check-in snapshots the targets.

### 7.4 Per-nutrient goals

## 11. Widgets, Siri, shortcuts, and the share extension

From the bundle (`Runner.app/Metadata.appintents`, `PlugIns/`):

- **Widgets** (`mfwidgetsExtension.appex`, WidgetKit): CurrentAndRemainingWidget (`mfremainingwidget`), NutrientFocusWidget (`mfcenternutrientwidget`, one nutrient), NutrientCollectionWidget (`mfnutrientcollectionwidget`, up to four nutrients: "Second Nutrient", "Fourth Nutrient"), QuickActionsWidget (configurable quick actions), and a Control Center control, QuickActionControlWidget. Lock Screen accessory families are supported. Widgets have a theme option (System, Light, Dark) and a "plan last updated … check in" reminder string. There is also a watch nutrition widget deep link.
- **Quick action targets** (`QuickActionsAppEnum`, also the "Screen" intent): search, barcode, AI, weight, dashboard, foodlog, libraryRecipes, libraryFoods, libraryFavorites, quickAdd.
- **App Intents (Shortcuts and Siri)**:
  - Log Recent Food (foods from history, ask to update serving sizes, servings / volume / weight, date)
  - Log Weight (weight, body fat %, ask for body fat)
  - Quick Add (energy, protein, fat, carbs, a meal name, and every micronutrient as an optional parameter)
  - Log by JSON (a JSON payload, confirm parsing, date) and Add LLM Formatting Prompt (wraps a meal description with instructions so an LLM produces that JSON; detail levels simple, commonFoodLabel, allNutrients)
  - Log Water (volume, name), Log Beer (volume, % strength, name)
  - Set Serving Count / Set Serving Volume / Set Serving Weight (adjust a recent food's serving)
  - Nutrient Remaining (one nutrient), Macros Remaining (macros and pinned nutrients), Get Nutrition State (today's consumption and progress for a nutrient as JSON; calculation consumed, remainingToMinimum, remainingToTarget, remainingToMaximum)
  - Screen (open the app to a quick-action target)
- **Siri phrases**: "MacroFactor log food", "MacroFactor macros", "MacroFactor Scan Barcode", "MacroFactor Search", "MacroFactor log beer", "MacroFactor log water", "MacroFactor quick add", "MacroFactor weight", "MacroFactor ${nutrient} remaining".
- **Share extension** "Open in MacroFactor" (Safari action, web pages and URLs only): sends the page HTML, title, and URL to `com.sbs.diet://link.macrofactorapp.com/recipe?link=…&title=…` to import a recipe from a web page.
- **Nutrient ids** in the intents use USDA nutrient numbers (203-style: 291 fiber, 269 sugars, 307 sodium, 301 calcium …) plus `k`, `p`, `f`, `c`, and `nc` (net carbs). See [`nutrients.md`](nutrients.md).

## Backlog and open questions

- **Quick Export time frames** stop at "Last Month": the sheet is the same height in portrait and landscape, so it is sized to its five options rather than clipped. The **itemized food log** for all time comes from Granular Export's Logs section ("Export Food Log", All Time, a CSV with the Food Log columns), found on 2026-10-26 after this spec; the rest of this bullet predates it. Everything else is covered by the Granular Export. To get the full food log, run More > Data Export > Quick Export with the longest range on the phone, or export month by month there.
- **Weekly check-in flow**: not reachable; the owner runs a Manual program and no check-in is pending. Its modules are listed in §7.3 from settings only.
- **Coached program constants**: the expenditure, weight-trend, and V1/V2/V3 algorithms are not published. The help says expenditure is intake minus stored-energy change from the trend weight, with a fat-fraction assumption that depends on the rate. Protein levels, fat share, floor, and shift sizes were measured for one profile only (§7.1); other diets (low-fat, low-carb, keto) and other weights were not sampled.
- **Weight trend smoothing**: V1 and V2 formulas unknown. Observed: interpolation across gaps and an asymptotic approach to the last weigh-in.
- **Goal rate bounds**: the slider's minimum (0.2 lb/wk) and the edges of the "Standard" zone were sampled roughly; the maximum was not reached.
- **Maintain and Gain goals** were not walked through.
- **Label scan, AI Snap, AI Describe, Import from Link, Import with AI** were not run (camera or real input needed). Describe (deprecated) was run once.
- **Copy / Move bar**: items after "Multi-Paste" could not be scrolled into view; "To Dates" and "Paste Inline" were not executed.
- **Compare All Metrics, Compare Before & After, progress photo gallery**: not opened (needs two entries; the gallery shows personal photos).
- **Period tracking**: off for the owner; not explored.
- **Nutrient bar scale**: the target tick sits at ~59% of the bar for most nutrients, but a day at 100% of the protein target drew the fill at ~48%. The bar's maximum is unclear.
- **Food database**: MacroFactor's own database (Common and Branded) plus optional Open Food Facts. A replacement source for search and barcode lookup is an open decision (Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central, or both).
- **Siri sub-settings** (Always Ask For, Energy Input) were not opened.
- **Health**: which types MacroFactor reads vs writes could not be seen on the Mac build.
- **Widgets**: listed from the bundle only; their layouts were not rendered.
- The window is fixed at 788 x 1083 pt, so no phone-width layout and no bottom tab bar were seen.
