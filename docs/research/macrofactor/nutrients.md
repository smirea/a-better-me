# MacroFactor nutrients

The nutrients MacroFactor 5.8.9 tracks, their ids, units, names, grouping, and how their goals are set.
Labels: **found (bundle)** means a native table, App Intents metadata, or the Dart snapshot; **found (web)** cites a URL; **inferred** is our reading.

## The list

**Found (bundle)**: MacroFactor tracks 56 nutrients. The table joins four native sources on one id:

- `Metadata.appintents/extract.actionsdata`, enum `NutrientAppEnum`: the order, title, synonyms, and SF Symbol.
- `PlugIns/mfwidgetsExtension.appex/Base.lproj/Intents.intentdefinition`, enum `Nutrients`: the widget code (`m` + id) and the widget's name.
- The widget's `en.lproj/Localizable.strings` labels such as `Folate (mcg)`: the unit.
- `StrongerModels_StrongerModels.bundle/en.lproj/Localizable.strings`: four name forms, keyed `<Name>_COMPLETE`, `_SENTENCE`, `_SHORT`, `_MINI`. Complete is the full name, Sentence the in-text name, Short and Mini the chart and ring labels.

Rerun `python3 -I tools/bundle-nutrients.py` to refresh the table; it rewrites only the block between the markers.

## Ids

- **Found (bundle)**: numeric ids are USDA nutrient numbers from SR Legacy and FoodData Central (`404` thiamin, `606` saturated fat, `851` ALA). `en.lproj/Intents.strings` spells the mapping out as keys (`"404" = "Thiamine (B1)"`).
- **Found (bundle)**: macros use letters: `k` calories, `p` protein, `f` fat, `c` carbs, `nc` net carbs. `901` (omega-3 total) and `902` (omega-6 total) are MacroFactor's own ids, outside the USDA scheme.
- **Found (bundle)**: Vitamin A is `320` (RAE, mcg) and Vitamin D is `328` (mcg). `Intents.strings` also keeps the older IU-based ids `318` (Vitamin A) and `324` (Vitamin D) as labels; they are not in the current enum.
- **Found (bundle)**: Shortcuts' Quick Add takes one parameter per nutrient in camel case: `energy`, `carbs`, `protein`, `fat`, `fiber`, `starch`, `sugars`, `sugarsAdded`, `alcohol`, `caffeine`, `water`, `cholesterol`, `choline`, `monounsaturatedFat`, `polyunsaturatedFat`, `saturatedFat`, `transFat`, `omega3`, `omega3ALA`, `omega3EPA`, `omega3DHA`, `omega6`, `folate`, `vitaminA`, `vitaminB1` … `vitaminB12`, `vitaminC`, `vitaminD`, `vitaminE`, `vitaminK`, `calcium`, `copper`, `iron`, `magnesium`, `manganese`, `phosphorus`, `potassium`, `selenium`, `sodium`, `zinc`, `cystine`, `histidine`, `isoleucine`, `leucine`, `lysine`, `methionine`, `phenylalanine`, `threonine`, `tryptophan`, `tyrosine`, `valine` (`QuickAddFoodIntent` in `extract.actionsdata`). Net carbs is not an input.
- **Inferred**: a clone that keys nutrients by these ids can import USDA FoodData Central data without a mapping table.

## Units

- **Found (bundle)**: kcal for energy (kJ is a display option, `EnergyUnit`; Siri accepts "log kilojoules"), mL for water, g for macros, fats, carbohydrate parts, and amino acids. The App Intents description states the rest: "Minerals, caffeine, choline, cholesterol, vitamin C, vitamin E, and B vitamins use milligrams. Folate, selenium and vitamins A, B12, D, and K use micrograms."

## Derived values

- **Found (bundle)**: net carbs is carbs minus fiber: synonyms "Non-fiber" and "Net (Non-fiber)", and "“Net carbs” are the carbohydrates left after subtracting fiber and sugar alcohols from your total carbohydrate intake."
- **Found (bundle)**: the identifier `caloriesAndMacrosMatch` exists, so the custom-food editor checks the calories against the macros.
- **Inferred**: that check uses 4 kcal/g for protein and carbs, 9 for fat, and 7 for alcohol. `901` omega-3 total is at least ALA + EPA + DHA, and may include other omega-3s a food lists.

## Goals and default targets

- **Found (bundle)**: each nutrient goal has up to three parts. "In MacroFactor, each nutrient goal can have a Target, and a range from Floor to Ceiling." The target is the RDA, the floor the lower threshold intake (LTI), the ceiling the tolerable upper intake level (UL).
- **Found (bundle)**: defaults are automatic: "the floor, target and ceiling will be set for you automatically based on your age, sex, weight, and program where applicable." Inputs come from `AgeGroup` and `SexPregnancyLactation`; the table is `constants/nutrient_ranges.dart`, computed by `services/fitness_analytics/micro_nutrients_goal_service.dart`. Macros (calories, protein, carbs, fat) come from the program instead ([`algorithms.md`](algorithms.md)).
- **Found (bundle)**: users override any part ("Custom Floor", "Custom Ceiling", "Set the floor to a custom number", "Nutrient will not display a ceiling") and choose which nutrients show ("Nutrient Explorer settings", "pinned nutrients"). Goals are dated: "All days prior to the first goal will default to Auto mode."
- **Found (web)**: same definitions; targets use RDAs and depend on age, sex, weight, and program. https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/138-what-are-the-micronutrient-floor-target-and-ceiling
- **Not found**: the numbers. `nutrient_ranges.dart` is compiled code, not a string table.
- **Inferred**: the targets are the US National Academies DRI tables (RDA, or AI where no RDA exists) by life stage, and the ceilings are their ULs. "Weight" in the copy points at the amino acids, whose requirements are per kilogram of body weight (DRI 2005 adult values in mg/kg/day: histidine 14, isoleucine 19, leucine 42, lysine 38, methionine + cystine 19, phenylalanine + tyrosine 33, threonine 20, tryptophan 5, valine 24). A clone can ship the DRI tables as its default goal source and compare against R1's screenshots.
