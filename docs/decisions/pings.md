# Pings

A ping is something the owner logs in a tap or two: an event (a glass of water, a trip to the bathroom) or a quale on a scale (mood or energy from 1 to 5). The goal is speed, so every screen shows the least that conveys the state and adds detail only when asked.

- **Two kinds.** A boolean ping records that it happened. A range ping records a step from 1 to its step count, 2 to 10 steps, each with an optional label. Steps are always discrete buttons, never a slider. `PingKind` is `.boolean` or `.range(labels)`, so a boolean ping cannot hold steps.
- **Two synced tables.** `pings` holds the definition: name, an SF Symbol as `icon`, `kind`, the step labels as a JSON array in `steps`, and `position`. `ping_logs` holds one row per log: the ping, the instant, `value`, and an optional `note`. A boolean log's value is 1, so a day's count is its rows. Deleting a ping deletes its logs on that device in the same write ([Sync](sync.md)).
- **A logged ping keeps its shape.** A log stores a step number, which means a step of the ping as it was. Once a ping has a log, its editor fixes the kind and the number of steps; the name, icon, and labels stay editable.
- **One day at a time, under the Food Log's header.** At the owner's ask (2026-11-19), the Pings tab has no title: its top is the Food Log's header row (`DayHeaderRow`, `DayStrip`, `DayPager`, shared in `DesignSystem/DayHeader.swift`), with the same Calendar and Go to Today button, day pills, native calendar sheet, and swipe between days. The list shows the chosen day's logs, and Log writes onto that day: now on today, and the clock's time of day, seconds included, on any other day (`PingLog.instant`), as the Food Log logs a food onto the day it shows. The pager reaches future days as the Food Log's does; a ping's history counts its last 30 days only up to the end of today, so a log on a later day waits for its day. Picks are not tied to a day: they stay picked while the owner changes day, so picking Energy 3 on today and then tapping yesterday logs it on yesterday. A day pill's outline fills with the share of the pings logged on that day (`PingLog.pingsLogged`), in the ping tint. The ⋯ menu holds New Ping and Edit Pings; the Food Log's items are all about food and stay there. The header stays put while the list scrolls: a day of pings is a few rows, so the slide-away the timeline needs buys nothing here. Returning to the app on a new day moves the tab to the new today when it was showing the old one.
- **Pick, then Log.** `PingLogger` lists every ping, one per row. A control picks a value and a white Log pill appears; Log writes one row per picked ping with its note. Picking the same value again takes it back. Several pings go in one Log, and a mistap costs nothing, which is why a tap does not write at once. While something is picked the Log pill takes the tab bar's place. A note typed on a range ping with no step picked is not logged: it stays in its row after Log, and the sheet stays open for it. The Pings tab and the Dashboard's "+" sheet show the same list; the sheet closes after Log.
- **A row says the minimum.** The rows sit on the page with no card and no separators, at the owner's ask (2026-11-19). A boolean ping not logged that day shows nothing beside its +. Logged once, it shows the time. From two on it shows the count ("3 times"). A range ping shows its steps beside the name as 34 pt rounded squares (8 pt corners), filled white while picked, with a ring on the step the day's latest log holds; when the steps don't fit beside the name they drop under it.
- **A tap on the row, not its controls, expands it.** A range's steps move under the name with their labels below them, and when the labels don't fit side by side each step takes its own row. A step's label is part of its button. Every expanded ping has a note field, logged with that instance; typing a note on a boolean ping picks it. On the Pings tab the expanded row also has History.
- **Order lives in Settings > Pings.** `ManagePingsView` is the one place pings are reordered (drag), edited (tap), and added. `position` orders them in the tab, the add sheet, and the Dashboard. The Pings tab's ⋯ menu pushes the same screen (Edit Pings) and opens New Ping. Its rows sit on the page like the logger's, with no card, separators, or footer.
- **Dashboard.** The Pings section shows a card per ping in that order and has no card list of its own in Customize Dashboard, only a place in Section Order. A boolean ping draws the habits' 30-day dot grid, each day shaded by its count against the busiest day, over today's count. A range ping draws seven bars of each day's average on a scale fixed to its steps, over the average of the week's logs, the same number its history shows. A card pushes the ping's history: stats, a 30-day bar chart, and the logs, where a swipe deletes one.
- **Reads stay small.** Every observer filters `ping_logs` by `logged_at` in SQL (the shown day for the list and each day pill, 30 days for cards and history) and reduces inside the fetch, so nothing decodes a year of logs on the main thread. The full history loads off the main thread when See All opens.
- **Deferred.** Editing a log's value or time, reminders, a widget, and agent API routes for pings.
