# Lifter assets

Generated by `scripts/figure-asset.ts`; don't edit by hand.

- `lifter.bin`: the lifter mesh, skin weights, and bind pose (`viewer/lifter-asset.ts` reads it).
- `muscle-map.png`: each texel's muscle plate (green) and relief (red), in the mesh's texture layout.

The mesh, its texture layout, its body-shape targets, and its skin weights come from [MakeHuman](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman) at commit `a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`, whose assets are released under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md). The muscle plates come from the app's own body map (`app-ios/Sources/CoreResources/Catalog/body.json`).
